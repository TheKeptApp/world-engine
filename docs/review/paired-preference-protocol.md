# Paired preference — proposed A3 protocol

**PROPOSED; not a new gate or a scoring result.** Supplements [GRADING.md §M](../lookloop/GRADING.md#m-approved-mocks-are-the-target-owner-7-oct-2026) and proposed §N. Whole-point scores remain authoritative for the look gate; preference can describe progress within a 2/5 band. No captures, code or judgments were performed for this document. The repetition thresholds below are authored operating rules, not measured statistical reliability.

## Freeze and blind the pair

Before viewing, register before/after image hashes, renderer/builds, approved mock, site, camera, projection, resolution, date/season, exposure, atmosphere, loaded coverage and intended changed variable. Different fixtures or incomplete coverage mean a confounded pair, not an attributed improvement. Use untouched hold-outs with the same procedure. Keep native and web separate.

A preparer makes byte-exact neutral copies, keeps the identity/randomization key separately, shuffles pair order and assigns left/right positions using constrained randomness. Display equal-sized A/B panels (A left, B right), without variant names, commit labels, expected winner or earlier judgments. Preserve source credits; do not alter pixels to hide clues. Three presentations put before on the left and three on the right. Randomize their order and interleave other pairs; never show swapped duplicates consecutively. Keep the approved reference at the same size and position throughout. Judge whole frames at the frozen display size first; any detail inspection must use equal scale/regions on both sides and be logged.

Lock conventional 1–5 grades independently before revealing pair identities or preference results. If images/builds are already recognizable from previous work, record that limitation: neutral names cannot erase recognition. A grader who prepared the identity map or knows which visual recipe is new cannot claim full identity blinding. Use a separate preparer/reviewer when available; do not invent independence from the same remembered context.

## One judgment record

For each presentation, record **A / B / tie** and one visual reason for each of **saturation, materials, ground, foliage, light**, plus a separate **overall** preference. Prefer closeness to the approved look, not simply more saturation, detail or branches. Overall is a direct judgment, not majority voting across aspects. `tie` means no defensible visible preference at the registered scale. If an aspect is not visible, record `not assessable` with its reason; do not treat it as a tie or force a vote. Sky stays in the existing 1–5 rubric, outside this five-aspect preference panel.

For **each panel separately**, answer **“roofs compete with trees: yes/no”** with a reason: do roof brightness, saturation or contrast draw attention away from the intended foliage read? This concerns visual balance, not banning a roof hue or changing the inventory. Record not assessable when either roofs or trees are absent. Do not tell the grader the desired answer. Also record any artifact/regression, familiarity with the image, and suspected identity (or unknown) **after** the visual judgment is locked.

## Repeats and bias checks

- Pre-register **six judgments per distinct pair**, over **three separate review sessions**, two presentations per session with opposite positions. Sessions should omit earlier scores/keys from the review context; recognition still gets recorded. No claim that repeated judgments by one grader are six independent people. Do not add repetitions until a preferred winner appears.
- An aspect or overall is **repeatable preference** only when the same underlying image wins at least **5/6** judgments, including at least **2/3 in each screen position**, and the checks below pass. **5/6 ties** is a repeatable tie. Other outcomes are **inconclusive**, even if one image wins a simple majority. Non-assessable repetitions leave that aspect incomplete rather than shrinking the denominator to manufacture a pass.
- Insert **two hidden identical-image A/A controls per session** (six total), with neutral labels. Each assessable preference should be a tie; roof answers should agree between identical panels. A directional vote or inconsistent roof answer flags that session's affected result as unreliable. Preserve the failure and all raw answers; correct presentation problems and, if warranted, commission a separately registered rerun of the whole affected pair, never silently discard failed controls.
- After unblinding, tabulate wins by left/right position, swapped-pair agreement, session and identity recognition. Flag positional inconsistency when the directional winner changes on swapping or fails the 2/3-in-each-position rule. Report pooled left/right counts as a diagnostic, not proof of bias or its absence. Compare suspected-identity accuracy and reasons with votes; a preference based on remembered implementation claims rather than visible evidence is invalid. Repeated pair recognition, session drift and ceiling/floor habits remain limitations even if controls pass.
- **Six is a minimum consistency screen, not a guarantee of reliability.** Do not attach binomial p-values, population confidence or “statistically significant” language to correlated self-repeats. For a consequential promotion, seek a fresh blinded reviewer using the same frozen inputs and report agreement/disagreement separately; replication across independent views/hold-outs is still required. Do not average reviewers into invented precision.

Report roof competition as yes/no counts **for before and after separately**, and publish all minority votes and confounds. “2 → 2; after preferred 5/6, controls passed” would mean repeatable partial preference, **not 3/5 or a gate pass**. Do not turn preference into a fractional grade or combine unlike views into a single hidden average. Gate requirements and the existing reject rule remain unchanged. A Sloan preference gain paired with a hold-out preference loss is additionally a proposed regression warning requiring review; it cannot cancel a whole-point hold-out loss or establish acceptance while hold-outs are missing.
