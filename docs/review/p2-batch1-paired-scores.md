## Method and limits

Three fresh review contexts, separate from P2 and from the coordinator who prepared the identity key. Each saw 14 references and locked grades for 18 neutral frames before inspecting 30 shuffled A/B panels: 12 distinct comparisons presented twice with opposite positions, plus the supplied identical-image control for each of six views. This gives six judgments per comparison, three in each position, and 18 controls. All raw reasons, timestamps, recognition notes and roof answers are retained in [review records](p2-batch1-scoring/results.json); the [separate key](p2-batch1-scoring/key.json) records each presentation. These are repeatability checks, not six independent human reviewers or statistical significance.

User-requested aspects are ground, houses, foliage, shadows and overall (houses covers architectural materials/form; shadows covers visible contact/depth/readability). [Paired protocol](paired-preference-protocol.md) supplements GRADING §§M/N. Panels show equal-sized full frames at 390 pixels per side without cropping; source copies are byte-exact. Whole-point grades were locked separately. A repeatable winner needs ≥5/6 votes and ≥2/3 in each position; ≥5/6 ties is a repeatable tie; other outcomes are inconclusive. Any failed control marks pooled results for that aspect unreliable conservatively across the batch; raw votes are never discarded.

**Exposure caveat:** native captures use pinned gain **1.0** and may look darker than the real app with automatic exposure. These scores describe these captures only. All modes share October 15 2026 20:30Z, clear/cloud0/wind0, 1005×565, heading270°, pitch45°, FOV50°. Cameras and main counts match within each view. No native/web or exposure-normalized improvement is claimed.

SHA-256 verifies all 18 delivered frames and six OFF-copy controls; see [identity audit](p2-batch1-scoring/identity-audit.json). Controls are byte copies, not independent render repeats. Against main Batch0, four OFFs are exact; Sloan150 differs in36 channel bytes by at most1/255; Sloan600 differs in316 channel bytes by at most2/255. Keep this residual noise disclosed; it is not a lossless baseline.

References: approved calibration Sloan/Lakeview plus approved house contrast/archetype boards; ground and look-fix boards provide research context only, without promoting their numeric values. Full reference paths are in the key. No approved exactly camera-matched aerial mock exists for these six poses; the target is the reference look, not literal October-to-summer pigment matching.
