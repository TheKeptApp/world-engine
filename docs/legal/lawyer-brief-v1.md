# One-page lawyer brief — A11

**9 October 2026 · Not legal advice.** Decision agenda, not approved terms. **Status: first external-user release remains RED**: ODbL offer pending; credits draft unwired. Resolve A before external access; B before UGC intake; C before sales. Disabled features may remain deferred, with their restrictions explicit.

## A — Before first external user (8)

- **A1 · ODbL offer:** Missing release route blocks distribution; counsel decides whether the versioned database/alterations offer and unrestricted parallel copy satisfy ODbL for web, exports and app stores.
- **A2 · Pilot boundary:** Invite-only access may still trigger obligations; counsel decides which external viewers, reviewers and team members count as public use and what must exist before access.
- **A3 · Source licences and credits:** An open source does not clear the combined map; counsel decides exact-release compatibility and required notices for included datasets, keeping unresolved municipal/height sources excluded.
- **A4 · Buildings and landmarks:** Commercial 3D views, downloadable meshes and public art can differ legally; counsel decides panorama, copyright and trademark limits per launch country and permitted output.
- **A5 · Live feeds, including ADS-B:** Feed grants and privacy controls may restrict relay, caching and export; counsel decides permitted commercial use, filtering, provider conditions and any separate database offer before enabling them.
- **A6 · External-user terms and age:** A pilot still needs enforceable terms and complaint handling; counsel decides liability, age/guardian rules, applicable service duties and initial notice/removal process.
- **A7 · Homes, sensitive sites and privacy:** Open geometry can expose people or restricted places; counsel decides lawful processing, detail exclusions, resident complaints, retention and location/metadata safeguards.
- **A8 · Private Builder team layer — uploaded maps/photos:** Team sharing needs upload/processing rights and confidentiality; counsel decides hosting, editing, AI/export permissions, tenant access and deletion, and whether OSM-derived edits feeding shared map data trigger ODbL share-alike, attribution and an offer without forcing release of unrelated private originals.

## B — Before UGC builds (before public submission intake)

- **B1 · Contributor grant:** Approval alone cannot authorize publication; counsel decides a versioned grant covering edits, commercial map use and downstream exports, warranties/authority, moral rights and guardian assent where allowed.
- **B2 · Third-party inputs:** Photos, plans, game assets and protected works may lack redistribution rights; counsel decides admissible evidence and exclusions, separating private upload consent from public contribution permission.
- **B3 · Promotion and withdrawal:** OSM-derived contributions and privacy deletion can conflict with distributed copies; counsel decides database scope, compatible grants/notices, public-offer contents and realistic withdrawal/rollback promises.
- **B4 · Moderation, credit and rewards:** Curated submissions need an operational rights/safety process; counsel decides notice/counter-notice/appeal duties, records and repeat abuse, optional alias credit, and payment terms before any rewards are promised.

## C — Before selling data/API

- **C1 · Saleable rights:** Permission to display is not permission to resell; counsel decides each source's raw/derived redistribution, API fan-out, sublicensing and commercial restrictions, including contributor rights.
- **C2 · ODbL commercial model:** Paid access and customer restrictions must fit open-data obligations; counsel decides what can be charged for and how share-alike, free internet offers and unrestricted parallel distribution affect exclusivity, DRM and customer terms.
- **C3 · Customer contract and privacy:** Sales add reliance and data-processing obligations; counsel decides warranties, liability, service/freshness claims, security, retention, cross-border processing and deletion duties for the actual offering.
