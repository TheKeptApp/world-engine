## Shared licence rules used in the tables

- **ODbL**: commercial use allowed; preserve notices and licence URI. Publicly used derivative databases require share-alike and a machine-readable offer of the database or qualifying alterations/method. Restrictions or technical measures require an unrestricted parallel route under §4.7. Software is not automatically relicensed. Whether a joined layer is derivative or collective is for counsel. [Legal text §§2–4](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), V.
- **BY4**: commercial copying, redistribution and adaptation permitted; retain supplied attribution/notices, link licence/source, identify changes; no share-alike, no additional restrictions on the licensed material. No universal mandatory sentence. [CC BY 4.0 §3](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en), V. **BY3** needs its own [3.0 text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode), R.
- **CC0**: no attribution or share-alike condition; other rights remain outside the waiver. [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en), V.
- **CDLA2**: no share-alike or mandatory attribution sentence; supply the agreement text with shared data, including modified data. Computational results have no agreement-imposed restrictions; do not assume every repackaged extract is a “Result”. [Agreement §§1–3](https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/), V.
- **PD**: no copyright share-alike or copyright redistribution restriction for verified public-domain data; exclude third-party material and respect privacy, access conditions and non-endorsement. Provider citation requests remain recorded separately.
- **OGL**: generally commercial reuse/adaptation/redistribution with source attribution; no share-alike; exclusions include third-party rights and official marks. UK/Canada full texts were blocked here, so associated legal conclusions remain R/U pending text review. [UK v3](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), [Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada). [Toronto text](https://open.toronto.ca/open-data-licence/) was read, V.
