# Visual review — illustrative sheets

All 27 final sheets were opened and visually inspected. No readable business names, logos or text were observed. Every sheet has a street-facing and aerial panel on neutral ground, with the named mass/material/entry concept recognizable. This is a qualitative design review, not a look-loop score or an engine acceptance result. Paired-camera distances, exact sun bearings, window ratios, albedo and dimensions remain **requested, unmeasured**. The aerial views frequently enlarge the building to show detail; do not infer literal 150 m apparent size.

The generated style is muted and architectural but more textured, furnished and photo-like than the approved calibration frames. Build simplified matte forms from the specification; do not port the raster's full masonry grids, grain, roof seams, interiors, HVAC, decorative lighting or incidental planters. Clear-direction afternoon shadows are illustrative; they do not validate real-sun studies. Cross-panel geometry/window drift remains possible. This review accepts the sheets as variation concepts, not exact implementation targets.
