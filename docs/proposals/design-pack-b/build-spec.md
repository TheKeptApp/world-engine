# Build contract — storefront prototypes

All geometry values below are authored design defaults, not surveys, building code or verified dimensions. Fit actual map geometry first; do not stretch a real roof or add a storey to match a variant.

## Coordinates, silhouette and component assembly

Metres, +Y up. Prototype frontage runs along X, front outward normal −Z; front edge Z=0, rear Z=depth. Origin at frontage centre/ground base; corners X=±width/2. Ground must be sampled in a reconciled vertical datum; floors stay level. Width is occupied frontage length, not necessarily the host building's width. A storefront inside a larger building is a facade attachment; retain its host's entire roof/mass, party walls and other occupancies.

Use `total_height_m` as top roof/parapet elevation above local base. Flat roof: slab top H−0.45 m, parapet top H, parapet thickness 0.20 m; stepped parapet centre segment H, adjacent segments H−0.30 m. Clip a corner only if actual footprint has it; illustrative chamfer defaults 1.2 m each leg. Gable/hip: ridge H, eave H−1.2 m (small kiosk roof H−0.5 m); preserve measured slope/ridge instead when known. Shed: high edge H, low edge H−0.6 m. Barrel: spring H−0.6 m, half-ellipse crown rise 0.6 m. Rounded parapet means rounded coping/front parapet corners, not a new curved footprint. Sawtooth C06 uses two equal roof bays, each low H−0.8 m/high H, without sawtooth detail when the roof is unsupported.

R08 deck: 10×8 m illustrative clear deck, 2 m frontage setback, guard height 1.1 m; H includes guard top, slab H−1.1 m. No inferred roof stairhouse. T04 upper-floor setback defaults 1 m front/rear only where evidenced; preserve map footprint/parts. T02 exactly four bays here, within the requested 3–5-unit family; never divide occupants from frontage width alone. T03 L-plan 42×30 m bounding box minus southwest 24×18 m forecourt; two perpendicular wings. Its 36×24 m example lot is separate illustrative geometry, never inferred parking. Unknown bay boundaries yield a neutral continuous frontage rather than fictitious shops.

R06/R07 optional 6×4 m patio: separate project component on the documented side, not part of footprint. R07 string lights: two 3 m posts, eight small bulbs, one span; off in afternoon, no emissive light cast. Roofed awnings and patios do not imply permits. R08 roof deck and T03 lot are optional only with requisite source geometry.

G04 store 12×10 m; separate canopy 22×10 m, slab thickness 0.35 m, soffit 4.8 m, four 0.25×0.25 m columns inset 1 m from corners, 5 m example store gap. G05 shop 9×14 m; separate 10×6 m gable canopy, soffit 4.5 m/ridge 5.5 m, four 0.25 m columns, 4 m gap. Canopies are distinct roof features attached to the same verified fuel site. In real data use source roof/column constraints; omit pumps and price pylons without mapped facts. These measurements do not prove vehicle clearance or structure safety.

## Frontage and windows

Ground commercial storey H_g=min(3.8, H−roof allowance); one-storey wall uses actual eave/slab height. Multi-storey upper floors divide the remaining wall height uniformly unless mapped facts provide storey heights. Ground sill 0.65 m; head min(H_g−0.35,3.1 m), door clear opening 1.0×2.2 m single or 1.8×2.2 m double, recess 0.25 m where specified. These are modelling dimensions, not access compliance. Porch geometry only when mapped/confirmed; never automatic steps across a public path.

Window ratio in specs is window aperture area divided by ground-floor occupied street wall area (width × wall height), doors excluded. Build 2–4 repeated bays for small frontages, 4–8 for wide detached fronts; count only confirmed units for strips. Compute total aperture area from target ratio, distribute across bays with 0.35 m minimum pier; clamp to available opening area and report achieved ratio if constraints bind. R02 large windows are lower-floor glazing; upper floors use 0.24 wall ratio. Multi-storey variants default upper ratio 0.24; house conversion 0.20. C04 has one arched bakery opening, spring 1.8 m and crown 2.8 m above floor, plus narrower rectangular windows only as needed. C05 service hatch 1.6×1.0 m, sill 1.0 m; target ratio is secondary to specified hatch dimensions. No interior merchandise, letter-like patterns or recognizable products.

Fabric awnings: 1.0 m projection, lower edge ≥2.4 m, 12° pitch, 0.12 m straight valance. Wrapped awnings stop at known occupied edges. Blade canopies: 1.2 m projection, 0.15 m thickness, level underside ≥2.6 m. R07 pergola 6×3 m with four simple posts and five top beams. Doors have plain dark frames, no symbols. Accent fascia is a solid unlettered rectangle, no pylon.

## Material and detail tiers

Hex values are sRGB authored albedo, identical in street/aerial/LOD versions. Read the calibration `sharedLook.materials`: masonry/plaster/wood/concrete roughness 0.82, fabric 0.90, painted metal 0.68, glass 0.28; simplified albedo fields, no brick/wood photo textures or procedural dirt. Default glazing #435A66, frame #4F504B, coping #C8C1B0, roof #8B877D, ground #C5C0B3. These supplemental colours are authored pack values, not measured reference pixels. Keep distinct wall/accent families restrained.

At 40 m retain awning outline, entry recess, structural piers, window groups, coping and meaningful roof shape; selective brick courses at structural corners only, no full-face micro-mortar grid. At 150 m retain identical mass, parapet, canopy/roof/patio extent, occupancy frontage rhythm and large colour blocks; omit tiny fixtures, individual string bulbs and small mullions. Never brighten albedo or increase saturation at distance. Architectural shadows derive from real mass/roof/awning geometry; no painted shadow texture or camera-facing shadow proxy. Detail allocation is projected-size driven, not a place-specific distance switch.

Prototype component classes are mass, roof/parapet, frontage bays, entrance, awning, optional patio/deck/canopy/site pad. Keep IDs/materials stable across variants and use seeded selection only among evidence-compatible candidates. No new numerical triangle/frame/memory budget is claimed; consumer measures composite budgets under current DECISIONS and archetypes gates. Far simplification must preserve shadow reach and real silhouette.

## Resolver boundaries

Use tag → unambiguous host/occupied edge → observed dimensions/form → region-compatible candidate. Restaurant/cafe/bar use is direct-tag evidence, not inferred from windows; cuisine must never select stucco, an arch or any ethnic style. Historical decade labels are aesthetic descriptions, not automatic age classifiers. Wood, arched windows, rounded roofs, deck/patio and converted-form variants require matching mapped facts or explicitly labelled private choices. If no candidate is compatible, use registry fallback B on known footprint or P for unresolved point. Grocery/convenience and small-service extensions remain proposals; names/brands do not break ties. Stable source-derived choice may vary colour only within an approved regional palette and always yields to explicit `building:colour`/`building:material` evidence; missing colour is inferred and labelled.
