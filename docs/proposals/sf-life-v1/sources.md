# Primary evidence and limits

Checked 2026-10-07. Source facts below support mechanisms/placement, not invented numeric density or fleet statistics.

- [Cable Car Museum — brakes](https://www.cablecarmuseum.org/the-brakes.html): describes constant9.5mph when held to cable; supports approximate15.3km/h cruise, not average trip speed. [Museum mechanism account](https://www.cablecarmuseum.org/road-track.html): underground cable/grip through track slot. Exact generic vehicle dimensions, rider counts and animation are authored.
- [SFMTA electric trolleybuses](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/munis-electric-trolley-buses): roof trolley poles transmit electricity from overhead wires, and modern vehicles can travel off-wire on battery. The kit's5.5m contact height/30m pole spacing are UNVERIFIED authoring targets; no exact SF OCS survey or engineering design was performed.
- [SFMTA parking guidance](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/drive-park/how-avoid-parking-tickets): hill wheel-curbing and different uphill/downhill orientation; angled parking depends on designated signs/markings. [SFMTA back-in angled parking](https://www.sfmta.com/blog/back-angled-parking-meets-bike-lane): documents steep-street back-in parking and explains curbing behavior. These sources do not locate the unnamed illustrated15% block.
- [SF Port getting around](https://www.sfport.com/visit/getting-around): waterfront historic streetcars, ferry/water access and places. [Port waterfront plan](https://sfport.com/files/2024-01/waterfront_plan_jan2024_final_web_version.pdf): maritime/passenger/cargo/recreational uses; not live positions or ship dimensions.
- [Foodwise Ferry Plaza market visitor information](https://foodwise.org/markets/ferry-plaza-farmers-market/visitor-info): market outside the Ferry Building on the Embarcadero. No operating-day schedule is hardcoded here; obtain active event hours separately.
- [City of SF Shared Spaces manual](https://www.sf.gov/file/shared-spaces-manual) and [SF parklet-permit dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/parklet-permits): curb/parking-lane placement and permitting context. This pack's12×2.2m deck/clear-path proposals are not a compliance determination.
- [Pier39 sea lions](https://www.pier39.com/sealions/): supports K-Dock haul-out and variable abundance with season/food/migration. No numeric current census, typical count or fixed summer rule is claimed by the kit.

Local approved inputs: `../us-metros-wave2-v1/sf-r2/sf-values.json`, `sf-cable-car.png`, `sf-block.png`, `sf-district-04.png`, `sf-fog-advection.png`; Japan regional vehicle sheet inspected as a real-proportion comparator. Source images supplied to generation are exclusively SF-r2. No Chicago calibration image was used.

UNVERIFIED: all per-block/hour/season density numbers, private shuttle/robotaxi prevalence, specific shop/stall/dock positions, overhead clearance/support spacing, generic dimensions except the cited cable cruise conversion, regulatory parking/parklet fit of any pictured location, maritime horn schedule, actor/vehicle animation performance and pixel-level renderer calibration. All are explicit proposals or mapped/live-data inputs; no blocked sites were bypassed.
