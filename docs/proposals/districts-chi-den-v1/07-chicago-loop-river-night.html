The same river/bridge/tower district in full night, with readable cool architecture, sparse grouped warm windows and restrained fragmented river highlights.

Signature elements

  • Readable bridge silhouette and center leaf joint
  • Dark river walls with isolated warm lamp pools
  • Grouped warm/cool window apertures within dark facades
  • Inland elevated L silhouette
  • Depth-correct night haze and fragmented water highlights

Must be exact before building

  • River polygon, banks, wall elevations and riverwalk access from map/survey
  • Bridge deck endpoints, span type, trunnion/leaf axes, clearances and opening state for each actual bridge
  • Elevated L alignment/supports and road clearances; do not infer tracks from a scenic river composition
  • Every building footprint and height; real camera heading and occlusion
  • Night lighting replaces day state; exposure applied once
  • Window selection deterministic by existing aperture IDs; mapped fixture inventory only

Can simplify

  • Rivets and fine rail lattice collapse into a few broad beam/brace solids
  • Opaque glazing and repeated opening bands; no reflective facade
  • Bridgehouses as simple volumes; no logos or carved sculpture
  • Plain flat river with low-frequency highlights; no water simulation
  • No per-window local lights or room detail
  • Water highlights as broad broken bands, no reflective glass or mirror river
