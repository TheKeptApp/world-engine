Dense mixed-height river canyon, Chicago-type bascule bridges, stone river walls and the inland elevated L.

Signature elements

  • Double-leaf bascule bridge profile and center joint
  • River walls and lower riverwalk ledges
  • Steel elevated L structure on mapped streets
  • Mixed stepped tower massing
  • Brick and limestone street podiums

Must be exact before building

  • River polygon, banks, wall elevations and riverwalk access from map/survey
  • Bridge deck endpoints, span type, trunnion/leaf axes, clearances and opening state for each actual bridge
  • Elevated L alignment/supports and road clearances; do not infer tracks from a scenic river composition
  • Every building footprint and height; real camera heading and occlusion

Can simplify

  • Rivets and fine rail lattice collapse into a few broad beam/brace solids
  • Opaque glazing and repeated opening bands; no reflective facade
  • Bridgehouses as simple volumes; no logos or carved sculpture
  • Plain flat river with low-frequency highlights; no water simulation
