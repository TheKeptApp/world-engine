Street and map rules

Stepped lot foundations and entries on hills; narrow attached frontages and bay-window rhythm. Concrete sidewalks, small tree pits, selective planted front gardens. Oakland Craftsman has distinct detached yard/porch grain. Coastal exposure and mapped species govern trees; no palm default. Utilities and rail/cable routes from mapped geometry only.

Positions and sightlines come from the map. Bridges are separate far-view witnesses; never place both into a generic street panorama. Cable cars use underground cable slots; electric streetcars use overhead power.
