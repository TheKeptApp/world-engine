# San Francisco hero-market revision r2

Open [index.html](index.html). 18 sheets: six archetypes, four districts, one steep block paint-over, a30% profile/street/aerial witness, cable car, waterfront historic streetcar, three regional fog states and bridge silhouettes. Editable HTML and artwork PNG; full-sheet PNGs include metres, hexes and projected-size detail tiers.

The earlier [SF pack](../sf/index.html) is **superseded** and retained without deletion. Oakland Craftsman and bridge artwork are kept after audit. Modern six-storey massing, Marina three-level massing, Edwardian three-floor massing and Embarcadero geometry remain; their required palette/tree/background corrections are documented in prompts. The old Chicago calibration image is excluded from generation: only its shared numerical lighting object is retained. This pack uses the approved SF waterfront as its appearance reference.

The attached rows have zero open side gaps and7.6m review frontage (25ft exact=7.62m); real parcels override. Painted Ladies use four colours per facade and different colours on neighboring houses. Edwardian/Marina/Sunset rows have varied whites and light pastels. Small narrow street trees are intermittent, with long treeless frontages. Oakland remains detached.

The hero block drops25%, the cable car21%, and the geometry witness30%=16.699244° (30m rise per100m horizontal run). A side profile makes the witness undeniable. Floors remain level, walls vertical, foundations step, garages meet the sloping street and stair runs resolve entry heights. Parked generic cars follow road pitch, park parallel to curb and turn wheels toward it. Camera positions and streets must follow the map; no uniform steep grade is applied to Marina, Mission or Embarcadero.

Fog states are geographically different: incoming western advection bank with sunny downtown/east side; offshore Pacific fog at sunset; full-overcast June-gloom ceiling cutting off hills. Bands use MSL metres, not height above each camera. The density field is terrain/coast aligned; incoming fog is not a uniform citywide overlay. Values are authored rendering targets, **not live observations**; real weather/cloudbase and DEM geometry override. No fabricated common panorama combines Twin Peaks, Golden Gate and downtown.

`sf-values.json` contains a dedicated `sfRevisionValues` section: slope ranges, lot widths, per-family facade palettes (hex/linear RGB/roughness), fog base/top, spatial/extinction rules, tree spacing, transit and parked-car geometry. Existing matte base materials and approved clear-day lighting are preserved. Weather-state changes replace clear-day values and apply extinction only once. Raster panels are illustrative; their pixels are not calibrated measurements of the numerical proposals.

Primary evidence: [DOT/Volpe SF street-grade study](https://ntlrepository.blob.core.windows.net/lib/59000/59900/59997/DOT-VNTSC-SFMTA-16-01.pdf) reports Filbert31.5%, supporting the physical plausibility of an illustrative30% witness. [Archived SFMTA cable-car route specification](https://archives.sfmta.com/cms/rhomemu/genmuinfo.htm) supports the21% Hyde witness. Neither establishes this unnamed25% block as a surveyed location. [NWS San Francisco fog explanation](https://www.weather.gov/mtr/ask_expert) describes coastal advection and the Golden Gate channel; [NOAA marine-layer explanation](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/ocean/marine-layer) supports shallow coastal cloud with sunny nearby inland areas. Numeric review bands and palette/tree-spacing choices are not taken from those sources.

`prompts.json` records the built-in imagegen prompts, edit targets, input hashes and selected outputs. `verification.json` reports offline image/link checks,390px layout checks and explicit visual audit. It is not a phone GPU benchmark, geospatial survey, or proof that every raster slope equals the specified number. No git, logos, signage, dogs, app UI, real murals or public art.
