# Sources and status

Checked 7 October 2026. Source existence/product scope is distinct from verified exact regional shares.

## US-AGE

**Verified national mean.** US light vehicles average 12.8years in 2025; not a local age distribution. [Primary source](https://press.spglobal.com/2025-05-21-U-S-Vehicle-Age-Rises-Again-to-12-8-Years-in-2025%2C-According-to-S-P-Global-Mobility).

## UK-COLOUR

**Verified new-car ranking.** Grey most popular UK new-car colour in 2025 for eighth year; not existing-fleet local colour shares. [Primary source](https://www.smmt.co.uk/grey-tops-uk-car-colour-chart-for-eighth-year/).

## UK-DATA

**Verified inventory; no local extraction.** Aggregate licensing data files can support geography/fuel/age studies; no fine-body extraction completed. [Primary source](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/vehicle-licensing-statistics-data-files).

## US-FHWA

**Verified.** 2024 state registration counts; automobiles/trucks are administrative categories, not sedan/SUV/pickup street shares. Covers IL,CO,SC,NY,CA,FL contexts. [Primary source](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/policyinformation/statistics/2024/mv1.cfm).

## US-EPA

**Verified.** New model-year production distinguishes sedan/wagon, car SUV, truck SUV, pickup and minivan/van; not in-use stock or local visible traffic. [Primary source](https://www.epa.gov/automotive-trends/highlights-automotive-trends-report).

## CA-STOCK

**Verified.** Canada 2024 LDV stock: SUV/crossover 41.9%, passenger 35.0%; EV 5.2% includes HEV,BEV,PHEV. BEV 487618 of about24.6million ~=1.98% derived. [Primary source](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/251017/dq251017c-eng.htm).

## UK-STOCK

**Verified.** Year-end 2025 licensed stock and age; cars average 10years. Broad body types do not identify hatchbacks vs SUVs. [Primary source](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/vehicle-licensing-statistics-2025/vehicle-licensing-statistics-united-kingdom-2025).

## UK-TABLES

**Verified inventory; detailed extraction not done.** VEH0105 local authority broad body/fuel; VEH1107 age; fine-body crosswalk remains unverified. [Primary source](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/vehicle-licensing-statistics-data-tables).

## NL-CBS

**Verified inventory.** Active passenger-car stock by vehicle attributes and region; not a direct seven-body street mix. [Primary source](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/cijfers/detail/85237NED).

## MX-INEGI
