# WorldEngine mascots — travel, globe and map direction
7 October 2026 · Style B: rich stylized, warm and vibrant · Revised to emphasize travel and exploring places

This pass responds to “more around travel, globe, map.” The identity now starts with a world, map, compass or route silhouette. Weather, sky and seasons remain supporting reactions, rather than the reason the character exists. A short neighbourhood walk should feel like a journey; the mascot should also work as WorldEngine's broader exploration identity.

The first set is preserved: [earlier guide](README-first-pass.md), [earlier gallery](index-first-pass.html). All new filenames begin `travel-`; nothing in the previous image set was overwritten. All deliverables remain in this same folder.

## Top three
**1. Globindle — best overall WorldEngine identity.** A little living globe communicates the world/exploration idea immediately. Its turquoise sphere, coral land shapes, cream meridian curves and incomplete gold arch give it a warm, recognizable silhouette. It adapts across apps without becoming a dog, a weather symbol or a jobs supervisor. The main risk is a conventional globe-face category: the open arch, pear-like body and consistent broad land patches must carry the distinction.

**2. Waynookle — clearest map and route character.** The three-panel fold and broad route with two endpoints make travel/wayfinding explicit at small size. It is a natural guide for neighbourhood discovery, walk planning and jobs. The main risk is becoming a generic talking map: retain the tall middle panel, planar fold profile and endpoint band; avoid scrolls, explorer clothing and a familiar cartoon face.

**3. Wayarclet — best compact compass traveller.** The low coral disc and single turquoise needle create a concise travel/orientation cue. It is simpler to rig and less scenery-dependent than a bird with a route tail. It suits exploration UI, games and an app icon. The main risk is that the needle may read as a fin or arrow alone; retain the low instrument-like body and cream edge band, and test the silhouette with the other two.

**Specialists:** Terrapodlet is the strongest DogWell companion in this pass; Atlasnookle suits saved places/planning and jobs; Meridindle strongly connects globe scale to local places; Roamplet is the most abstract route mark; Routuft is the most lively travelling local.

## Deliverables and authority
Eight new `travel-*-sheet.png` files each contain 14 studies: front, side, back; joy, curious, concerned, determined, sleepy; rain, snow, sun, night; a small neighbourhood placement; and a flat icon treatment. Eight separate `travel-*-icon.svg` files are newly authored vector simplifications. [index.html](index.html) compares icons at 32/64/128 layout pixels, crops each street vignette to phone width, and opens full sheets.

[image-prompts.json](image-prompts.json) preserves the previous prompt record alongside every new prompt, source path and selected-file hash. New PNGs used the built-in image generator; SVGs were authored directly, not extracted/traced from bitmap pixels. The vector sketches deliberately simplify geometry, feet and facial detail; reconcile them with the chosen 3D design before production.

User's Style B definition governs: warm saturated colour, broad simplified forms, softly bevelled edges, strong warm light, bright highlights, deep coloured shade and atmospheric depth. The style-only image input was `Desktop/world-engine/docs/proposals/look-fix-v1/images/lighting-03-ordinary-1530.png`. No existing character imagery was used as a character-design reference.

Eight new authored directions, with no intentional copying of known mascots, brand faces, costumes or signature props. Originality has not been exhaustively cleared against all existing IP. Every proposed name and final visual mark **needs a legal check**.

## Construction and small-size rules
All dimensions, colour choices, pixel thresholds and mesh suggestions are authored proposals, not measured phone tests. Keep true physical scale. A 390 layout px-wide camera at 60° horizontal FOV has focal length 337.75 layout px; projected width = **337.75 × width_m / depth_m**. At 8 m the new characters' 0.30–0.42 m longest dimensions produce roughly 12.7–17.7 px. At 20 m they produce 5.1–7.1 px; expressions will not resolve.

- Over 32 px: retain broad face and a large gesture.
- 16–32 px: primary globe/fold/needle/loop, two large body colours and eyes only if resolved.
- 8–16 px: coarse silhouette, primary colour contrast; no tiny facial/land/grid marks.
- Under 8 px: simple optional silhouette or hide the decorative companion; a separate selected portrait may be larger in the UI.
- Preserve gaps in Globindle's arch, Roamplet's open route and Meridindle's frame. If an open gap becomes subpixel, merge small features rather than thicken the character or enlarge its physical size.
- Icon sketches at 32/64/128 px are display comparisons, not final platform exports. Use an unmasked full canvas for platform export. Never add text, small scenery or delicate map lines.
- Initial near mesh suggestion 600–1,200 triangles; medium 150–350; far 40–100, inside existing scene budgets. Shared opaque materials, 2–3 major body parts and broad palette fields. No transparency-heavy globe interiors, fur or feather shaders.
- Broad fillets around 1–3% of member width; large planar faces stay flat on map/atlas characters. Do not subdivide everything into soft toys. No individual paper fibres, shell scutes, feather marks, bricks or grass blades.
- Slow sparse idles, one greeting, one direction/exploration gesture, and four weather poses. Reduced motion uses static poses.

Map/globe features are symbolic character graphics, not a political map, scientific globe or real route. In a production mascot, choose one fixed version of its broad land patches and meridian lines; do not let them change shape between expressions. In WorldEngine's actual map, geographic and live-data correctness stay with the mapped world. The mascot never moves roads, changes real sun/moon position, invents transit, or claims verified route access.

Compass needle and route endpoints are character design cues. Unless explicitly integrated with a navigation function, they do not claim geographic north, a route destination or a live location. Weather reactions reflect app environment state; data freshness, uncertainty and warnings belong in explicit app information.

For DogWell, slow exploration is a positive identity: no guilt for short/missed walks, chase cues or unsafe-weather encouragement. Terrapodlet's position must remain clear of the actual dog's route. Other grounded mascots stay off doors, ramps and crossings.

## Review and remaining work
All eight new raster sheets were visually inspected for their 14 requested cells, readable broad identities, face-free backs, weather reactions, street placement and flat icon version. They remain exploratory: turnaround views are not guaranteed orthographic measurements or rigging references. Land patches and line placements vary slightly in generated expressions, especially Terrapodlet; fix a single canonical pattern when modelling. Some side views prioritize a readable profile over strict orthography.

The written construction and vector sketches govern broad shapes.
