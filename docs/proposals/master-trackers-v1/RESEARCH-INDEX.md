# WorldEngine research and design index

Snapshot: 7 October 2026. Status synthesis only; no new approvals. Existing sources read only.

Approved-binding means explicit controlling approval, scoped in the entry. Approved-reference means named governing reference or filed adoption/in-use evidence. Concept means proposal without matching approval. Research-unverified means unresolved claims/rights/coverage, even when individual facts are verified. Superseded means historical. Pending means announced but not present. Approved input does not approve output; approval never establishes an engine build.

Dates are pack dates, not survey dates. All 46 drop packs at the read cutoff are represented. Filed copies are linked, not double-counted. Filed-only packs and alternate Style C concepts have separate entries. Binary art/ZIPs were inventoried through README/values/manifests; no fresh visual acceptance audit. Superseded folders are history only.
