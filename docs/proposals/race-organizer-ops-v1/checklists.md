# Checklists by race type
Checked 2026-10-07. All checklists are **A: proposed review workflow**, informed by source IDs in sources.json; they do not claim universal legal requirements.

## Every type
- Confirm landowner/manager, usable survey, permitted polygon, public access, date and current closures.
- Resolve permits, insurance, road/trail traffic control, neighbor notices, structures, vendors and sound.
- Record participants plus staff/spectators; model arrivals, toilet/gear queues and finishes.
- Approve medical/AED/EMS plan, accessible arrival-to-services routes, communications and incident command.
- Confirm shelter capacity, WBGT policy, lightning evacuation, cancellation communications and reopening authority.
- Inspect surface, ballast/cables/barriers; brief marshals and volunteer deputies.
- Verify timing and backup, exact signs/turnarounds, aid/waste, sweep and participant reconciliation.
- Reserve cleanup and teardown crews; do not release services until incident command confirms clear.

## 5K
- Prefer simple loop/out-and-back; measure advertised5,000m independently.
- Prove waves clear the first pinch; separate start and finish where overlaps occur.
- Confirm start/finish water and an initial midpoint opportunity, heat-specific revision.
- Compact medical/toilets/washing; no distance exemption from medical planning.
- Sources: rrca, usatf, psai, ada, nws (rrca is the source ID).

## 10K
- Repeat common/5K checks; verify5,000m turn if out-and-back.
- Initial water opportunities2.5/5/7.5km; place outside racing envelope.
- Check fast/slow runner opposing flow and crossovers; intermediate timing if required.
- Longer operating window, course toilets and sweep ownership.
- Sources: rrca, waterrule, usatf.

## Half marathon
- Document21,097.5m target, certified restrictions and cutoffs.
- Initial aid gaps2.5km plus fueling/toilets; confirm supplies and medical escalation.
- If point-to-point, transport bags and provide return/accessible transport.
- Check late participants remain supported even after elite finish.
- Sources: usatf, rrtc, rrca.

## Marathon
- Document42,195m, certification and road reopening by sector.
- Approve prolonged heat/cold exposure, medical escalation/cooling and accessible support.
- Replenishment/communications redundancy; toilets/fueling and late sweep at every sector.
- Plan major transport, bag recovery, finish dispersal and hospital coordination.
- Sources: usatf, rrtc, rrca, wahealth.

## Trail
- Survey singletrack width, blind bends, slopes, stream crossings and wildlife/habitat limits.
- Stage small waves; avoid pass/counterflow assumptions on singletrack.
- Set aid by slowest time-on-feet, mandatory carried supplies and extraction access.
- Reconcile checkpoint counts and withdrawals; sweep all branches; radios tested in valleys.
- Mark emergency references and evacuation points without implying a vehicle can use the trail.
- Sources: rrca, nws; trail-specific numeric capacities are unverified.

## Triathlon
- Obtain water access/quality approval and qualified swim-rescue plan; do not repurpose5K rules.
- Approve swim waves/buoys/cutoffs and water-weather policies with applicable federation.
- Secure racks; equalize routes, separate swim-in/bike-out/bike-in/run-out.
- Place mount/dismount lines and timing boundaries with officials; keep spectators out.
- Traffic plan for bike speeds, corners and aid; no generic running-flow coefficient.
- Sources: tri, tri-flow; current sport rule details require event officials.

## Obstacle
- Require engineered obstacle inspection, fall/runoff envelopes, queue capacity and escape/bypass.
- Never improvise obstacles from decorative map assets; no obstacle over emergency egress.
- Assess water/electrical/fall hazards individually with qualified safety/medical lead.
- Provide extraction and incident reporting; sanction-specific medical requirements override size defaults.
- Sources: obstacle, obstacle-site; obstacle dimensions/throughput remain supplier-specific.
