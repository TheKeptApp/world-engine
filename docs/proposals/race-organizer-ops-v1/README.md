# Race organizer operations v1
Research and Builder AI proposal rules · checked 2026-10-07.

**Place safety and permission before appearance.** Builder AI may propose a village and course; it cannot authorize them. Unknown usable widths, jurisdiction, emergency access, shelter capacity or permits keep the plan pending.

V = verified published source; A = authored planning assumption; U = unverified/conflicting. Rules retain scope and evidence. Current local permits and qualified plans override defaults.

## Five recommendations [A]
1. Require a measured usable-width and hazard layer; OSM paths alone cannot establish crowd capacity.
2. Prefer wave starts and keep public/cycle traffic separate under an approved access plan.
3. Budget toilets around the pre-start surge, including accessible units in each cluster and spectators/staff.
4. Protect finish runoff and medical/EMS access before adding expo, gear check or stage.
5. Require medical-director heat policy and a shelter-capacity/evacuation plan; do not adopt a conflicting generic WBGT table.
