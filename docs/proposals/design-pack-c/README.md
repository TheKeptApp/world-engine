# Design pack C — apartments, offices, hotels

16 fictional variants: eight apartments, five offices and three hotels. **Concept proposal, not approval, implementation, a building survey or a visual-score pass.** Generated paired views are present locally under `images/`; images follow the repository's existing ignored-image policy. The merged deliverables are specifications, prompts, index and hashed image manifest. Open [the variation sheets](index.html); [specifications](specs.md), [all 120 pair checks](sameness.md), [16 exact generation prompts](prompts.md) and [machine-readable variant records](variants.json) are the build handoff.

## Look and camera contract

Style: stylized-realistic, warm muted materials, clean simple masses, restrained dark glass with broad reflection, selective physical reveals/cornices, matte fields without photographic grain or dirt. Use the right/middle simplified material treatment in facade-detail-v2; do not copy brick microtexture from the detailed comparison panels. Neutral warm-grey ground isolates each form. No readable text, signs, brands, logos, real buildings, people, animals or incidental city scenery. Sheet IDs and region labels live outside the image in HTML.

Each PNG is one two-panel sheet, left **40 m street view** (horizontal centre distance, eye height 1.6 m, southwest bearing, 45° in plan to the front); right **150 m aerial view** (horizontal centre distance and 150 m elevation, 45° downward). Front is south, east right in plan. Perspective framing may zoom to fit; lens and actual generated camera matrices are not calibrated or recovered. These are authoring instructions, not proof an image generator physically obeyed every dimension. Dimensions in specs.md govern construction. For large bars/clusters, frame both panels to include the entire object; do not crop to hide a failure.

Lighting inherits style-b-calibration-v2 `sharedLook` rather than adding a city grade. **This requested fixture explicitly replaces sun azimuth 225° with west 270°**, retaining the reference's 40° elevation; shadows extend east. Reference brightness/material targets stay inherited, not resampled from PNGs or applied twice. Hex colours are authored sRGB appearance swatches, not measured reflectance; brick/stucco/wood hue families are content differences, not regional exposure or saturation changes. No engine/mock-values override is filed by this pack.

## Build contract

All dimensions are metres. Each exemplar uses south-facing frontage; the engine must rotate it to the mapped frontage, not compass-lock real features. For the listed U/C/L/H/T footprints, use orthogonal polygon unions/subtractions as specified; A08 consists of three independent building footprints within an explicit site, never a new merged building footprint. Exclude courtyards and gaps when calculating A. Split townhome modules along explicit attached-part geometry; do not add six houses to one mapped detached footprint.

Flat top = plinth + occupied levels × floor pitch + parapet/roof allowance. Pitched roof rise derives from half the stated short span × tan(pitch), except mono-pitch/shed roof span × tan(pitch). A05 gables span each 6 m module; A08 hips span each 12 m member; O03 sheds use a 10 m wing span. Roof allowances are cap/thickness, not occupied levels. H03 is four occupied levels: three below 9.9 m cornice and one mansard storey, top 13.8 m; its 60° lower slope is 1.85 m run over 3.2 m rise, capped above. Sidewall/main spans and roof-part boundaries must remain within the original polygon. Windows repeat by physical bay pitch, shorten at corners, keep ≥0.6 m pier clearance and do not cross a shared party wall or courtyard void. Clamp bay count to floor(face length / pitch); minimum one opening only when clearance allows.

Visual trim defaults: 0.12 m window recess, 0.10 m sill projection, 0.20 m lintel band and 0.35 m cornice projection when the variant calls for one. Use simple boxes/planes; rails are 1.05 m high, slab thickness 0.18 m. Balcony depths and stair widths are in each row. Entry doors, glazing and trim must not overlap piers. These are **new authored proposal values keyed `design-pack-c/build.*`**, not measured dimensions or building-code guidance. Stair runs are authored geometric connections to a landing (0.15 m rise, 0.28 m tread), not accessibility/egress certification.

LOD uses projected screen size, not per-place exceptions: retain footprint/height and distinctive roof profile at far range; collapse pane grids, rail bars and stair treads to continuous strips before losing primary silhouette. Middle range retains window rhythm, balcony/gallery projections and courtyard voids. Near range may add sills, door recesses and simple stair treads. Exact distance thresholds and triangle/draw budgets remain with the existing renderer/device-tier specs; this pack invents no override or performance claim. Use shared batches and deterministic OSMRef/StableRandom selection, never unseeded randomness.

## Selection and existing archetype mapping

First follow [archetypes.md §§1–2](../../execution/archetypes.md): form, use and site are separate. Apartments require apartment/residential-form evidence; office use alone must not invent office physical form; motel/hotel requires explicit compatible use/form association. A point inside a mixed-use building does not own the whole facade. Unknown/contradictory form remains the registry's neutral B/P fallback. No name, address, area ID, postcode, coordinates, camera or building-specific condition occurs in these selectors.

The row's A (filled mapped footprint area), L (mapped valid levels) and broad regional palette provide a **candidate**, not a measured architectural classification. Shape/use/roof/part evidence gates additional detail. Region selects palette, not truth about age or function; all three region palettes can apply to compatible forms, with explicit material tags overriding them. Missing L uses a separately labelled existing height/level fallback; missing use cannot be supplied by size. Unknown roof cannot select the H03 mansard or special pitched form as an observed fact. Balconies, exterior stairs, gallery access and period labels in mock exemplars remain **inferred design detail**, disabled in factual/default generation absent mapped geometry or an approved inferred-detail policy.

Overlapping candidates (e.g. A01/A05) resolve by explicit form/parts first. If several equally compatible approved inferred variants remain, use stable-ID seeded choice, with the `commonness` number excluded from weights until independently approved/censused. Prefer exact geometry compatibility; never reshape a mapped rectangle into a courtyard or add a wing because the sheet looks attractive. Height, levels, roof tags, footprint and material evidence override exemplar dimensions/palette. Geometry too small for details omits them and records the reason.

Registry rows 04 `apartments` → proposed `ResidentialBlock`, 11 `office` → proposed `OfficeMass`, 15 `hotel` and 16 `motel` → proposed `LodgingMass` are semantic parents, **not implemented archetype symbols**. A08 additionally needs mapped `SiteAssembly`; A05 needs `AttachedMass`. Named `chicago-02-flats`, `denver-01-square` and `miami-05-suburban` / `townhouse-module` entries are existing house-pack **analogies**, not proof these 16 variants are wired. All new subtype IDs are the stable A/O/H IDs in variants.json. No pack approval, generator integration or tracker edit is implied.
