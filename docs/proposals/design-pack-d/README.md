## Cameras and viewing

Each sheet has a near street three-quarter view and aerial three-quarter view; parking, sports and playground also have a bottom plan view. Prompt fixture: street eye 1.7 m, 40 m horizontal setback from the near edge, 45° azimuth to the long axis; aerial 150 m AGL, 45° down; plan orthographic vertical. This interprets “40 m street-level 45°” as distance and azimuth, not a 40 m camera height. The AI pictures are approximate camera compositions, not calibrated engine captures. Large-site near views may crop the far edge. West sun at 40° elevation; cast shadows east. No labels, signs, real places or characters in artwork. Region is marked in the index/specs outside the artwork.
