# Recommended-area block-mix paint-overs

[Open gallery](block-mix-gallery.html) · [Prompts, lighting and provenance](block-mix-values.json)

Four proposed block mixes use the test areas and look notes in metros.csv. This is an illustrative local mixture, not a surveyed reconstruction or measured archetype frequency.

- Brooklyn Heights: attached brownstone/brick rowhouses, stoops/areaways, tree pits and paved frontage; a compatible apartment building, no suburban front lawns.
- Angelino Heights: Victorian bays/roof variety with Craftsman and Mission Revival neighbours, hillside placement, diverse green trees and mixed small lawn/drought planting.
- Coronado: bungalow, early ranch, Spanish Colonial Revival and English cottage examples; varied irrigated lawns, shade pockets and low-water yards. Cool Corridors species are not asserted as a Coronado tree census.
- Queen Anne/Kerry Park: Craftsman/Tudor/cottage mix with an occasional modern replacement; sloping street, terraced yards, stepped foundations and downhill daylight basements.

All exact mixes, building placements, finishes, retaining treatments and visibility are proposals. Candidate-specific primary evidence links are retained in block-mix-values.json and the research CSV. The existing Denver engine render is the camera-composition reference because no corresponding engine captures were found for these four areas. It is not a claim of camera-pose equality or existing NYC/LA/Phoenix/Seattle geography. Seattle slope and LA hillside are deliberate proposed terrain adaptations.

The approved house-contrast-v1 shared mid-afternoon lighting object is copied unchanged. The approved Denver hero image is the visual style/light reference. All four use the same clear-sky lighting test fixture; this does not model each metro's typical weather or seasonal distribution. The illustrations target the fixture but are not calibrated renderer outputs or proof of numerical exposure/pixel equality. No logos, real addresses or public-art replicas are requested.

Images were created with the built-in image-generation tool. Full prompts and input-image hashes are saved in block-mix-values.json. Underlying render map attribution: [© OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
