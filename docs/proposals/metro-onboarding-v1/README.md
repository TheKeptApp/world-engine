# Metro onboarding v1 — NYC, Los Angeles, Phoenix, Seattle

Research checked **7 October 2026**. Recommended first tests are residential areas with distinctive forms and terrain, followed by the dense alternatives. These are lead-agent design recommendations. Eight approximately 1.5 × 1.5 km boxes are proposed in [metros.csv](metros.csv); coordinates are not surveyed neighbourhood boundaries. [datasets.csv](datasets.csv) contains 61 dataset/access/licence entries. [sources.md](sources.md) centralizes exact excerpts and source links.

## Data and look decisions

Reuse the local `regional-look-catalog-v1` house families and street-character notes for these four metros. Its shares, hexes, roof pitches and dimensional defaults remain proposals, not measured prevalences. New local additions are LA Victorian forms in Angelino Heights, NYC dense commercial/tenement massing, Phoenix Coronado's documented mixture and Seattle slope/daylight-basement handling. Observed footprints, elevations and reliable height fields override procedural defaults; source URLs are carried with the relevant CSV fields.

NYC has a city footprint dataset with height and year-built fields, alongside PLUTO and an older 3D model. LA has footprint HEIGHT/ELEV attributes and a documented OSM import. Seattle has 2023 building outlines. A dedicated Phoenix municipal footprint/3D release remains unclear. These do not establish current coverage inside our eight boxes. Do not substitute parcel boundaries for building footprints. [NYC building metadata](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md), [LA service](https://maps.lacity.org/lahub/rest/services/Landbase_Information_WFS/MapServer/0), [Seattle outlines metadata](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/building-outlines-2023), [Phoenix public service directory](https://maps.phoenix.gov/pub/rest/services/public).

Overture offers global buildings and monthly releases, but no candidate-box completeness percentage was established here. Its buildings theme is ODbL; it is not a permissive escape from OSM share-alike obligations. USGS 3DEP is a useful terrain/lidar source; exact local project dates, quality and tile intersections still require verification. No bulk geographic data, imagery or feed archives were downloaded. [Overture buildings guide](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/buildings/), [theme licences](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/), [3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program).

Use weather-driven skies and species-driven seasonal vegetation. NYC has nor’easter/coastal-flood/snow considerations, rather than a default lake-effect treatment. LA needs coastal low-cloud versus inland-clear variation and offshore fire-weather events. Phoenix needs monsoon cloud, thunderstorms and episodic dust, with no permanent orange haze. Seattle needs rainy-season cloud and a drier summer rather than perpetual rain. The CSV provides local NWS offices and primary climate links. [NYC storm archive](https://www.weather.gov/okx/stormarchive), [LA observations](https://www.weather.gov/lox/observations), [Phoenix monsoon](https://www.weather.gov/psr/2025MonsoonSeasonReview), [Seattle climate study](https://www.weather.gov/media/wrh/online_publications/TMs/TM-257.pdf).

## Licence interpretation and top risks

**GREEN** means an affirmative open-use or redistribution basis was identified, subject to its conditions. **YELLOW** means permission, item-specific inheritance, commercial scope or onward redistribution needs clarification. **RED** means the stated use is restricted under the inspected terms without separate consent. A RED website-media row is not a blanket ban on every city open dataset. Item metadata, a public URL, an email copyright field and an access disclaimer do not by themselves grant redistribution. Exact quotes in the CSV point to Q identifiers in sources.md; some excerpts demonstrate missing permission or restrictions rather than grants.

- **NYC:** Strong city open-data permission, but additional agency terms and source/version notices remain relevant. MTA requires WorldEngine's server to mediate user access, a notice when realtime lag exceeds one minute, and preservation of supplied data. Maps, logos and symbols have separate licensing rules. Subway trip updates are not measured street-level vehicle coordinates. [City FAQ](https://www.nyc.gov/opendata/get-started/FAQs), [city public policies](https://cityofnewyork.github.io/opendatatsm/publicpolicies.html), [MTA terms](https://www.mta.info/developers/terms-and-conditions), [developer resources](https://www.mta.info/developers).
- **LA:** Footprint source age and the older sidewalk inventory need visual spot checks; the sidewalk metadata says it is not actively maintained. Keep city open releases distinct from licensed LARIAC consortium imagery/lidar. Metro's current feed/key and agency-specific redistribution terms were not verified; Swiftly vendor terms are conditional evidence only, not proof that they govern every Metro endpoint. [Sidewalk metadata](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=10854b6040a74950abeab5502c69fe77), [LARIAC historical licence evidence](https://egis7.gis.lacounty.gov/hub/lariac_documents/LARIAC3_kickOff_county_overview_20100729.pdf), [Metro API](https://api.metro.net/), [Swiftly terms](https://www.goswift.ly/api-license-agreement).
- **Phoenix:** Most examined city GIS items lack a verified commercial-redistribution grant. The paid Landbase agreement requires city consent for onward distribution; it does not automatically govern every public REST layer. Assessor year-built data have separate policies. Cool Corridors polygons carry project species/count information, not exact positions of individual trees. Valley Metro's current official GTFS-RT access/terms remain unclear after failed retrieval; city rail geometry is not realtime data. [GIS agreement](https://www.phoenix.gov/streetssite/documents/eas_pdf_gis_request_packet.pdf), [city copyright/portal scope](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/communications/copyright.html), [Assessor policy route](https://www.maricopa.gov/509/Data-Requests), [Cool Corridors layer](https://maps.phoenix.gov/pub/rest/services/Public/STR_CoolCorridors/MapServer/0), [Valley Metro developer route — retrieval unverified](https://www.valleymetro.org/developers).
- **Seattle:** Verify current SDOT item licences rather than relying solely on collection-level PDDL. One Assessor GIS extract is explicitly non-public; a separate official download route exists but its reuse terms still need checking. Regional consolidated static GTFS ends at the end of 2026: integrate agency feeds and namespace IDs. King County Metro and Sound Transit have different data conditions. Space Needle branding/likeness and artist copyrights need separate treatment. [SDOT collection](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/sdot-gis-datasets), [Assessor GIS status](https://www5.kingcounty.gov/sdc?Layer=resbldg_extr), [Assessor alternative](https://info.kingcounty.gov/assessor/DataDownload/default.aspx), [GTFS decommissioning](https://www.soundtransit.org/help-contacts/business-information/open-transit-data-otd/consolidated-schedule-decommissioning), [Space Needle policy](https://www.spaceneedle.com/trademark).

For shared OSM/Overture data, retain attribution and satisfy applicable derivative-database notice/share-alike/source-offer conditions. Distinguish the distributed database and its produced visual works from WorldEngine's software; the precise tile/mesh packaging classification needs review before sublicensing. NWS's API is free open data with reasonable unspecified rate limits and a required User-Agent; NOAA/NWS marks are separate. [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [OSM attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [NWS API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api).

Landmark designation is not itself proof that geographical building depiction is forbidden. US copyright law allows specified pictorial representations of constructed buildings visible from public places, but this does not clear trademark uses, separate sculptures/murals, protected imagery or every exported 3D asset. Use registers to flag assets for review; omit logos and exact public-art replicas until cleared. No owner/contact/occupancy information is needed for building-age enrichment. [17 USC §120](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#120), [NYC public art](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dcla/publicart/publicart.page), [Seattle artist rights](https://www.seattle.gov/documents/Departments/Arts/Downloads/Public%20Art%20Roadmap/Roadmap_Section3.pdf).

## Verify before ingestion

1. Freeze the eight proposed boxes and check both representative blocks and skyline cameras in the engine.
2. Measure OSM footprints against an independent, appropriately licensed local reference; report polygon recall/precision, not raw object-count ratio.
3. Run count-only OSM tag audits for `height`, `building:levels`, `roof:shape`, `roof:height`, `roof:angle` and `roof:levels`, keeping complete buildings separate from `building:part` objects. Tag presence is not value accuracy. Record timestamp and query. [Overpass count/union syntax](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API/Overpass_QL).
4. For each box, union `height` OR `building:levels` before calculating height-information coverage, and report each roof tag separately. Do not turn global Taginfo statistics or historical import completion into local percentages.
5. Inspect Overture source, confidence, geometry and optional height fields in the same boxes. Deduplicate against OSM to prevent doubled buildings. No numerical result is claimed by this research. [Overture schema](https://docs.overturemaps.org/schema/reference/buildings/building/).
6. Confirm vertical units/datum and ground-versus-roof height; reject zeros, nulls and placeholders before extrusion. Check terrain/project age and slope interactions. [NYC metadata](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md), [3DEP products](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services).
7. Resolve YELLOW city/assessor licences, LA vendor-versus-agency transit terms and Valley Metro access in writing before distributing affected data. Confirm Seattle feed migration and MTA server/staleness requirements.
8. Review public-art and isolated branded-landmark assets separately; maintain a source/licence manifest for shipped layers.

The lead and one sub-agent per metro reviewed primary documentation. No blocked-site bypass, bulk dataset download or git operation was performed. Missing permissions, byte sizes, local tag percentages and camera visibility are deliberately marked unclear or unverified.
