## Authored / unverified

Every object envelope, motion speed, seasonal multiplier, activity count, customer pattern, placement rule and calendar window in `values.json` is an **authored scene preset**. None is presented as a measured New York average, supplier specification, current municipal policy, live observation or actual service timetable.

Generated artwork is illustrative. Exact perspective, camera elevation, physical lighting calibration and metre accuracy remain unverified. The PNG sheets are raster art-direction deliverables; JSON is the modeling handoff. Original generated paths, actual reference inputs and hashes are retained in `generation-manifest.json`.

The horse-carriage exception follows the user's explicit request. The prohibition on dogs and animals walked on leads remains in force.
