# Sources and evidence register

Research cutoff and access date: **2026-10-07**. Primary official documents, vendor documentation, and original research are used below. A vendor claim is not a tested WorldEngine capability. Historical figures retain their reference year. Commercial prices may change; conflicting pages require checkout confirmation. No inaccessible evidence is treated as verified.

CSV source URLs are direct links; M identifiers refer to market records below. Parts references establish parametric tooling only: catalogue selection, modelling bounds and priorities are proposed, not observed usage frequency or supplier specifications.
