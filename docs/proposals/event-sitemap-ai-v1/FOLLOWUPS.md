# Event Site-map AI · schema and import review

Two follow-ups completed on 7 October 2026. Existing research is retained. No git. The validation pilot plan was skipped.

1. [Parametric contract documentation](parametric-schema/README.md), [versioned schema](parametric-schema/kit.schema.json), and [ten complete part definitions](parametric-schema/examples/catalog.json).
2. [Interactive import review](import-review/index.html), [screen boards](import-review/screen-boards.html), and [flow/state documentation](import-review/README.md).

The schema covers typed parameters, canonical units, modelling bounds, planning and unverified supplier-size presets, footprint/clearance/queue/utility envelopes, Style B material slots, detail tiers, geographic/route/array placement, provenance and approval history. Included examples are stage, canopy tent, water table, barrier, finish arch, toilet block, gate, sign, generator and sponsor booth.

The import concept covers upload, page/crop, scale/units/north, anchor fit and independent check point, detection draft, per-item side-by-side review, unresolved triage, homogeneous group approval and final summary. It includes desktop/tablet/phone captures and blocked alignment/group states. Its exported approved scene was checked against the new kit contract.

Verification: the schema's 13 example documents pass Draft 2020-12 plus semantic checks; eight invalid cases are rejected. The normal sample browser flow passes with 16 approved objects, and the 16-object scene export passes schema/semantic validation. Viewport checks cover 1440×900, 1024×768 and 390×844. Reports are saved with each deliverable.

Limits are explicit: supplier candidates and bounds are design assumptions; scene, recognition and scores are synthetic. The prototype is a design review tool, not an operational importer or engineering approval system.
