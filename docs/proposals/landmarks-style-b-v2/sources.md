## Verify first

1. NY: One World Trade Center spire versus roof endpoint and faceted transition; Empire State setbacks; exact Deco crown counts and sculpture-free top.
2. Brooklyn Bridge: bridge/deck elevations, suspension versus diagonal-stay topology, portal dimensions and actual mapped river alignment.
3. Grand Central: real footprint and road viaduct relationship; check every rooftop/relief figure is absent.
4. LA City Hall: reconcile 454-ft city-clerk versus 464-ft historical public-works height statements and their datums.
5. Griffith Observatory: roof/dome proportions, actual hilltop terrain and city viewshed; do not infer building height from hill elevation.
6. San Francisco Coit Tower: resolve 212-ft official brochure versus other 210-ft values; the entire geometry stays unverified.
7. Golden Gate Bridge: tower top above water versus foundation datum, suspension span and channel alignment.
8. Smith Tower: reconcile operator's 469-ft completion and 522-ft pinnacle claims before numeric adoption. Keep null meanwhile.
9. Seattle: library cantilevers/diamond lattice and tower lobes; Queen Anne slope/camera, bay side and mountain visibility from actual map.
10. DC: Capitol published 288 ft includes excluded rooftop statue. Do not use that as the sculpture-free shell height; White House historic shell scope excludes changing annex works.
11. Philadelphia: remove rooftop/attached figures and use an architectural endpoint rather than full statue-inclusive height; courtyard and tower topology need mapped validation.
12. Atlanta/Arlington/Phoenix/Pasadena/Boston venues: actual bounded footprints, roof poses, span heights, bowl topology and absence of all graphics; never apply city-center skyline placement to suburban venues.
13. DFW and Houston: keep distinct real clusters and landmarks in their own city/district; skyline concept panes do not establish sightlines.
14. Austin/Nashville: resolve glass-crown, twin-spire and classical shell geometry against official plans; primary identity references are not surveyed dimensions.
15. Museum/campus compounds: bound the exact Kahn-building, Lovett Hall and Taliesin West exterior scope; exclude adjacent art; obtain intended-use rights review and geometry provenance before release.
