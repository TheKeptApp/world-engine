## Music and sound

Quiet instrumental pulse, 88–96 BPM, sparse felt-piano notes, steady movement. No vocals, trailer swell or drop. Low street air, restrained rain onset, a subdued rail pass and one placement click. Duck music under speech. Use cleared original or licensed sound; no commercial track is prescribed.
