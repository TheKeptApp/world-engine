# Style B Bible v1
## Proportions real. Surfaces simplified. Light soft.

**Default WorldEngine Style B:** a believable real-scale world with restrained materials and detail. Architecture, vehicles, people, trees and infrastructure must share the realism level of the approved Empower Field stadium sheet and house-archetypes-v1. Simplification removes surface information; it does not turn the subject into a toy.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep real wheelbase, body length/height, hood, cabin, roof slope and wheel diameter. | Shorten a car into a stubby block, inflate wheels, or make a cartoon cab. |
| Use normal adult body/head/limb ratios, natural posture and readable movement. | Enlarge heads/hands/feet or use doll, chibi or mascot anatomy. |
| Keep real floor heights, window rhythm, structural spans, footprint distances and rail-car length. | Compress streets, shrink a stadium, thicken train cabs or resize objects for visibility. |
| Use broad matte albedo, opaque restrained glass and selective geometry for meaningful recesses. | Add photographic textures, dense brick/grain patterns, panel-line noise, plastic gloss or illustrative outlines. |
| Let real silhouette curvature survive mesh simplification; organics stay smoothly shaded. | Treat a low triangle target as permission for angular bodies, balloon crowns or faceted skin. |
| Inherit soft, coherent world lighting; maintain useful recess/contact darkness. | Give each asset a separate spotlight, orange wash, purple shadows or bright rim outline. |
| Reduce detail with projected size while preserving scale and identity. | Enlarge tiny cars/people, inflate cabs or thicken every detail to keep it readable. |

**Calibration light:** the exact sharedLighting object from house-archetypes-v1 is inherited unchanged: illustrative sun elevation40°, azimuth225°, +0.35EV, contrast1.06, saturation1.08, no additional warmth. Sun #FFE8C6; neutral sky fill #AEBCCA; shadow cue #7F8F99. These are authored review targets, not measured image pixels or a promise of renderer equivalence. Actual location/time/weather overrides this illustrative fixture.

**Car test:** a proposed generic sedan remains4.5 ×1.8 ×1.45m, with wheel diameter approximately0.62–0.70m and wheelbase approximately2.6–2.8m. Keep a believable hood/cabin/trunk relationship. These illustrative ratios are art calibration, not a surveyed specific make. The paired image shows a rejected toy-style interpretation and the accepted proportion direction under the same light. Do not use the rejected half as a production reference.

**One-world test:** hide the labels and compare a person beside the bus, the bus beside the three-flat and the train beside its viaduct. If any looks like a different game's miniature, revise proportions before adding surface detail. The calibration scene deliberately combines Chicago house families, an unmarked stadium edge, elevated rail and a glimpse of lake; it is an art-test composite, not surveyed adjacent geography.

**Tier test:** near retains recognition-critical form, mid merges details, far keeps real-scale silhouette/color. The current48/16/5px ambient thresholds and density/performance budgets remain unchanged; they do not authorize cartoon proportion exaggeration.

**Style C – Illustrated concept:** the earlier chunky kit is preserved separately. It is an alternative illustrated concept, not default Style B. No logos, identifying faces or dog-on-lead asset in the revised Style B kit.


## Transport realism anchor · approved 7 October 2026

[Japan regional kit](../japan-regional-kit-v1/index.html), specifically its [vehicles](../japan-regional-kit-v1/05-vehicles.html), [rail](../japan-regional-kit-v1/06-rail-vehicles.html) and [stations](../japan-regional-kit-v1/08-stations.html), is the controlling reference for **all vehicles, trains and boats**, alongside Empower Field and house-archetypes-v1. Match its real class proportions, smooth matte simplified surfaces and restrained detail; adapt silhouettes and dimensions to US forms rather than copying Japanese vehicle types. Boats inherit this treatment; that kit has no boat-specific sheet.

US road scenes use right-hand traffic, normally left-hand driver placement and curb-side/right-side bus boarding; Japan retains left-hand traffic. Rail direction/platform placement and vessel operations follow local/source data, not an automatic road-traffic mirror. Preserve the ambient kit's existing tiers, palettes and density budgets. Existing US illustrations are supporting concepts; the Japan transport anchor takes precedence where their surface detail differs.
