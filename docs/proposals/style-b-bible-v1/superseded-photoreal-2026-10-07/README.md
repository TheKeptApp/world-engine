## Transport realism anchor · approved 7 October 2026

[Japan regional kit](../japan-regional-kit-v1/index.html), specifically its [vehicles](../japan-regional-kit-v1/05-vehicles.html), [rail](../japan-regional-kit-v1/06-rail-vehicles.html) and [stations](../japan-regional-kit-v1/08-stations.html), is the controlling reference for **all vehicles, trains and boats**, alongside Empower Field and house-archetypes-v1. Match its real class proportions, smooth matte simplified surfaces and restrained detail; adapt silhouettes and dimensions to US forms rather than copying Japanese vehicle types. Boats inherit this treatment; that kit has no boat-specific sheet.

US road scenes use right-hand traffic, normally left-hand driver placement and curb-side/right-side bus boarding; Japan retains left-hand traffic. Rail direction/platform placement and vessel operations follow local/source data, not an automatic road-traffic mirror. Preserve the ambient kit's existing tiers, palettes and density budgets. Existing US illustrations are supporting concepts; the Japan transport anchor takes precedence where their surface detail differs.
