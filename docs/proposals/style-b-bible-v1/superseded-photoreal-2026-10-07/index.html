One-page style rule

Realism comes from proportion, scale and coherent light. Simplification removes surface information while keeping real silhouettes and meaningful recesses.

Do

  • Real wheelbase, hood/cabin/trunk and wheel size.
  • Normal adult anatomy and natural posture.
  • Real structural spans, floors and object spacing.
  • Smooth matte planes and restrained opaque glass.
  • Soft daylight, useful recess/contact shadows.
  • Lose detail with screen size; keep scale.

Don't

  • Stubby cars, cartoon cabs or balloon wheels.
  • Doll heads, oversized hands/feet or mascot bodies.
  • Compressed streets or miniature stadiums/trains.
  • Photo textures, noisy seams or plastic gloss.
  • Orange washes, purple shadows or rim outlines.
  • Enlarge distant people/vehicles for readability.
