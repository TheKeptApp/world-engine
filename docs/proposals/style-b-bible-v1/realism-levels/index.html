One street, three realism levels

Chicago calibration composite: brick bungalow, three-flat, unmarked stadium edge, generic sedan/SUV/bus, elevated train, three anonymous pedestrians, street trees and a lake glimpse. Approved soft mid-afternoon lighting.

The latest direction retains the accepted rich vehicle/person realism while removing make/model cues and identity. A is the current Japan-kit Style B baseline; B and C are comparison options, not a change to the production target. The same source composition was edited for each level; generative images have small local differences and are not a pixel-registered renderer benchmark.

A) Rich Style B

Japan regional kit level: real proportions, matte broad forms, restrained seams, simplified glass and foliage.

Hard on a realtime phone engine · Medium

Preserving convincing silhouettes at low mesh counts; smooth crown shading, stable contact shadows and consistent colour across an entire city. Dense foliage and elevated infrastructure still need instancing and careful LOD.

B) Grounded realism

Realistic brick, asphalt, foliage and glass; near-photoreal generic vehicles and anonymous people; buildings slightly simplified.

Hard on a realtime phone engine · High

Coherent PBR material authoring and streaming, glass/environment reflections, foliage overdraw, softer indirect light, contact shadows and natural human animation. Control texture frequency to avoid shimmer; use bounded probes rather than a live reflection camera per object.

C) Full photoreal

Photographic materials, subtle weathering, detailed foliage, realistic sky and reflections; same anonymous people and fictional transport.

Hard on a realtime phone engine · Very high

High asset and texture memory, fine geometry, complex foliage, realistic reflections/transparency, indirect light and shadow quality, temporal stability and sustained GPU/thermal load. A photographic still does not establish a viable 60 fps phone renderer.

What stays cheap

Keep the existing ambient near/mid/far tiers: ≥48 / ≥16 / ≥5 CSS pixels, with hysteresis and optional-life culling below5px. Reduce seams, wheels, clothing and animation detail by tier; keep real proportions. No face or identity details at any tier. These existing thresholds remain design proposals, not a device benchmark.
