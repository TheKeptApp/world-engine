Two-panel eye-level street and identical 45-degree aerial of a Japanese residential lane beside a small neighborhood park: low neutral plaster and wood houses, tiled shallow gables, compact walls and gardens, no temple replica. Only the small cluster of mapped-style cherry trees along park edge has pale pink blossoms; ordinary street trees are green/new leaf and evergreen vegetation persists. Two modest picnic mats with a few generic non-identifying adults and children in park, no house decoration. No every-tree pink transformation, giant lantern parade, brands, readable signs, dogs or faces. Genuine local bloom event, not every April day.
