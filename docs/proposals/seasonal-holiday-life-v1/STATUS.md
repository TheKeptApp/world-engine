**Owner lane:** P2, 5A, L1. **Phase:** Ambient life layer, after the look gate.

54 AI-generated scene pairs (street plus 45-degree aerial) and a data file for seasonal and holiday street life: ten states on each of five US blocks (Chicago, Denver, Greenville, New York, San Francisco) plus four regional celebrations, with proposed decoration shares and date windows.

Binding rules from the pack:
- Packs own content, calibration v2 owns look: no per-city grade, exposure or material override; live time and weather replace the clear-day fixture, never a second grade. No logos, brands, readable signs, dogs, animals or host-app content; people are generic with non-identifying faces; striped and star flags only for July 4.
- All percentages are art-direction proposals, not prevalence; the denominator is a residential building frontage (a flats building counts once). Stable cosmetic seed per building and event, never race, religion, income or belief; installed lights and finished jobs persist. City targets already include street type: apply the street multiplier only when moving to another type.
- Props only on real eligible private land: none on sidewalks, roads or invented lawns. Rare summer extras per 100 eligible frontages: 3 sprinklers, 2 pools, 1 lemonade stand. Denver sprinklers off (irrigation ban); unknown water policy disables spray. A block party needs a verified permit or authored event: off by default, one per block.
- Weather and plants decide the environment, not the calendar: snow only from current observed depth (unknown means no snow; SF shows a wet, no-snow fallback); leaves and blossom from species stage (dates are only a search hint); evergreens never go bare; no automatic Christmas snow; unknown inputs keep a neutral state.
- Date windows are proposed envelopes with a ramp (lights: 20 percent on 20 Nov, 70 on 1 Dec, full 15 Dec). Other countries get no US Thanksgiving or July 4 default; Dia de Muertos keeps its flower and offerings context, never a Halloween swap, no portraits; Kings Day has no royal portraits.
- Night strings: 70 percent warm white, 20 mixed colour, 10 cool white; on when the sun is below -3 degrees, off at 23:00 (New Year until 01:30); no strobe, no giant bloom, no city-wide orange grade; use photometric or relative emission once, never both.

Authored or unverified: (1) Every percentage (0 to 45), display window, ramp, light value, prop size and job threshold (2 cm snow, 20 percent leaf cover, 1.5x mowing height) is an authored proposal. No household survey, measured prevalence or measured lighting; Halloween scenes cite no source. (2) Base blocks, the 45-degree aerial and cross-image identity are generated and unproven: no mesh, camera matrix or engine test. Sample states (10 cm snow, sprinklers off) are authored scenarios, not observations. The phone check is browser layout only, no device performance test. (3) Sourced (official pages the pack says it checked 2026-10-07; not re-fetched here): 2026-27 school first days (Chicago Aug 24, Greenville Aug 11, New York Sep 10, San Francisco Aug 17; Denver unclear), US holiday dates, Kings Day rule, Denver Water ban. All else authored. (4) No live feed, schedule or event data was ingested. Real household decorations, permits, snow-clearing deadlines and community practice (Dia de Muertos, Kings Day, hanami) are unknown and need local confirmation; school dates cover 2026-27 only.
