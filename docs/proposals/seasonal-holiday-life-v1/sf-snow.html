When it appears

display: None; peak: None; displayRule: any date with observed current depth; no calendar window

PROPOSAL display envelope, except cited event dates; weather/phenology gates take priority

Required gate

confirmed current local ground snow depth; snowman requires moist packing snow; sled requires safe mapped terrain. Any date is possible if data supports it. If missing data, use no-snow fallback.

Regional look

Sloping bay-front Victorian block, compact stoops and planting pockets. Evergreen/deciduous mix and species-specific leaf change. Snow-day sheet uses a no-snow wet-day fallback unless verified local snow depth exists. SF snow props remain disabled without independently verified local ground depth. The sheet deliberately shows the fallback.

Sparse participation

0% of residential building exteriors carry these decorations/activity props as a tuning target.

PROPOSAL: conservative art-direction target, not surveyed prevalence; persistent seed; affected buildings counted once

  • patchy or continuous observed snow depth
  • one snowman if packing snow exists
  • one plain sled on eligible gentle private slope
  • mixed cleared/uncleared walks

Snow/leaf coverage is environmental, not a decorated-house percentage. School and hanami have no house decorations. Small samples approximate percentages through sparse rounded counts.

Time / lighting

overcast_day; calibration v2 material/exposure rules persist while local sun/weather replace the clear-day fixture.

Neighborhood Jobs

Shovel snow

new observed ground accumulation >=2 cm on a selected uncleared walk OR safe snow-clearing task; positive snowfall forecast can queue, not render completion

Completion: mark mapped walk cleared, move snow within eligible property; preserve drains/accessible route

Scale and detail

  • <6 px: regional silhouette, snow/foliage state, a tiny sparse warm light cluster; cull standalone decorations/actors. Omit pumpkin faces, bags, individual bulbs, pool clutter
  • 6-20 px: grouped porch props, leaf pile/pool void, warm strings as a few points; real scale. Omit readable details, tiny faces, bulb wiring
  • >20 px: plain gourd/pool/snowman geometry, a few leaf/petal shapes, sparse generic people. Omit brands, identifying faces, text, religious-only symbols unless separately requested

Generic real-scale props: pumpkins 0.18-0.35 m diameter; pool 1.2-2 m diameter; leaf pile 0.1-0.4 m high; snowman 0.7-1.3 m. Authoring proposals.
