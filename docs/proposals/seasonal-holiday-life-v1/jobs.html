Tasks are simulated/authored. Do not claim knowledge of a real property owner’s behavior. Local obligations and permits need jurisdiction-specific validation.

Hang lights

local chosen display opens, selected frontage opted in, not installed, dry/unfrozen access, daylight/safe weather; equipment/access checked

Completion: store installed style/date; removal becomes a separate job after display end

PROPOSAL task rules

Rake leaves

actual simulated/observed local leaf deposition covers about >20% of eligible path/lawn OR selected leaf pile cleanup; tree phenophase alone insufficient

Completion: reduce local loose leaves, gather selective pile/bags according to local collection rules; retain selected bed leaves

20% is a tuning proposal, not ecological/legal standard

Shovel snow

new observed ground accumulation >=2 cm on a selected uncleared walk OR safe snow-clearing task; positive snowfall forecast can queue, not render completion

Completion: mark mapped walk cleared, move snow within eligible property; preserve drains/accessible route

2 cm gameplay tuning; local legal deadlines unclear until jurisdiction verified

Mow lawn

living turf actively growing and height >=1.5 x grass-specific target, safe dry unfrozen ground and no heat/drought stress

Completion: remove <=one third of current height per cut; suppress job on gravel/xeriscape/paved frontage

one-third rule primary-source guidance; target height chosen by species/site

Take lights down

chosen display ended + dry safe daylight + previously installed strings exist

Completion: remove only previously installed set; no new religious/cultural inference
