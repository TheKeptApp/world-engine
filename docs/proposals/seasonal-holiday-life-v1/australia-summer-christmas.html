Two-panel eye-level street plus identical 45-degree aerial of a quiet temperate Australian suburb, low brick and pale weatherboard houses, simple shallow hipped roofs, verandahs, eucalyptus street trees, mixed green and dry lawn depending soil moisture. December warm-weather Christmas example, just about a third of houses with generic unlit roof-edge strings/plain wreaths, shaded backyard outdoor dining table, one private small pool if eligible. Daylight, no snow, no autumn-bare European canopy, no snowman, no northern-winter wardrobe or giant Santa. No religious symbols, brands, readable text or dogs. Retain realistic proportions and the calibration material response.
