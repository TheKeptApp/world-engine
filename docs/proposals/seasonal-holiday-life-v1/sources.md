# Primary sources and uncertainty

No source photographs were used as asset downloads. Cultural timing and environmental guidance are separated from authored prevalence/visual proposals. Sources were checked through official search results/pages on 2026-10-07; detailed dataset ingestion is not implemented here.

## phenology

USA-NPN first leaf/bloom indices use recent temperature; a regional spring index is not a species-specific tree observation. [https://usanpn.org/data/maps/spring](https://usanpn.org/data/maps/spring).

## snow

Snow analyses include depth, temperature and melt, drawing on observations and modeled conditions; snowfall accumulation is not equivalent to current ground depth. [https://www.nohrsc.noaa.gov/help/](https://www.nohrsc.noaa.gov/help/).

## holidays

US New Year January 1; Independence Day July 4; Thanksgiving fourth Thursday in November; Christmas December 25. Work-observed dates do not shift exterior celebration dates. [https://www.opm.gov/frequently-asked-questions/pay-and-leave-faq/pay-administration/what-are-federal-holidays/?os=v](https://www.opm.gov/frequently-asked-questions/pay-and-leave-faq/pay-administration/what-are-federal-holidays/?os=v).

## cps

CPS K-12 first day for 2026-27 is August 24, 2026; calendar must be refreshed yearly. [https://www.cps.edu/sites/back-to-school/](https://www.cps.edu/sites/back-to-school/).

## dps

DPS publishes school-year calendars. A district-wide first day was not verified here; use selected school calendar, not one school as a district proxy. [https://newlon.dpsk12.org/o/dps/page/calendars](https://newlon.dpsk12.org/o/dps/page/calendars).

## gcs

Greenville County Schools 2026-27 student first day is August 11, 2026. [https://www.greenville.k12.sc.us/News/main.asp?titleid=2412calendar](https://www.greenville.k12.sc.us/News/main.asp?titleid=2412calendar).

## nycschool

NYC public school 2026-27 first day is September 10, 2026. Other school systems can differ. [https://www.schools.nyc.gov/school-year/back-to-school](https://www.schools.nyc.gov/school-year/back-to-school).

## sfusd

SFUSD 2026-27 first day is August 17, 2026. [https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/back-school-2026-27](https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/back-school-2026-27).

## sfclimate

Official downtown SF climate reference; do not extrapolate a fixed snow or leaf calendar to individual blocks. [https://www.weather.gov/mtr/sfd_climate](https://www.weather.gov/mtr/sfd_climate).

## gspclimate

NWS local climate tables include Greenville-Spartanburg snowfall/temperature resources; climate normals are not live event triggers. [https://www.weather.gov/gsp/climate_local](https://www.weather.gov/gsp/climate_local).

## water

As checked 2026-10-07, Denver Water prohibits lawn watering and spray irrigation beginning October 1 until further action. This takes priority over older routine summer guidance. [https://www.denverwater.org/drought](https://www.denverwater.org/drought).

## watersummer

Routine summer lawn watering excludes 10 a.m.-6 p.m.; 2026 drought policy can be stricter. Query current policy before activation. [https://www.denverwater.org/tap/get-facts-summer-watering-rules-run-may-1-oct-1](https://www.denverwater.org/tap/get-facts-summer-watering-rules-run-may-1-oct-1).

## mow

Species-specific cutting heights; remove no more than one third of grass height per mowing. Growth, not month, supplies the task. [https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/mowing-lawns/](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/mowing-lawns/).

## grass

Grass choice and mowing response depend on site conditions and grass type; avoid mowing wet turf. [https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/turfgrasses-for-the-carolinas/](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/turfgrasses-for-the-carolinas/).

## canada

Canada Day July 1; Thanksgiving second Monday in October. Country holiday dates are not evidence for decoration prevalence. [https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/public/visit/holiday-closures.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/public/visit/holiday-closures.html).

## uk

Use the local council/school to resolve term and holiday dates; one UK-wide school-start date is inappropriate. [https://www.gov.uk/school-term-holiday-dates](https://www.gov.uk/school-term-holiday-dates).

## ukautumn

UK autumn includes changing leaves and unsettled weather. This is context, not a per-tree phenology observation. [https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/seasons/autumn](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/seasons/autumn).

## nl

Kings Day is April 27, moved to the previous day when April 27 is Sunday. [https://www.royal-house.nl/topics/monarchy/king%E2%80%99s-day](https://www.royal-house.nl/topics/monarchy/king%E2%80%99s-day).

## nlcalendar

Government lists official annual Dutch holiday dates; 2026 Kings Day is April 27. [https://www.government.nl/faq/work/public-holidays-in-the-netherlands](https://www.government.nl/faq/work/public-holidays-in-the-netherlands).

## mexico

Día de Muertos festivities occur at the end of October/beginning of November; documented practices include flower petals, candles and offerings. Practices vary by community. [https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/dia-de-muertos-the-indigenous-festivity-dedicated-to-the-dead-00054](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/dia-de-muertos-the-indigenous-festivity-dedicated-to-the-dead-00054).

## australia

Southern temperate Australia summer is December-February. Northern tropics use wet/dry regimes; hemisphere alone is not a climate classification. [https://www.bom.gov.au/news-and-media/solstices-equinoxes-and-the-seasons](https://www.bom.gov.au/news-and-media/solstices-equinoxes-and-the-seasons).

## japan

Bloom progresses by location from roughly March to May; forecasts change with weather. Hanami requires actual local blossom state, not an April-wide overlay. [https://www.japan.travel/en/see-and-do/cherry-blossom-forecast/](https://www.japan.travel/en/see-and-do/cherry-blossom-forecast/).

## Unverified/proposed

All participation shares, prevalence, prop dimensions, lighting intensities, micro-block rounding, task thresholds and candidate display envelopes are authoring proposals. No household survey was used. Base-block geometry, precise aerial angle and across-frame camera identity require engine verification. Real household decorations, sidewalk clearing, religious/cultural participation and school routes are not known. Unknown water policies/permits suppress affected activity. Missing phenology/snow inputs select neutral/no-snow fallback rather than seasonal invention. Per-tree species is required before species-specific blossoms; spring indices do not supply an autumn phenology map. District/year calendars must be refreshed; Denver exact first day remains unclear. Community-specific Día de Muertos, Kings Day and park picnic practices require local confirmation.
