## Licence handling

[Toronto OGL](https://open.toronto.ca/open-data-licence/) permits commercial copying, adaptation and redistribution with attribution; excludes personal information, third-party rights and protected identifiers. Required credit: “Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto.”

[Vancouver OGL](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/) is the licence named on the lidar/tree pages; commercial grant was visible in the official search index. Full licence page rendering was incomplete: **UNVERIFIED archived full-text retrieval and exact final attribution wording**. Pin that licence text before distributing derived assets. Ordinary City website imagery is not automatically covered.

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial sharing and adaptation with credit, licence link and indication of changes. It does not clear privacy or every third-party right.

[OGL Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) v2 permits commercial copying, adaptation and redistribution with credit and exclusions. Official indexed text checked; direct page access failed. Attribution: “Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.”

MSC attribution: “Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada”. Retain upstream-originator credit and unaltered warning intent. Licence header says 2.1.1; the definition text still refers to 2.1, so archive the exact release used.

## Audit before launch

Sample the six pictured districts with fixed bounding boxes and dated OSM/municipal snapshots. Report footprint recall and precision with documented matching tolerances, split/merged building treatment and source vintage. Measure road connectivity and access classifications separately. Calculate building height, building:levels, roof:shape and roof:height fractions against explicit eligible denominators. Label inferred geometry separately from surveyed geometry. Never imply a live traffic phase, convoy position or transit vehicle position from static source data.

[Montréal Ville-Marie snow procedure](https://montreal.ca/articles/le-deneigement-dans-ville-marie-100716), updated 2026-01-08, supports the snowblower-to-truck removal pattern. Borough trigger examples are not citywide simulation rules. Values in this pack are art proposals rather than code requirements or observed city statistics.
