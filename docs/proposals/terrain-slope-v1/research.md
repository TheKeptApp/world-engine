# Terrain + Slope — research and build rules
Checked 2026-10-07. **Verified** claims cite IDs linked in sources.md. **Assumption** means an authored engine/design target. **Unverified** means do not treat it as an input fact. All pictures are fictional concept blocks, not surveys, exact camera projections or engineered construction plans.

## Elevation and street grade
**Verified — USGS-1M, USGS-LICENSE, USGS-QUALITY, USGS-DATUM.** 3DEP bare-earth DEMs are free public-domain elevation inputs. The 2015 1 m product specification gives 1 m cells, 10 km square tiles and 6 m overlap. A QL2 acquisition targets 0.10 m RMSEz; this is a quality specification, not an accuracy guarantee for every pixel. One-metre spacing cannot resolve a 0.15 m curb reliably. Inspect each tile's actual quality, collection date, horizontal/vertical datum and hydroflattening. UTM metres are appropriate for local grade calculations; transform OSM coordinates into the same local metre frame, and never mix ellipsoidal and orthometric heights.

**Assumption / arithmetic.** A 10,000² float32 grid is approximately 400 MB before compression/overlap/metadata. Download and mesh offline or on the package service; never send full DEM tiles to the phone. Store source tile IDs, dates, license and vertical reference with derived geometry.

**Unverified — USGS-ACCESS.** Full current 1 m coverage for SF, Seattle, Pittsburgh, Kansas City, San Diego and LA hills was not established. No full-metro coverage claim is made. Required gate: query the exact proposed street/block polygon in the TNM catalog, intersect all product footprints, check gaps and dates, then inspect samples at roads, foundations and bridges. If absent, use documented coarser 3DEP only for broad hills and label grade inference low confidence; omit precise stairs/foundations until local lidar or survey arrives. Do not equate national baseline acquisition with published complete 1 m DEM coverage.

**Verified — OSM-INCLINE.** Numeric incline tags can express percent or explicit degrees; positive follows way direction. up/down supplies direction, not magnitude. **Assumption:** retain both DEM-derived and tagged grades with provenance. A credible surveyed/profile value outranks a smoothed DEM inference; conflicting values trigger review, not silent averaging.

**Assumption / math.** grade% = 100 × elevation difference / horizontal run; angle = atan(grade/100). Sample road centrelines every 2 m, robustly fit 10–20 m windows, retain real crests/intersection breakpoints. Compare segment and whole-street grades; do not average away a short steep section. Infer cross slope separately. Bridges and tunnels need deck profiles; bare earth is the wrong height. Where unresolved, flag the crossing instead of drawing a road down into the valley.

## Buildings: level floors, footprint foundations
**Assumption — core generator rule.** Preserve observed XY footprints. Keep floor planes horizontal and walls vertical. Never rotate a building to the terrain normal. Choose a floor datum using mapped entrances, garage level, terraces and building data; if absent, choose a conservative frontage-based pad with uncertainty, not the highest terrain pixel under the whole footprint.

Sample every footprint vertex, along edges at 2 m, and at entrances/garage thresholds. Each perimeter foundation closes vertically from the chosen floor/base to local ground. A negative exposure signals uphill penetration: resolve with a bounded cut/pad or a distinct split-level part, not an invisible buried door. Step foundation tops/bottoms in broad 0.3–0.6 m increments where appropriate; floor slabs within each storey remain level. Architectural split levels are separate horizontal floor datums. Avoid a single giant pedestal.

At 12 m depth along slope, height differences are 0.36 m at 3%, 1.20 m at 10%, and 2.64 m at 22%. These are exact arithmetic examples, **not typical foundation heights**. Lot orientation and existing grading matter more than band labels. Authored exposed-foundation guides are 0.15–0.6 m gentle, 0.6–1.8 m moderate, 1.8–3.6 m steep; exposure above 3 m triggers review. Do not synthesize a basement or garage solely because a slope exists.

Garage-at-grade: street-facing threshold follows a short mapped/local driveway transition while the habitable floor remains above or offset. Entries: uphill stoop, downhill stairs, side-street access and split-level landing are alternatives selected by evidence. Corner lots sample both street profiles independently; use local intersection transitions, not a folded road surface. Retaining walls and stairs follow the actual terrace boundary; limit inferred pad changes to roughly 1 m outside the footprint, without cutting across neighbours.

**Assumption.** Ordinary visual wall guide 0.6–1.8 m, review above 2 m; use stepped terraces instead of a huge uninterrupted wall. These are content heuristics, not structural limits or engineering advice. Mark wall evidence inferred if absent from data. Never invent support beneath a house extending over an unmapped canyon.

## Streets, stairs and parking
**Verified — SEATTLE-GRADE.** Seattle design criteria give maxima 9% principal/commercial, 10% collector/minor and 17% residential access/alleys; usual crown/sidewalk cross slope 2% and curb 6 inches. These are design standards, not the steepest existing street records. **Verified — LA-GRADE.** LA's manual prefers grades <=6%, with major-highway desirable 6%, absolute 7%, and other streets normally 15%. Apply the relevant street class and current project rules. A fictional 22% LA sheet illustrates the generator's steep case; it is not a conforming new-street design.

**Verified (official indexed text) — SD-STREET.** San Diego's 2024 manual uses typical 2% crown. **Unverified:** exact existing maximum street grades for all six cities, and current maximum-design tables beyond the specific Seattle/LA claims above. Claims about famously steep streets were not accepted without municipal profile evidence. KC's standards portal is a source to consult, not proof of a numeric maximum.

**Assumption.** Street curb height remains measured normal to a vertical local section, tracking road grade; the curb does not stair-step with each house. Break meshes at kerbs, retaining edges, road/sidewalk limits and stair landings. Do not smooth across a wall. Road banking/crossfall is independent of longitudinal grade. Preserve mapped parking configuration. **Verified — SF-PARK:** curb-wheel orientation depends on uphill/downhill and curb, not a universal parking-bay angle rule.

**Verified — PITTSBURGH-STEPS.** Public stair routes are part of Pittsburgh's real transport system. **Verified — ADA-STAIRS:** applicable covered flights use uniform 4–7 inch risers and >=11 inch treads; scope is limited to covered facilities. **Assumption:** visual target riser 0.16 m, tread 0.30 m, width 1.5 m, landing 1.5 m after around eight risers, handrail 0.9 m. Fit an integer step count to measured total rise and keep each flight uniform. A 28% route with landings is not a claim that individual stairs have 28% pitch. Mapped stairs override defaults. **Verified — ADA-ROUTE:** stairs alone do not establish an accessible route. Do not claim legal compliance from these dimensions.

## Renderer-neutral terrain and LOD
**Verified — CESIUM-LOD, THREE-LOD, THREE-MESH, REALITY-MESH.** Map terrain can refine with screen-space error; three.js offers LOD and indexed geometry; RealityKit supports custom or descriptor-based meshes. These APIs establish feasibility, not achieved WorldEngine performance.

**Assumption — proposed implementation.** Bake a shared indexed mesh package in local ENU, with broad material IDs, terrain borders, road profiles, wall breaklines and canonical building anchors. Use 64 m chunks and deterministic simplification. Preserve road/curb/foundation/canyon silhouette constraints; avoid uniform dense grids. Share tile-edge vertices or stitch borders. Hidden edge skirts may hide outer seams, never become visible walls through a street.

Select geometry by projected error (near target 1 drawable pixel, far 2); three.js needs application logic beyond distance LOD. Add 20% hysteresis, stable anchors independent of LOD, and crossfades/morphs only if they keep fixed road/building contacts. Never resample a house datum from the currently rendered coarse terrain. Local-origin coordinates avoid floating-point jitter. Bake pads/foundation seams consistently so floors do not float or bury at distance. Merge distant stairs into terrace silhouettes; preserve landing/rail silhouette near, omit small wall joints below 48 pixels. Do not use texture grain to hide seams.

**Verified repository target — visual-v2 §8.1.** 60 fps, <=10 ms GPU total; baseline world/ground bucket 4.25 ms, near-geometry additions 0.35 ms; total main ceiling 400k triangles /100 draws, shadow ceiling 150k. **Assumption:** start terrain at <=40k visible triangles and <=12 terrain draws, replacing flat ground within baseline; nearby foundations/stairs share the existing additions ceiling. No new budget bucket or use of reserved safety margin. These are proposed subcaps, not benchmarks. Count all buildings, foliage, weather and shadow geometry too.

Preprocess DEM, footprints and meshes off the render loop. Stream/cull chunks; cache static geometry. Broad matte surfaces use shared materials; no per-frame DEM solves or full-screen terrain effects. Keep accurate slopes when degrading: remove wall joints, distant rails and optional stairs detail before terrain silhouette. Profile sustained iPhone 13-class load in both renderers, including shadows/weather/thermal throttling. Render screenshot concepts cannot prove frame cost.

## Visual validation and remaining gates
**Assumption — Style B v2 authoring target.** Inherit the calibration's shared look exactly: true proportions, broad matte surfaces, no photographic grain, restrained glass, irregular lobed crowns; clear fixture sun elevation 40°/azimuth225°, EV+0.35, contrast1.06, saturation1.08. Live sun/weather replace fixture timing. No city-specific grade/exposure overrides. Keep buildings and retaining walls distinct through soft shadow/contact depth; avoid crushed-black foundation cavities.

Seven sheets provide STREET /45° AERIAL /FAR. Phone HTML presents the complete triptych and three enlarged panel windows; the raw wide sheet alone is too small for detailed inspection. The exact grade diagram below every sheet is quantitative; generated imagery is qualitative. No signs, logos, murals, actors or app content.

Before implementation: verify AOI DEM coverage; map true stair/entry/garage levels; decide pad evidence precedence; benchmark mesh caps; inspect contacts during LOD changes; check the steep intersection/drainage geometry; reject invented remote city landmarks. These are unresolved implementation gates, not reasons to postpone this proposal.
