# Status

**R approved – 2026-10-08.** Approved; Australia drives on the left.

**Owner lane:** P2, P1. **Phase:** Launch countries Mexico and Australia.

Style B regional design pack for two launch countries: Mexico (Mexico City, Guadalajara) and Australia (Sydney, Melbourne). Each country gets 16 responsive sheets covering building archetypes, districts, trees, seasonal lawns, street furniture, signals, transit, weather and night, plus authored, unverified modelling targets and a data-source research note.

Binding rules from the pack:
- Australia drives LEFT, Mexico RIGHT (matches road-signs-signals-v1 and R, 8 Oct 2026). Drawn traffic placement is concept only and needs engineering review before reuse as a road plan.
- style-b-calibration-v2 owns light, colour, exposure and material response (sharedLook inherited, not newly certified); this pack owns regional geometry and base albedo; weather and night replace the daytime fixture; exposure and wetness apply once.
- Building types: Mexico five (Mexico City courtyard house, Art Deco apartment, contemporary apartments; Guadalajara patio house, infill mixed-use). Australia five (Sydney sandstone terrace, Federation cottage, contemporary infill; Melbourne weatherboard house, brick walk-up).
- Regional wall, roof and turf colours are authored albedo targets. Storeys, floor heights, lot widths, setbacks, street widths and tree mixes are representative UNVERIFIED choices, not city averages or zoning; absent real heights are explicit archetype estimates.
- Seasons: Australia follows Southern Hemisphere seasons; Mexico highland follows a dry/wet pattern (autumn trees stay green, jacaranda blooms in spring only, no snow); turf colour depends on irrigation, species and recent rain.
- No logos, readable signs, murals or public art, dogs, people or app content; sign plates stay blank; landmark shapes are generic context; signals link to the road-signs-signals-v1 country pages.

Authored or unverified: (1) All numeric regional targets (storeys, floor heights, lot widths, setbacks, street right-of-way, carriageway and sidewalk widths, tree heights, crowns, mix shares) and the wall, roof and turf hex colours are authored representative choices, not measured; every archetype carries confidence 'authored UNVERIFIED target, not measured'. (2) Building data are candidates only, none downloaded: no complete open measured height layer verified for Mexico City or Guadalajara; no complete freely licensed measured footprint-plus-height source established for Sydney (the NSW height layer is a planning limit, not observed height); Melbourne 2023 footprints are municipal only; (3) Live-feed rights: SMN/CONAGUA commercial reuse unverified; BOM permission is product-specific (radar and specialised products may need agreements); no verified current Guadalajara GTFS or live feed; CDMX GTFS edition currency unclear; TfNSW and Victoria feeds need keys and currency checks. No live weather or transit data appears in the images. (4) Block paint-overs come from generated neutral source images with approximately retained geometry and cameras; no surveyed block or photograph. All images are visual studies, not meshes, measured-scale assets or navigational maps; generated pixels do not certify scale.
