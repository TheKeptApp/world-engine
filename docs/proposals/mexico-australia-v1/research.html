Mexico + Australia / Primary-source review

Launch-country data research

Reviewed 7 October 2026. “Verified” means the cited publisher states the property. It does not mean we downloaded, audited or implemented that dataset. City coverage, measured completeness and live-feed uptime are unverified unless explicitly stated. These generated boards do not use downloaded map or weather data.

Mexico

Footprints — verified candidate, city accuracy unverified. Microsoft's global ML footprints release includes Mexico; data is CDLA Permissive 2.0. Imagery dates vary and detections can miss courtyards, join adjacent roofs or include false positives. Use country tiles, confidence and source dates, then inspect each launch district. Building height availability must be checked per tile; do not interpret footprint availability as complete height coverage. Microsoft release and licence ↗.

INEGI — verified open-information terms; not a national building-height guarantee. Its free-use terms allow commercial inputs, adaptations and redistribution with attribution, preserved metadata and disclosure of transformations; avoid endorsement claims. Census blocks and DENUE establishment points are not individual building footprints. No complete open measured height layer for Mexico City or Guadalajara was verified here. INEGI terms ↗.

Elevation — verified national candidate. INEGI CEM offers terrain elevation; the relief catalogue describes 15 m continuous products and separate surface products. Local high-resolution terrain/surface/LiDAR tiles are discoverable through its download selector; exact launch-city resolution, age, vertical datum and gaps remain unverified. Fifteen-metre terrain is unsuitable for precise curb/stoop geometry; a DSM is not bare terrain or a reliable roof-height layer without a matching DTM. Relief catalogue ↗, CEM and local tile selector ↗.

SMN/CONAGUA weather — access verified, commercial reuse UNVERIFIED. SMN publishes historical station information and climate products. No clear product-level commercial redistribution licence for the intended forecast/observation/radar feed was established in this review. INEGI's terms do not grant rights to SMN. Public access or a government footer does not establish rights to cache and resell a live feed. Record the selected product, endpoint, update interval, retention and permitted derived display; obtain applicable terms before production use. The weather scene in this pack is authored, not SMN-fed. SMN station information ↗, SMN portal ↗.

Transit — Mexico City verified candidate. SEMOVI's static GTFS page declares CC BY 4.0 and lists multiple modes. Catalogue updated 24 February 2026 but description still references a 2022 edition; inspect downloaded feed calendar/date ranges before treating it as current. Static GTFS is schedules and geometry, not real-time vehicle positions. Guadalajara: official IMEPLAN planning material exists; a current downloadable official GTFS with explicit reuse terms and a live feed was not verified here. Do not substitute an unofficial feed without checking rights and currency. CDMX GTFS ↗, IMEPLAN metropolitan planning portal ↗.

Australia

Footprints — verified open candidates, spatial scope varies. Microsoft's global release includes Australia under CDLA Permissive 2.0. Melbourne's 2023 Building Footprints dataset is a municipal source, not all metropolitan Melbourne: the catalogue states May 2023 capture, vertically stacked polygons for changing built form, and CC BY with a 4.0 licence link. Check its height fields, capture date, units, coverage and licence record before ingesting. Sydney: its municipal open-data hub publishes data under Creative Commons unless otherwise stated, but no complete freely licensed measured citywide footprint-plus-height source was established in this review. Sydney open-data access ↗. NSW planning Height of Buildings is a regulatory limit layer, not observed building height. Microsoft ↗, Melbourne 2023 footprints ↗, NSW planning height layer ↗.

Commercial adjacent source. Geoscape National Buildings documents outlines and elevation-related attributes. Its dataset/API requires applicable licence terms; do not assume national coverage makes it open/free. Pricing and redistribution rights UNVERIFIED. Geoscape building documentation ↗.

Elevation — verified discovery route. Geoscience Australia's ELVIS aggregates Commonwealth/state/territory open elevation and depth products. Local LiDAR/DEM resolution, date, AHD/geoid transformation and dataset licence must be checked per tile. Urban roof/ground height estimation needs compatible DSM/DTM and footprint alignment; trees and pitched roofs complicate it. GA elevation and ELVIS ↗.

BOM reuse — verified restriction, product-specific. The Bureau says content has varied licences. Material explicitly marked CC BY can be reused under those terms with attribution; other products may need a data licence. Where no terms are stated, use is limited to personal/internal use, without supply to others or commercial use. Radar/high-resolution/specialised products can require separate agreements. Select and verify each observation/forecast/radar product; do not scrape the public site as if it grants blanket commercial rights. No live BOM data is embedded here. BOM copyright ↗, BOM data catalogue ↗.

Transit — verified candidates. TfNSW Open Data Hub defaults to CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise stated, with agency attribution and portal/acceptable-use terms. GTFS/GTFS-R endpoints may require registration and API keys. Victoria's GTFS Schedule catalogue states CC BY 4.0, weekly/as-needed updates, and a rolling 30-day horizon; it warns route paths can differ from operations. Victoria's APIs moved to the current Open Data Portal with new account/key arrangements. Feed currency, service calendars, quotas and launch-city modes still need checking at integration. TfNSW licence ↗, TfNSW specification ↗, Victoria GTFS and licence ↗, Victoria API migration ↗.

OSM completeness — all four cities UNVERIFIED numerically

OSM is useful for street topology, POIs, land use and contributed buildings, but this review did not run a current extract or a reference-based completeness audit. No defensible percentage is claimed for Mexico City, Guadalajara, Sydney or Melbourne. Roads, footprints, height/building:levels, sidewalks, crossings and transit relations require separate assessments; a busy-looking map does not prove completeness. Measure an AOI against independently licensed reference geometry, report missing/extra footprints and height-tag coverage, and inspect district variation. Licence is ODbL with attribution and applicable derivative-database obligations; a tile service is a separate permission/capacity question. OSM copyright and licensing ↗.

Rendering recommendation

Use licensed footprints plus compatible terrain, preserve provenance/date/confidence, and treat absent heights as explicit archetype estimates. Keep independently licensed source layers distinct during ingestion. Review heights, entrance/curb relationships, transit placement and driving side before publication. Australia is LEFT-hand; Mexico RIGHT-hand. Signals link to the existing regional road-signs-signals-v1 pack. Painted concept boards remain representative regional studies, not surveyed parcels, navigational maps or safety-certified traffic layouts.
