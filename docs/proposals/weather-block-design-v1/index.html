Home block

Interaction and acceptance

Open to one local house; street/aerial toggle; four-hour rail; nearby station observation is secondary and explicitly different from the area forecast.

Every visible numeric weather value has its own source badge. Station details must include observation time and station identity in the expanded Sources view.

Hour scrub

Interaction and acceptance

Drag or play; the selected valid time is pinned while the same world geometry stays fixed; stop at the available forecast horizon.

Interpolation is visual only. Readouts snap to actual valid-hour records. No interpolation presented as an observation; missing intervals are grey/unavailable.

NWS warning example

Interaction and acceptance

Warning sheet takes priority over the world and playback; official issuer, validity, original text and official source link.

This screen is a SIMULATED warning example. Production warning is FORECAST · official warning, not proof the hazardous event is observed at the home.

Rain forecast

Interaction and acceptance

Grey light, wet broad pavement planes and restrained streaks; area precipitation rate remains readable.

Wetness is simulated from history. Precipitation probability differs from rate; no deterministic raindrop arrival at the house.

Snow forecast

Interaction and acceptance

Cold light, bare trees, exposed pavement and uneven snow appearance; distinguish forecast snowfall from estimated ground cover.

Missing accumulation history yields unknown ground coverage. Demo art is illustrative; production should neutralize unsupported coverage rather than initialize a blanket.

Fog forecast

Interaction and acceptance

Distance contrast fades while near house and controls stay sharp.

Area visibility forecast is not a measured sight distance on the user’s street; do not obscure the text with world fog.

Night and simulated lights

Interaction and acceptance

Quiet blue sky, restrained warm lamps/windows, coherent light pools and simulated shadows.

Lights do not reveal residents’ activity or smart-home telemetry. Reduce city star density; solar/moon models, geometry and weather govern production light.

Settings and stale preview

Interaction and acceptance

Persistent local-home policy; units, motion, notification preferences, deletion and source links; stale example is prominent.

Mandatory local home storage is not a toggle. Warning permissions are optional; declining does not hide in-app warnings. Refresh never makes stale data live without a new valid fetch.

Name and icon exploration

Recommendation: Blocklight. Name availability is not checked; boards are concept directions.

Blocklight

A pale buff miniature roof and one warm sun arc, with a broad teal ground shadow; focus on daylight moving across your own block.

Nearcast

Three simple stepped teal house/roof masses under one soft slate cloud; one bold rain drop, generous negative space; local weather made tangible.

Porchcast

One simple porch roof silhouette frames a warm horizon and a single blue raindrop; approachable everyday planning, no people.
