# Greenville SC · Style B v1

Open index.html for the offline responsive gallery. Six house/storefront archetypes, four district boards, a generated block source and paint-over, rolling Piedmont terrain, five regional tree families, a summer thunderstorm, a night triptych and four seasonal lawn comparisons are included. Each principal sheet has street-level, intended 45° aerial and far-context views. Seasonal lawns use four repeated street views for direct comparison.

## Look authority

style-b-calibration-v2 owns lighting, exposure, colour response and materials. Its sharedLook block is copied exactly into values.json, with source hash. Regional base wall/roof/lawn albedos and geometry belong to Greenville. Weather and night replace the clear-day fixture. Apply exposure and material wetness once. The calibration file’s proposal/approval status is retained in provenance; this pack does not assert a new approval.

Rich Style B means real proportions, selective porch/roof/trim geometry, simplified matte surfaces, coherent soft light and organic tree silhouettes. Generic landmark shells only. No logos, readable signs, real murals/public art, dogs, animals, people or app/host content in these studies.

## Coverage

Archetypes: mill-village cottage, 1920s bungalow, brick ranch, Craftsman, newer infill townhouse, downtown mixed-use storefront.

Districts: Main Street and river-park/pedestrian-bridge area, North Main, Augusta Road and a textile mill-village area. These are representative district concepts rather than literal parcel or road reproductions. The river bridge is a generic shell and all public art is omitted.

Trees: oaks, Southern magnolia, crape myrtle, flowering dogwood and loblolly pine. The tree sheet presents silhouette families, not botanical identification or a measured canopy survey. Seasonal lawn reference uses warm-season turf; an alternate cool-season palette is provided because Greenville lawns do not universally brown in winter.

Block paint-over: an authored neutral block concept is edited into the shared calibrated look, keeping geometry and cameras approximately. No user-supplied photograph, cadastral model or engine camera was available. The source and edited images remain separately saved.

## Values

All storeys, floor heights, lot widths, setbacks, street widths, grade ranges and tree mix percentages are **authored, unverified modeling targets**. They are not zoning rules, city averages, surveyed lots or inventory estimates. Setbacks are measured from a hypothetical parcel boundary, not the curb. Street right-of-way includes planting/verge and other residual width beyond the listed carriageway and sidewalks. Tree mix sums to one by specimen count; choose suitable sites and crown spacing before spawning.

Base palettes are albedo targets, not sampled displayed pixels. Weather values are illustrative, not connected forecasts. Raster images do not certify metric scale, exact species, shadow bearings or precise 45° camera elevation. No meshes, DEM, engine implementation or performance benchmark are included.
