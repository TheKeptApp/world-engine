## Counts and placement

values.json contains 39 activity/asset records with metres, speeds, local 6am/noon/6pm/11pm counts, seasonal and game-day multipliers, locations and activation gates. All dimensions, speeds and counts are authored starting targets, not surveys, observed current footfall or guaranteed engine density. People counts are expected simultaneous presence, not throughput; fractional counts mean stochastic expectations. One eligible face is 100m on ONE side. Site-specific stations, garages and moorings do not scale with arbitrary frontage length.

Mapped geometry and schedules win. Food fronts, vendors and taverns require eligible sites rather than every block. Fixed garages/bleachers and installed equipment retain site identity. Alleys may show blue/black carts and rear garages; poles are MAPPED ONLY in engine. Illustrative poles are not evidence of actual placement. Garbage trucks require collection schedule and clearance.

Transit stays on mapped routes and tracks. Elevated platforms/trains need station/track masks; the water taxi is Chicago River city-wide context, not Lakeview lake transport. Denver light rail is a city-wide dedicated corridor, not a Sloan path asset. Actual service changes override the concept. Scooters stay in permitted lane areas, not the lake path. Boats require a mapped channel/dock or harbour.

Festival tents, opened hydrants, farmers markets, chairs in shoveled spaces, plows and salt trucks default OFF until a verified schedule/event/history or labelled demo enables them. Heat alone never opens a hydrant. Chairs are an authored cultural cue, not an official parking reservation. Snow piles require clearing history and preserve crossings/ramps. Season multipliers are allowed-weather eligibility, not proof an event is active.

Game-day crowds require actual start/end and venue routes. Event people replace that site's baseline before multiplying; do not add a second crowd. Food queues, transit crowds, patio patrons and walkers share one actor pool. Do not sum all table rows onto one block. Maintain mapped access and at least the authored 1.8m pedestrian corridor target. This is not a statement of local code.
