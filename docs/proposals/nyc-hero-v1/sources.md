# NYC typology references

Accessed 2026-10-07. Primary references support the type distinctions below. The pack’s numeric presets, palettes, district mixtures and light angles are **authored proposals**, not values extracted from these sources unless explicitly identified.

- [NYC DOT Street Design Manual](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/pedestrians/streetdesignmanual.shtml): reference framework for street, sidewalk and bicycle infrastructure. This pack does not claim that its illustrative allocations are approved street designs.
- [CUNY Baruch — Manhattan’s grid](https://www.baruch.cuny.edu/nycdata/infrastructure/the_grid.html): historical grid distinguishes 100-foot avenues and standard 60-foot cross streets. JSON converts these to 30.48 m and 18.288 m; local conditions vary. Carriageway/sidewalk subdivisions are authored here.
- [NYC LPC Prospect Heights designation report](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/2314.pdf): Brooklyn rowhouse details including stoops, cornices and ironwork. Used as neighboring typology evidence, not a surveyed Park Slope inventory.
- [NYC LPC rowhouse study](https://a860-gpp.nyc.gov/downloads/sb3979399?locale=en): traditional narrow lot and rowhouse dimensions support the frontage/depth vocabulary. Individual presets remain representative proposals.
- [NYC LPC Grand Concourse Historic District](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/lpc/lp/2403.pdf): Bronx apartment and Art Deco vocabulary, including brick/stone banding and corner windows. No exact facade is copied.
- [NYC LPC SoHo–Cast Iron Historic District map](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/lpc/downloads/pdf/maps/HistoricDistrictMaps/Manhattan/soho.pdf): official district context; does not independently prove facade parameters.
- [MTA Astoria station neighborhood map](https://www.mta.info/document/82151): Astoria transit geography. Elevated infrastructure is confined to the outer-borough study and Astoria district composition; no MTA map artwork or logos are used.
- [NYC DOT Brooklyn Bridge](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/infrastructure/brooklyn-bridge.shtml): stone towers, suspension system and promenade distinguish the stone suspension family. Pack dimensions are generic and differ from the real bridge.
- [NYC DOT Manhattan Bridge](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/infrastructure/manhattan-bridge.shtml): steel-tower suspension and multi-level transit/road organization distinguish the second family. Generic dimensions are not a landmark specification.
- [NYC DOT Queensboro Bridge](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/infrastructure/queensboro-bridge.shtml): cantilever truss structure and island crossing distinguish the third family. It must not acquire suspension main cables.
- [American Museum of Natural History — Manhattanhenge](https://www.amnh.org/research/hayden-planetarium/manhattanhenge): cross-street sunset alignment. The pack uses a representative west-facing bearing, not a date-specific solar calculation or a guarantee that any particular cross street aligns.
- [NYC Parks Tree Planting Standards](https://www.nycgovparks.org/pagefiles/53/Tree-Planting-Standards.pdf): relevant planting reference; full PDF retrieval was unavailable during this run. **Unverified:** tree pit/spacing values in this pack are authoring choices, not claims of current planting compliance.

## Limits

No exact real-site reconstruction, address identification, statistical district mix, metric raster geometry, code compliance, bridge engineering, solar ephemeris verification or mobile rendering benchmark is claimed. Those are not deliverables of this visual hero pack.
