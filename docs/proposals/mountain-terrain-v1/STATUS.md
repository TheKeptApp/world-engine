# Status

**R approved – 2026-10-08.** Approved (R's 8 Oct approvals list names mountains): v1 plus the r2 fixes (Sloan's Lake far-view correction, ski area re-render) and the hiking trails.

**Owner lane:** P1, 5A, P0. **Phase:** Engine capability: terrain, slope and mountains.

Style B concept sheets and a values JSON for mountain terrain, Colorado Front Range first: Sloan's Lake seasons with west and east views, snow driven by observation history, true far-mountain silhouettes with haze, DEM detail levels, foothill roads and buildings, ski area, treeline, lake, and seven hiking-trail types.

Binding rules from the pack:
- Look and slope are inherited unchanged: sharedLook from style-b-calibration-v2, slopeRules from terrain-slope-v1. Floors stay level, buildings never tilt, roads use cut and fill with retaining geometry, bridges are never draped on bare earth, and mapped data overrides every authored number.
- Snow comes only from valid observed or analysed data (SNODAS or NOHRSC, land-manager report, satellite mask) with a timestamp, never from month, elevation or cold air. Soft age 36 h, hard age 72 h, then the last mask is kept as stale, never as current. Snowline is reported by aspect. Ski grooming needs operator data.
- Far mountains are DEM-derived true silhouettes on a curved Earth: no baked or painted peaks, depth-bearing horizon impostors from about 50 km rebuilt as the observer moves, haze applied once in linear light (T = exp(-beta*d)), no surface texture, same field of view at 20, 50 and 100 km (5.711, 2.291, 1.146 degrees for 2,000 m relief).
- Trails keep true width: never widened for phone visibility, cull below 1 px. Centreline from the map, terrain from the DEM, bridge decks separate from bare earth. Roots, drainage bars, cairns, steps and bridges appear only where mapped or observed. Width, visibility and difficulty stay separate; unknown tags stay null. AllTrails is excluded as data.
- Colorado numbers (elevation bands, RMNP treeline 3,352.8-3,505.2 m, palettes, trail widths) are Front Range authoring guides, not global rules; a general mountain kit swaps geology, vegetation bands, snow sources and treeline. Rock, scree and snow are masks, not altitude stripes. The race overlay is a private versioned layer apart from the verified base map.

Authored or unverified: (1) Every number is an authored target (values status: 'Authoring targets, not engine-validated'). Only product and method facts are verified: USGS 3DEP and GMTED descriptions, SNODAS daily 1 km, NPS RMNP treeline and zones, clipmap method. (2) Local data: exact 3DEP 1 m tiles, dates and quality, local road profiles, peak positions and the Sloan's Lake viewshed; NOHRSC endpoint and latency (retrieval failed); SNODAS has Basic service, no SLA; USFS trail layer snapshot (XML metadata failed); NPS live trail service (retrieval failed); COTREX reuse licence. (3) Colorado OSM trail completeness is null (no extract audit was run); local trail widths, tag coverage and access are unknown, and missing OSM tags stay null. (4) Images are concept art: not georegistered, not camera-calibrated, not current conditions. Seasons and trail states (mud, supported snow, golden hour) are independent illustrative scenarios; no live snow, rain or soil data was fetched. The v1 note says artwork carries small foliage and rock detail the JSON tiers forbid;
