New York block mix

Attached brownstone and brick rowhouse majority, one five-floor tenement at far corner, modest urban midrise in far distance; Queens Tudor excluded from this dense rowhouse block. Replace front lawns with paved stoops/areaways and tree pits, retain same road corridor and horizon; no driveways. Broad deciduous crowns and granite/concrete curb; no palms or invented wires.

No New York repo render found in inspected fixture/capture lists. Closest current Lakeview postcard camera used; all target-metro scene content proposed, not a captured real block.

Street character · proposal

Ground / yards
Paved core frontages; lawns/plant beds on detached lots, proposed—not measured shares
Trees / palms
Broad deciduous elm/plane/oak-like crowns, tree pits in paved frontages; no palms. Geometry illustrative, not species inventory.
Sidewalk
Standard grey concrete; historic bluestone/granite alternatives [https://www.nyc.gov/site/designcommission/design-review/design-guidelines/distinctive-sidewalks.page]
Curb
Granite, steel-faced and concrete alternatives [https://www.nyc.gov/site/designcommission/design-review/design-guidelines/distinctive-sidewalks.page]; relative prevalence unclear
Overhead wires
Proposal: render only mapped/verified utility spans; optional inferred art mode clearly labelled, off by default. Do not infer pole locations from house style.
Alley access
Use highway=service+service=alley geometry only; regional alley coverage unclear
