Los Angeles block mix

Low Spanish Revival stucco houses and California Craftsman bungalows with one ranch further down, occasional dingbat near far corner only. Varied small lawns and drought beds, occasional fan palm among broad shade trees; side driveways conditional and no repeated palm rows.

No Los Angeles repo render found in inspected fixture/capture lists. Closest current Denver street camera used; all target-metro scene content proposed, not a captured real block.

Street character · proposal

Ground / yards
proposal: varied lawn/drought planting/mulch/gravel by land-cover evidence; avoid uniform regional stereotypes
Trees / palms
Broad evergreen shade crowns and occasional tall fan palm; do not use palms as every street tree.
Sidewalk
proposal: concrete slabs; instantiate interruptions/curbless exceptions from geometry
Curb
proposal: concrete curb/gutter where mapped
Overhead wires
Proposal: render only mapped/verified utility spans; optional inferred art mode clearly labelled, off by default. Do not infer pole locations from house style.
Alley access
proposal: mapped alleys only; never uniform by metro
