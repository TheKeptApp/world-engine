6–20 pxAdd porch/stoop shadow, bay/entry projection and carport/areaway voids.
>20 pxAdd supported dormers, doors/windows, cornices and broad trim. No subpixel ornament.

Access. Front/side carport apron conditional on mapped access; keep entry walk separate.

Yard. Gravel and desert-adapted beds, palo-verde-like airy crown, small irrigated lawn pocket optional; no obligatory cactus.

Broad low one-storey Sonoran suburb ranch, pale sand stucco with small masonry accent, low gable wide eaves, shaded entry, deep open carport at one side, no tile roof required.

All dimensions, roof choices, mix weights, palette combinations and access/yard arrangements are proposals. Inherited surfaces are unchanged approved contrast witnesses; colourVariations supply this archetype wall/trim/roof targets. Actual mapped geometry/tags override. No legal access/survey claim.
