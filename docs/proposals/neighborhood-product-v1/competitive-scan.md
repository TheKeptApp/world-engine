# Competitive scan

Desk review, accessed 7 October 2026. “Works” and “fails” below are design interpretations unless specifically documented. No measured retention, business success or current market-share claims are made.

| Product | Documented mechanic | What to borrow (interpretation) | Failure / tension to avoid (interpretation unless stated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pokémon GO | Routes connect walking and exploration; Party Play supports group play. [Routes](https://pokemongo.com/en/routes), [Party Play](https://pokemongo.com/partyplay) | Small destination-driven loops and shared tasks make ordinary movement purposeful. | IP and collection depth are hard to replicate. Location rewards can pressure travel or repeated phone checking. WorldEngine should support browse equivalents and have no AR requirement. |
| Strava | Layered privacy controls cover activity, map and group visibility. Its FAQ says map visibility restrictions do not transfer to third-party services receiving authorized data. [Privacy FAQ](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401776-strava-s-privacy-controls-faq) | A completed effort is a satisfying social object; clear personal progress can motivate. | Precision routes and repeated patterns can reveal routines. Endpoint hiding is weaker than never uploading a route; integrations can undermine familiar privacy expectations. Do not import activity tracks. |
| Nextdoor | Local communities can be public or private; the policy hub describes community governance and transparency. [Communities](https://blog.nextdoor.com/2024/07/22/new-communities-feature-opens-lines-of-communication-between-neighbors), [Policy hub](https://about.nextdoor.com/policy/) | Local relevance and practical coordination solve concrete needs. | A broad local discussion feed creates moderation and interpersonal conflict burdens. WorldEngine should use bounded plans and preset responses, not residential identity or address verification. This is a product risk, not a quantified claim about Nextdoor users. |
| Geocaching | Community caches require permission, suitability and maintenance; Premium supports features and ongoing operations. [Hiding guidelines](https://www.geocaching.com/play/guidelines), [Premium](https://newsroom.geocaching.com/premium) | A small discoverable goal and a lasting collection can make exploration memorable. | Permission and stale physical objects are operational obligations. Use virtual prompts at vetted public places; no hidden containers or home-based clues. |
| Zenly-like social maps | Zenly was a social mapping app and closed on 3 February 2023. [Official closure notice](https://zenlyapp.zendesk.com/hc/fr/articles/11084230178577-est-ce-que-c-est-vraiment-la-fin-pour-zenly) | A warm, legible map can feel social without a conventional feed. | Friend whereabouts become the attraction and an expectation. Closure alone does not prove a privacy or product failure. WorldEngine should reproduce warmth through shared objects, never live people dots, visit history or home inference. |
| Snap Map | Location sharing has audience controls and Ghost Mode; live-location sharing is a distinct setting. [Snap privacy: Snap Map](https://values.snap.com/privacy/privacy-by-product/snap-map) | Spatial browsing can make places easy to understand. | Privacy exceptions and multiple sharing modes can make a user's mental model fragile. Avoid any location-sharing mode, inferred presence or public user content tied to map coordinates. |

## Implication

Keep real-world context and cooperation; discard live presence, precision activity histories and residential identities. The differentiator to test is a recognizable 3D neighbourhood whose light and weather change the activity or plan. Neither attractive maps nor cooperative loops establish product demand on their own.

## What remains unverified

Revenue potential, competitor user satisfaction, causal reasons for retention/churn, current regional availability and paid-tier pricing were not evaluated. No competitor assets, game IP, map screenshots or user data were copied. The scan does not authorize ingesting third-party data; any later integration needs a separate data-rights review.
