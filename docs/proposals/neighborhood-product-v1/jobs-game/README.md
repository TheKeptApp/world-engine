# Neighborhood Jobs Game v1

**Lead concept: a cozy jobs game on real neighbourhood geography.** Common Ground and Gather remain secondary. Internal only; 7 October 2026.

Open `index.html`: 14 phone screens and document links. `concept.md` defines the loop, progression and cosmetic-only monetization. `job-catalogue.md` covers 13 jobs, triggers, seasons and uncertainty. `privacy.md` defines the data boundary. `prototype-90-days.md` starts with Snow Plow and Paper Route in Denver/Chicago. `values.json` inherits calibration-v2 and records authored gameplay rules. `prompts.json` records the built-in imagegen prompts. `phone-check/` contains PNG exports and verification.

## Read the mockups honestly

Three new world images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and imagegen skill, referencing style-b-calibration-v2. They are representative Denver/Chicago concept scenes, not surveyed exact streets, engine footage or working physics. The route diagram is schematic, not a georeferenced street network. Gameplay buttons change local demo coverage, paper completion, earnings and selected cosmetics; no live environmental feed, resident data, multiplayer service or purchase exists. Snow, dawn, leaf and season states are synthetic or illustrative snapshots; jobs marked “example live” demonstrate future behavior rather than assert today's weather.

Plow scene shows a cleared strip; mowing shows stripes; dawn scene shows a blank paper mid-flight. Generative geometry and lighting are visually reviewed, not measured calibration equality. Actual route/target geometry, solar time and persistent masks must come from the engine implementation. No brands, pets, dogs, host-app content or real customers.

## Privacy

This is in-game work, not real-world chores or tracked physical movement. Play from anywhere. No GPS, people map, heatmap, resident name/occupancy or home pin. All prototype saves are local; use Reset demo to remove them. Home is not collected. Co-op totals are synthetic and delayed in the proposed design; aggregation must pass privacy review before real data is used.

## Verification and destination

Phone export/checks cover 320, 390 and 430 CSS px; scripted tests cover navigation, clearing persistence, paper completion, earnings idempotency, cosmetic purchase behavior, sparse co-op and missing-data display. See verification.json for actual results. These are browser mockup checks, not real-device game performance, engine physics or privacy certification.
