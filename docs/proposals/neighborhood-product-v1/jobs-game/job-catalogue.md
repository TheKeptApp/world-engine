# Job catalogue: environmental rules

These are game rules proposed for Denver/Chicago, not instructions to work at actual properties. Houses are generic customers. All actions happen inside WorldEngine. Month ranges below are broad editorial context; live eligibility uses conditions and data confidence. No pets or pet-sitting job.

## Trigger contract and sources

Each eligibility record stores `districtId`, `jobType`, `eventId`, `validFrom`, `validTo`, input product/time/units, spatial resolution, observation-vs-analysis-vs-model class, uncertainty, rule version and fallback reason. Never silently call a gridded analysis a street observation. The prototype supplies synthetic fixtures and archived observed events; no live integration is present in these mockups.

For snow, distinguish **new snowfall**, **snow depth**, and **snow water equivalent**. Depth differences alone cannot isolate new snowfall from melt, compaction or revisions. Proposed adapter: station snowfall reports and [NOAA's recent snowfall analysis](https://www.nohrsc.noaa.gov/snowfall_v2/) identify an eligible event; [SNODAS](https://www.nohrsc.noaa.gov/archived_data/) provides modeled/assimilated snowpack context. NOAA describes the operational snow model at roughly kilometre resolution, not individual streets. [NOHRSC technology](https://www.nohrsc.noaa.gov/technology/)

[NWS API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api) provides forecasts, alerts and observations; select an appropriate precipitation product rather than assuming every station reports snowfall or rain totals reliably. Product cadence, coverage, quality flags, availability and commercial redistribution terms must be confirmed in implementation. No NOAA feed is wired into this concept.

`foliage-seasons-v1` supplies phenology priority and stable canopy state, not verified live per-tree leaf drop. Observations lead, validated species/site models follow, inferred weather heuristics are labeled. Regional dates are context. [USA-NPN data quality](https://www.usanpn.org/data/quality) documents uncertainty in its products; it is not evidence for a particular local tree today.

## Rule tests before launch

Forecast-only snow never enables plow. Snow-depth-only rise is insufficient new-snow evidence. Duplicate event/feed revision never duplicates accumulation or payout. Restart retains cleared masks. New event overlays only fresh snowfall. Dormant turf never grows just because rain occurs. Unknown phenology does not create observed leaf jobs. Dawn respects Denver/Chicago timezone and DST. Every live job has a visible freshness label; stale inputs create labeled replay/paused state. No paid item changes eligibility.
