# Source register

Checked 2026-10-07. References are scoped: UK guidance, US ADA/OSHA, model-code excerpts, and local US examples. Current applicability at a selected event site is unverified. No blocked-site bypass. No supplier product/wind/ballast value was invented.

## R01 — HSE venue and site design

[HSE venue and site design](https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/venue-site-design.htm)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: UK event guidance; not US code.

Capacity depends on layout, audience, exits and activities; ground/anchoring/underground services and wind exposure require assessment.

## R02 — HSE crowd controls inside venue

[HSE crowd controls inside venue](https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/crowd-management-control-inside.htm)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: UK event guidance.

Keep attractions/queues out of circulation routes and avoid crowd pinch points and stage surges.

## R03 — 2024 IBC Chapter 10 table 1004.5

[2024 IBC Chapter 10 table 1004.5](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IBC2024V2.0/chapter-10-means-of-egress)

Status: verified official indexed excerpt; subsequent direct fetch failed. Scope: Model-code reference only; local adoption/amendments unverified.

5 net ft² standing; 7 net chairs only; 15 net tables/chairs. These calculate design occupant load, not a safe crowd-density standard.

## R04 — Seattle Fire tent assembly permit conditions

[Seattle Fire tent assembly permit conditions](https://www.seattle.gov/documents/departments/fire/business/permitcond_3201.pdf)

Status: verified official indexed table; direct reader internal error. Scope: Seattle example permit conditions; local applicability unverified.

Tent exit count/width table and 100 ft travel-distance reference. Not a generic site exit certificate.

## R05 — 2024 NC Fire Code Chapter 31

[2024 NC Fire Code Chapter 31](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/NCFC2024V1.0/chapter-31-tents-temporary-special-event-structures-and-other-membrane-structures)

Status: verified official indexed excerpt. Scope: North Carolina code edition example; local applicability unverified.

Tent aggregate width reference 0.2 inch per occupant and distributed exits within 100 ft travel.

## R06 — US Access Board ADA Chapter 4

[US Access Board ADA Chapter 4](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch04/)

Status: verified standard. Scope: US ADA technical standard; application/scoping requires review.

Accessible route 36 in minimum; walking slope <=1:20 and cross slope <=1:48; ramp rules separate.

## R07 — US Access Board ADA Chapter 2

[US Access Board ADA Chapter 2](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch02/)

Status: verified standard. Scope: US ADA scoping; fixed seating and portable-toilet clauses have distinct scope.

Portable clustered toilet exception uses 5%; fixed-seat wheelchair table and assistive-listening requirements are not interchangeable with loose-chair planning.

## R08 — San Diego special event planning guide

[San Diego special event planning guide](https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/legacy/specialevents/pdf/planningguide.pdf)

Status: verified municipal guide; PDF pp23,28,44 as printed. Scope: San Diego local example; other local rules unverified.

10% accessible restroom/sink facilities, at least one per location; public handwash additional to food-provider sinks; generators >=20 ft from tents and guarded from public.

## R09 — CDC generator safety

[CDC generator safety](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/psa-toolkit/use-a-generator-safely.html)

Status: verified guidance; page reviewed August 2026. Scope: CO guidance; not electrical sizing.

Keep combustion generators outside at least 20 ft from doors/windows/vents.

## R10 — HSE electrical safety

[HSE electrical safety](https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/electrical-safety.htm)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: UK technical guidance; US electrical rules separately required.

Competent electrical design; segregated generator intake/enclosure; wet-environment suitability and cable protection.

## R11 — OSHA occupational noise

[OSHA occupational noise](https://www.osha.gov/noise)

Status: verified standard summary. Scope: US occupational exposure; not city noise permit or audience loudness target.

90 dBA eight-hour PEL, 5 dBA exchange; general-industry hearing conservation at85 dBA eight-hour TWA.

## R12 — NWS lightning sports

[NWS lightning sports](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-sports)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: Outdoor activity weather guidance.

Named weather monitor, stop/shelter/resume plan; wait at least30 minutes after last thunder.

## R13 — NWS lightning outdoors

[NWS lightning outdoors](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-outdoors)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: Outdoor lightning safety.

Tents and picnic shelters are not safe lightning refuges; substantial buildings or enclosed hard-topped vehicles are options.

## R14 — NWS Event Ready Guide

[NWS Event Ready Guide](https://www.weather.gov/media/crh/eventready/Event_Ready_Guide.pdf)

Status: verified official guide excerpt. Scope: Event preparedness.

Determine warning, clearance and sheltering time; obtain facility wind thresholds; shelter suitability/capacity requires specialist review.

## R15 — NWS WBGT vs Heat Index

[NWS WBGT vs Heat Index](https://www.weather.gov/ict/WBGT)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: Heat-stress indicator; no single universal shutdown threshold.

WBGT accounts for heat, humidity, wind, cloud and sun; activity/acclimatization influence response.

## R16 — HSE temporary structures

[HSE temporary structures](https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/temporary-demountable-structures.htm)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: Temporary demountable structure procurement/design guidance.

Supplier/competent structural design and installed configuration determine load/anchoring/wind limits.

## R17 — HSE crowd management controls

[HSE crowd management controls](https://www.hse.gov.uk/event-safety/crowd-management-controls.htm)

Status: verified guidance. Scope: UK event guidance.

Arrival, onsite circulation and departure are separate crowd-management phases.
