Builder planning proposal: five event templates (outdoor wedding, concert/festival, game-day activation, experiential activation, corporate/community), each with rules for 100, 500, 2,000 and 10,000 guests, one shared rules file with 17 sources, 26 added parts, generated two-view art and read-only client pages.

Binding rules from the pack:
- Starting plans only, never approvals: no all-clear, safe or certified label from geometry; every review needs a person, scope, date, code edition and expiry. Code design occupant load stays null and separate from guest count. Per-segment numbers are assumptions unless tied to a scoped verified reference.
- Proposal defaults: 1 person per m2 target, review at 1.5, halt admission at 2; staff = 10% of guests; toilets = max(2, ceil(N/75)), 10% accessible, one per bank; gate width = max(3 m, N x 0.00508/(K-1)) rounded up to 0.25 m. At 2,000 and 10,000 guests use separately serviced districts, never one bigger tent.
- Accessible route target 1.8 m clear and a 3 m main spine (ADA minimum 0.9144 m, slope 1:20, cross slope 1:48). Queues, ropes and ballast stay out of routes and exits; no ticket checks on exits. Lightning refuge is a substantial building or hard-topped vehicle, never a tent; wait 30 minutes after the last thunder.
- Weather labels say observed, forecast or simulated with issue and valid time; stale data is never labelled live; beyond the forecast horizon use named scenarios. Wind, heat, noise and refuge triggers stay unset until an accountable planner enters them; no generic ballast or wind numbers.
- Look: calibration-v2 sharedLook is inherited unchanged; its 40 degree, 225 degree sun is an art fixture, and event sun comes from the engine solar model. No logos, readable text or copied brand identity (generic material colours only); no dogs or animals. Client pages are read-only version-1 snapshots; guest cases live in JSON, not new art.
- Boundary: proposal data and illustrative art only, with no engine implementation, upload registration, interactive 3D, forecast link, approval or comment backend. The art is not a render of the rule coordinates, guest counts or bill of materials, and certifies no clearance or capacity.

Authored or unverified: (1) All per-segment dimensions, densities, ratios, quantities and layouts, the 26 part sizes and example power loads are assumptions. Wind, heat, noise, refuge capacity, code occupant load and medical staffing are left unset; supplier, ballast and generator values were deliberately not invented. (2) The 17 sources are the pack's 7 Oct 2026 desk check (UK HSE, US ADA, OSHA, NWS, CDC, IBC, Seattle, San Diego, North Carolina). Three code sources were read only as indexed excerpts (two direct pages failed). Not spot-checked by P3; local adoption unverified. The art is illustrative: not a render of the rule coordinates or guest counts, and no clearance, capacity or accessibility is certified. Cameras and layouts in the art are approximate. (4) Checks are layout only: 24 browser checks (six pages at four widths) and JSON parsing. The 20 guest-case formulas were re-run here and agree with the data. No safety, accessibility, performance or engine test exists; performance numbers are design targets.
