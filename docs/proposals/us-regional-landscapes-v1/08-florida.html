Terrain

  • Low coastal barrier/lagoon
  • Nearly flat freshwater sawgrass plain
  • Mangrove estuary and hardwood hammock islands

Vegetation families

  • Sawgrass as broad surface masses
  • Mangrove low irregular shoreline crown band
  • Pine/hardwood hammock island
  • Palms in appropriate habitat/planting, not every marsh

Farmland / field patterns

  • Agricultural rectangles outside protected wetland masks
  • Orchard/grove and sugarcane-like crop families only with source support
  • No farms or houses inside pristine Everglades scene

Small-town families

  • Coastal masonry ranch: 1 storey, shallow hip/gable roof
  • Generic timber coastal cottage, locally appropriate raised floor
  • Compact unbranded low-rise town strip

Rural roads

  • Town concrete sidewalk and curb; rural aggregate verge
  • Canal/causeway only where mapped
  • No road grid cut through sawgrass wilderness

Water edges

  • Freshwater slough separate from tidal mangrove edge
  • Pale beach/dune, lagoon and ocean as separate surfaces
  • Water level follows wet/dry hydroperiod and tide where connected

Seasonal states

  • Spring: late dry-season state in southern wetland; mature greens remain
  • Summer: wet-season freshwater expansion where observed
  • Autumn: wet-season recession timing depends on current water data
  • Winter: dry-season recession; no leaf-off or snowy Everglades default

Exceptions / data gates

  • Freshwater Everglades, saltwater mangroves and beach are distinct subprofiles
  • South Florida wet/dry cycle replaces temperate leaf calendar
  • Northern Florida seasonal cold response differs; generic statewide snow prohibition is wrong

Zoom reading

  • street: Local terrain grade, real plant silhouette, house/road scale and water-edge profile
  • aerial: Settlement footprint, canopy groups, field geometry, drainage connectivity
  • region: Broad relief and coverage fractions; no individual tree/house/crop geometry; preserve resolved coast/channel topology
  • continent: Reproject and aggregate the SAME class fractions and seasonal palette into matte fields; no demographic/place labels or texture substitution
