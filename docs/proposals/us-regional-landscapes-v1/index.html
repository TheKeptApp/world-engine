Terrain

  • Broad alluvial basin
  • Rocky bajada/foothill
  • Incised dry wash

Vegetation families

  • Sonoran: irregular palo-verde/mesquite crowns, creosote shrubs and locally appropriate columnar cactus
  • Mojave: sparse creosote/saltbush; Joshua-tree form only within its mapped habitat
  • Riparian: taller broadleaf corridor only where supported

Farmland / field patterns

  • Irrigated valley rectangles with canals; seasonal crop state distinct from surrounding desert
  • No invented farm grid across rocky slopes or unwatered basin

Small-town families

  • Low masonry/stucco ranch: 1 storey, shallow roof or parapet
  • Historic small commercial strip: 1–2 storeys, shaded recesses
  • Detached metal-roof shed/garage; gravel yard

Rural roads

  • Two-lane asphalt ribbon, aggregate shoulder, shallow drainage swale
  • Sparse rural sidewalks; paved sidewalk/curb in town only
  • Fenced irrigation access and simple culverts

Water edges

  • Dry wash: pale coarse bed and vegetated margins, no permanent blue stream by default
  • Canal: straight defined banks; live water state
  • Reservoir: exposed shore band when supported

Seasonal states

  • Spring: small local green-up and restrained flowers; desert base remains visible
  • Summer: dry exposed ground; monsoon response for Sonoran locations when supported
  • Autumn: shrubs mostly muted green, riparian leaf change locally
  • Winter: basin remains exposed; snow only from elevation and weather

Exceptions / data gates

  • Never use saguaros as a Las Vegas default; Sonoran range mask required
  • Phoenix/Tucson and Las Vegas are separate vegetation subprofiles
  • Season name is not a rain/snow observation

  • Aspen broadleaf groves and riparian cottonwood
  • Alpine low vegetation above mapped treeline

Farmland / field patterns

  • Plains pasture rectangles, irrigated fields and mapped pivot circles
  • Mountain meadows irregular; no crop grid on alpine slopes

Small-town families

  • Front Range brick/wood bungalow and ranch: 1–2 storeys

  • Summer: green alpine meadow and pine, drier plains grass
  • Autumn: localized aspen gold; conifers stable; plains straw
  • Winter: mountain snow coverage from altitude/weather; valley snow intermittent

Exceptions / data gates

  • Do not flatten Denver plains into alpine scenery
  • Treeline, snow and vegetation follow elevation/habitat masks
