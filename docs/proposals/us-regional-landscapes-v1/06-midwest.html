Terrain

  • Low rolling glacial landscape
  • Flat agricultural plain
  • Creek/drainage swale

Vegetation families

  • Mixed broadleaf shade trees
  • Farmstead windbreak/tree grove
  • Hedgerow and creek-edge woody band

Farmland / field patterns

  • Corn/soy-like rectangular mosaic with farm access lanes
  • Pasture, hay and orchard variants
  • Field boundaries preserve tree belts and creeks

Small-town families

  • Brick main-street storefronts: 1–2 storeys
  • Foursquare/gable farmhouse and postwar ranch
  • Gable/gambrel barn, metal shed and silo in observed farm compound

Rural roads

  • Grid-like rural roads with grass drainage ditches
  • Concrete sidewalk/curb in town core
  • Gravel farm drive; road remains full scale

Water edges

  • Soft grassy creek bank, wooded drainage corridor
  • Pond with narrow reed edge
  • No decorative canals replacing drainage

Seasonal states

  • Spring: bare planting ground, leaf-out and patchy green pasture
  • Summer: green crops and broadleaf shade; hay cuts form clean patches
  • Autumn: crop harvest/stubble and muted broadleaf ochre/rust
  • Winter: leaf-off groves and dormant fields; conditional snow/ice

Exceptions / data gates

  • Crop identity and harvest state need data; illustrative crop labels are proposals
  • Do not add individual corn geometry at region zoom
  • Blank storefronts must never become real brand signs

Zoom reading

  • street: Local terrain grade, real plant silhouette, house/road scale and water-edge profile
  • aerial: Settlement footprint, canopy groups, field geometry, drainage connectivity
  • region: Broad relief and coverage fractions; no individual tree/house/crop geometry; preserve resolved coast/channel topology
  • continent: Reproject and aggregate the SAME class fractions and seasonal palette into matte fields; no demographic/place labels or texture substitution
