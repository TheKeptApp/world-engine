Terrain

  • Rounded forested uplands
  • Steep coastal headland
  • Low estuary/floodplain

Vegetation families

  • Tall irregular conifer crowns: Douglas-fir/hemlock/cedar family forms
  • Deciduous alder/maple along settlement and water
  • Low coastal grass, shrubs and understory masses

Farmland / field patterns

  • Valley pasture rectangles and irregular wet margins
  • Row-crop/orchard patches where mapped; coastal bluff is not cropland

Small-town families

  • Timber-clad gable houses: 1–2 storeys, porches
  • Simple pitched-roof mill/coastal sheds
  • Small main street: 1–2 storey wood/masonry fronts

Rural roads

  • Narrow asphalt road follows valley contours; gravel verge and drainage ditch
  • Town concrete sidewalks; simple timber fences
  • Avoid straight road cuts across cliffs

Water edges

  • Rocky headland and pale beach bands; estuary mudflat separate from open sea
  • Freshwater stream gravel bank beneath broadleaf corridor
  • Tide state moves water edge without scaling rocks

Seasonal states

  • Spring: deciduous leaf-out; conifer mass remains dark green
  • Summer: evergreen forest; dry grass locally at valley edges
  • Autumn: deciduous pockets ochre, evergreen stays green
  • Winter: lower-elevation forest remains green; leaf-off pockets, upland snow by observation

Exceptions / data gates

  • No blanket winter snow at sea level
  • Forest canopy at far scale is a continuous matte land-cover field
  • Pacific Northwest coast and inland dry side require separate habitat inputs

Zoom reading

  • street: Local terrain grade, real plant silhouette, house/road scale and water-edge profile
  • aerial: Settlement footprint, canopy groups, field geometry, drainage connectivity
  • region: Broad relief and coverage fractions; no individual tree/house/crop geometry; preserve resolved coast/channel topology
  • continent: Reproject and aggregate the SAME class fractions and seasonal palette into matte fields; no demographic/place labels or texture substitution
