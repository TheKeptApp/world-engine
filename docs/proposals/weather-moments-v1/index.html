From a hero moment to a live world

These are designed comparisons. Weather inputs are planned and are not connected here. Observations, forecasts and history remain separate; missing or stale data must be labelled. Snow cover, wet roads, fog banks and foliage need history or local evidence.

Sky and lighting blend in linear light. Surface wetness persists after precipitation stops. Night lights stay local, and fog uses one coherent depth term across land and water.
