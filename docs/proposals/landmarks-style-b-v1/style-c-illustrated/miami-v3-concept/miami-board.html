Skyline concept

Miami skyline seen from Biscayne Bay east of downtown looking west, typical distance roughly 2 km. Low white unbranded waterfront arena north/right, ochre Freedom Tower modestly behind it, curved stepped blue-glass Miami Tower inland central, Brickell glass skyline south/left. Calm bay foreground. Do not insert Ocean Drive, Fontainebleau or Vizcaya into downtown skyline.
