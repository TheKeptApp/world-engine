Skyline concept

Denver skyline seen from City Park looking west toward downtown and the Front Range, typical downtown distance roughly 4 km. Broad low city base, restrained clustered high-rises, small gold Capitol dome toward left/south, small slender Daniels & Fisher tower where visible, mountainous horizon much wider than city. Do not invent Red Rocks inside downtown or force every selected low building into view.

Constructed view, not a paint-over of an available engine capture. concept – positions and sightlines come from the map. Typical distance is a proposed review camera, not a solved camera. Constructed skyline: context positions, terrain and visibility are illustrative, not a solved geographic camera.
