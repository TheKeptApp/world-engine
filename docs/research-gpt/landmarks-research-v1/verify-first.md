1. **Every geometry row:** a recommended OSM/3DEP route is present, but no per-place footprint IDs, lidar project or completed mesh was validated. Overpass returned HTTP 504 timeouts for the broad and smaller queries. Stop interpreting these failures as absent data; later use a normal OSM export or a properly licensed regional extract.
2. **Every rights row:** protection status is unverified. Research architectural fixation dates, original versus addition designs, ownership and the proposed interactive-3D use. Confirm whether an exception or permission applies. Do not use municipal geometry licences as architecture permissions.
3. **Every trademark row:** risk is editorial triage. Search current names and distinctive shapes, especially Empire State Building, Space Needle and Transamerica Pyramid. Assets contain no logos, sponsor signs, team graphics, university seals, mascots or ads.
4. **Cloud Gate and Chicago Picasso:** reference only, never built. Leave build order blank and omit decorative replicas or texture substitutes. Apply the same rule to embedded art at other sites, including museum lions, stadium animal sculptures and civic-building statuary.
5. **Latest top-20 order:** secondary 2025 table checked; Census Vintage 2025 publication checked. Direct Census retrieval was HTTP 403 and not bypassed. Reconcile with the official table through its ordinary authorized public access when available.
6. **Municipal alternatives:** Chicago footprints, NYC 3D and Miami-Dade local layers require explicit dataset reuse terms. Do not interpret Socrata's documentation software licence as Chicago's data licence or NYC's public download as public-domain status.
7. **Current world state:** check sponsor names, renovations, operable roof pose, Renaissance Center redevelopment and campus-site boundaries at the intended world snapshot date.

## Evidence each built asset must retain
- Identified source objects or project/tile IDs, source URL, retrieval date and geometry bounds.
- Dataset licence text/version and attribution; verify redistribution and derivative-database obligations.
- Horizontal/vertical CRS, units, vertical datum, point-cloud acquisition date and relevant classifications.
- Exterior footprint and roof checks against independently licensed evidence. Document uncertainty instead of silently guessing measured dimensions.
- Architectural review outcome and specific component scope. Separate buildings from artworks, drawings and decorations.
- Logo/signage removal and trademark/trade-dress review outcome. Search labels and marketing uses are a separate scope.
- Defined snapshot date and roof configuration; LOD version and inferred-versus-measured fields.
- Phone views at street level and aerial that preserve recognizability, true scale and the engine's existing performance limits. Per-asset triangle/draw targets are not researched in this pack.

## Release gate
An asset can move from research to production only after its actual geometry source and redistribution terms are recorded, its design/art/mark review has a documented outcome and its scope/date are fixed. A failed or inaccessible source stays unverified. Use another independently authorized source or defer the asset; never bypass a block.
