# University campus scopes

**7 October 2026 — proposed first build scopes for all 24 university entries.** Original files remain unchanged. These shortlists contain six or seven buildings each (within the requested 5–15 maximum), not an entire institutional estate. Every shortlist, recognition priority and modeling boundary is an **ASSUMPTION / proposal**. Official directories/maps below establish source availability and campus context as marked; individual current building names, footprints, dimensions and ownership boundaries still need reconciliation. Names are research labels only, never rendered signage.

## Boundary and source rules

**Proposed method:** start with the selected core below, enumerate OSM building IDs, and validate the relevant campus relation/polygon against an official campus map and openly licensed parcel records. Save a separate multi-polygon view scope; do not treat it as a legal ownership boundary. A visitor map, university address, Clery geography or search coordinate does not by itself define ownership. Scope paths and vegetation to the intended view, without moving mapped features. Disconnected clusters remain separate polygons.

**Verified source policy:** [OSM data is ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). An official PDF or interactive map is boundary/reference evidence, not permission to trace or redistribute its cartography. No open campus-boundary geometry licence or exact relation ID was verified for these 24 sites in this pass. Choose a licensed geometry source before building. Older maps are dated evidence rather than a current inventory. Sources checked or indexed on 2026-10-07; an indexed PDF confirms availability, not exhaustive inspection of every label.

**Proposed phone detail:** at 50–300 m aerial, preserve campus layout, height hierarchy, landmark towers/domes, rooflines, quads, shorelines and canopy gaps. At 5–40 m street level, add major entrances, column/arch cadence and broad material/color families. Projected size depends on field of view and camera distance: detail smaller than roughly 2–3 screen pixels should collapse into tone (assumption; validate on an iPhone). Preserve measured scale. No interiors, monuments, statues, murals, seals, mascots, names, sponsor marks or decorative replicas. Material/color simplification must not imply new physical measurements.

**Effort:** all multi-building scopes are initially high relative agent effort (assumption), because they require separate geometry and rights checks for each building. Phase them: anchors first, supporting quad edges second. Reuse facade/vegetation rules; do not budget every campus as one draw call. Existing engine limits still apply; no per-campus triangle performance was measured.

## Notable scope decisions (proposals)

- Northwestern means the Evanston lakefront, not the Chicago campus or stadium projects.
- University of Miami means Coral Gables; FIU means Modesto A. Maidique. Their medical, marine and Biscayne Bay campuses are separate projects.
- Harvard means the Yard; Columbia means Morningside; USC means University Park. Institution-wide coordinates are not build boundaries.
- Minnesota uses distinct East/West Bank view polygons. Washington Avenue Bridge is a separate infrastructure asset, not a campus building.
- Wayne State excludes Detroit Public Library from its university building set. University visitors may see the library nearby, but that does not make it a Wayne building.
- UC San Diego excludes the Stuart Collection; Harvard excludes John Harvard; USC excludes Tommy Trojan. Equivalent artworks are excluded everywhere.
- Current inventories and map age matter: reconcile older Rice, Penn and Denver references; never instantiate future master-plan buildings as current structures.
