# Verify first — fifteen consequential checks

October 7, 2026. These are specific acceptance questions, not statements that all integrations are cleared. Keep a dated copy of each provider's terms and written approval. The legal questions need qualified counsel; this research is not a legal opinion.

| Priority | Claim / uncertainty to check | Human check and pass evidence | Consequence if wrong | Primary starting points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | WorldEngine can distribute its intended mixed-source city package while retaining a proprietary engine. | Counsel reviews one actual exported package, its derivative-database offer, OSM/Overture source notices and independently sourced layers. Confirm notice/download obligations and separation from executable code. | Sale/export can breach data licences; open-data geometry is not an exclusive proprietary moat. | [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [Overture attribution](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/) |
| 2 | CTA and RTD feeds may be used for these consumer, gaming and licensed-media products. | Obtain purpose/redistribution interpretation in writing, not merely API credentials. Test the actual rail vehicle/prediction types and source timestamps. | A public feed may be purpose-restricted, or the displayed train may be a prediction rather than an observed location. | [CTA licence](https://www.transitchicago.com/downloads/sch_data/developers_license_agreement.htm), [RTD feeds](https://www.rtd-denver.com/open-records/open-spatial-information/real-time-feeds) |
| 3 | Amtrak train status can be retrieved and redistributed commercially. | Ask Amtrak for an authorized data/API agreement; do not build on an undocumented endpoint or infer public-domain rights from federal funding. All twenty station-page access checks here were unresolved. | Automated retrieval/redistribution can violate terms; product continuity is weak. | [Amtrak terms](https://www.amtrak.com/web-notices-terms-of-use), [official reports](https://www.amtrak.com/reports-documents) |
| 4 | The target minimum iPhone really sustains the full scene at sixty fps. | Run repeatable cold/warm, street/sky and weather/transit-on thermal soaks; collect percentile frame time, memory, upload stalls and battery/thermal state. Test real device, not simulator only. | The defining product experience or marketing promise fails under realistic use. | [RealityKit LowLevelMesh](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/realitykit/lowlevelmesh); repository performance budgets are goals until measured |
| 5 | The selected web/native asset path supports the intended compressed meshes/textures. | Exercise one exported production district through three.js and RealityKit, including decode, upload, materials and fallbacks. Do not infer native glTF/Draco/meshopt/KTX2 support from a web loader. | Download savings can become CPU stalls or unsupported materials/assets. | [Apple entity loading](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/realitykit/loading-entities-from-a-file), [three.js GLTFLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html), [KTX](https://www.khronos.org/ktx/) |
| 6 | HydroRIVERS and other global source rights fit ODbL derivative-pack distribution. | Review HydroSHEDS bespoke raw/derivative redistribution rules alongside the actual delivery licence; compare HydroLAKES separately. Require compatible permissions before import. | A commercially usable source can still be incompatible with the intended redistribution model. | [HydroRIVERS](https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrorivers), [HydroSHEDS technical documentation/licence](https://data.hydrosheds.org/file/technical-documentation/HydroSHEDS_TechDoc_v1_4.pdf), [HydroLAKES](https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrolakes) |
| 7 | Miami/coastal heights use compatible datums, vintages and surface definitions. | Validate benchmark locations and transformation uncertainty; distinguish bare-earth terrain from canopy/building surface, and chart/tidal datums from land height. Confirm actual bathymetry coverage. | Coastlines float or flood visually; false hazard accuracy is implied. | [NOAA VDatum](https://vdatum.noaa.gov/), [BlueTopo FAQ](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/data/bluetopo_faq.html), [Copernicus DEM licence](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs/SentinelHub/Data/DEM/resources/license/License-COPDEM-30.pdf) |
| 8 | The pipeline is using maintained hydrography and the intended terrain/imagery releases. | Pin 3DHP services versus frozen NHD legacy files; confirm NAIP acquisition date and item-level rights. GEBCO landing/linked metadata showed a 2026/2025 mismatch: inspect the downloaded release before use. | Silent stale geometry or mismatched model resolution undermines regional truth. | [3DHP access](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-hydrography-program/access-3dhp-data-products), [NAIP archive](https://registry.opendata.aws/naip/), [GEBCO current landing page](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry-data) |
| 9 | Current USGS water APIs and claimed feed cadences match operational freshness. | Test current API migration/deprecation notices and observations' actual timestamps. Sample outages and late positions; measure acquisition latency separately from refresh interval. | A nominally live display uses obsolete endpoints or old measurements. | [USGS Water Data APIs](https://www.usgs.gov/tools/usgs-water-data-apis), [current API](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/), [Metrolink GTFS-RT](https://metrolinktrains.com/about/gtfs/gtfs-rt-access/) |
| 10 | Air-quality, smoke and weather displays communicate what the data actually measures. | Read AirNow permitted uses, final/preliminary data caveats and forecast model documentation; test source/age labels. Verify WeatherKit broadcast/export rights separately from app access. | A modeled forecast becomes a purported block-level measurement or safety recommendation. | [AirNow guidelines](https://docs.airnowapi.org/docs/DataUseGuidelines.pdf), [HRRR-Smoke](https://rapidrefresh.noaa.gov/hrrr/HRRRsmoke/), [WeatherKit](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/), [NWS API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api) |
| 11 | Linked road-closure, bike-share, charger and partner-buoy data have usable downstream licences. | Inspect the exact feed's grant, attribution and commercial export rights. Confirm whether chargers have status or only inventory. Registry/software openness is not an underlying-data grant. | Unauthorized licensing, misleading real-time availability, or missing coverage. | [WZDx registry](https://data.transportation.gov/Roadways-and-Bridges/Work-Zone-Data-Feed-Registry/69qe-yiui), [GBFS FAQ](https://www.gbfs.org/documentation/faq/), [AFDC API](https://developer.nlr.gov/docs/transportation/alt-fuel-stations-v1/), [NDBC access](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/faq/rt_data_access.shtml) |
| 12 | The twenty-metro and national-place counts describe the intended universes. | Reproduce the Census sort; spatially join shortlisted sites to CBSA boundaries; resolve 64 blocked/unresolved operator references. Process agency datasets for exact counts with deduplication and scope, rather than treating stops as hubs or protected polygons as parks. | A priority inventory becomes a falsely complete or geographically incorrect national directory. | [Census estimates CSV](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest/datasets/2020-2025/metro/totals/cbsa-est2025-alldata.csv), [BTS transit map](https://www.bts.gov/national-transit-map), [CMS hospitals](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/xubh-q36u), [PAD-US analysis](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-spatial-analysis-and-reports) |
| 13 | Venue branding, public artworks and commercial event schedules can appear in partner exports. | Counsel distinguishes building depictions from artwork/photo rights; obtain owner licences for logos/sponsor treatments and supplier rights for schedules. Never assume visible-from-the-street means unrestricted copyrighted-art reproduction. | Takedowns or partner indemnity exposure. | [US copyright statute](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html), [architectural registration guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/register/va-architecture.html), [Ticketmaster developer terms](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/support/terms-of-use/) |
| 14 | Proprietary competitor/generated content can be cached, exported or integrated at advertised list prices. | Obtain written Google/Cesium/AI-vendor permissions for the exact offline/external-customer workflow. Review each model/software/output licence separately; check whether quoted tier applies to external solutions. | Vendor lock-in, surprise commercial contract, prohibited export or incompatible model artefacts. | [Google tile policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies), [Cesium integration pricing](https://cesium.com/platform/cesium-ion/pricing/), [Gaussian splatting reference licence](https://github.com/graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting/blob/main/LICENSE.md?plain=1) |
| 15 | Buyers will pay the proposed pilot prices and the work can be delivered profitably. | Ask for three scoped incumbent quotes where possible; secure one paid pilot with named operator, success metric, renewal decision and capped revision/traffic/feed costs. Reconfirm published anchors at purchase. | Attractive visuals do not translate into revenue; unbounded custom delivery consumes the team. | [Weather Company media](https://www.weathercompany.com/media/), [Mapme pricing](https://mapme.com/pricing/), [Racemap pricing](https://go.racemap.com/pricing), [vMix pricing](https://www.vmix.com/purchase/) |

No signup, procurement, legal approval, performance benchmark, live integration or buyer interview was performed by this research. These checks are the next evidence-producing steps, not hidden completed work.
