# WorldEngine country expansion shortlist v1

Research date: **7 October 2026**. Decision: select a few launch countries beyond the US for a stylized live 3D world built from OSM and open data. Research was split among Europe, Asia-Pacific and Americas agents, then reviewed and ranked by a lead agent. No Git operations were used.

## Recommendation

Launch **Netherlands, Canada and United Kingdom**, starting with bounded city regions. This is a product launch ranking, not a claim that entire countries are uniformly mapped or technically ready. All three still require the data and product checks below.

1. **Netherlands — strongest 3D foundation.** National building data and detailed 3DBAG roof geometry provide a practical route beyond OSM extrusion. A compact geography and recognizable canals, narrow gables, brick streets and cycling infrastructure make a coherent, distinctive first expansion. Cycling accounted for 27% of trips in 2023, a useful mobility signal, although it does not measure demand for WorldEngine. The downside is a smaller iOS audience than the English-speaking contenders: the September 2026 mobile-web usage proxy is 31.82%. Start with Amsterdam and Utrecht, plus a rural water/vegetation sample. Validate 3DBAG attribution, selected release freshness, tile conversion and KNMI's November 2026 warning-feed migration. [3DBAG manual](https://docs.3dbag.nl/en/) · [CBS cycling](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/visualisaties/verkeer-en-vervoer/personen/fietsen).

2. **Canada — strongest fit with a US-first iOS launch.** Statistics Canada's ODB v3 contains approximately 14.4 million building features; NRCan's HRDEM adds valuable local terrain and surface data. These sources permit substantial reuse of the US renderer and asset library while Vancouver's water and mountains and Montreal's urban fabric offer differentiation. September 2026 iOS mobile-web usage is 64.87%, the highest proxy among this shortlist. Start with Vancouver and Toronto; make Montreal a regional style and French-language follow-on. ODB footprints do not guarantee building height or roof semantics, HRDEM coverage varies, the selected MSC alert feed licence remains unverified, and TransLink's custom API terms can carry commercial conditions. [ODB release](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/250415/dq250415d-eng.htm) · [HRDEM](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/957782bf-847c-4644-a757-e383c0057995) · [TransLink terms](https://www.translink.ca/about-us/doing-business-with-translink/app-developer-resources/terms-of-use).

3. **United Kingdom — best combination of English-language reach and useful live layers.** The road/path ecosystem, London transport feeds, open terrain coverage in England and a recognizable brick/terrace/hedgerow palette support a practical launch. September 2026 iOS mobile-web usage is 46.88%. Natural England found that 66% of England adults visited a green or natural space in the preceding fortnight in its April 2024–March 2025 survey; this supports an outdoor use case, not a paid-app forecast. Start with London and one terrain-rich region in England. The major weakness is buildings: open road data should not be mistaken for an open national detailed building/roof model. Met Office Weather DataHub permits commercial application display but restricts original-form redistribution; do not bundle its raw feeds as open assets. England's lidar does not cover the whole UK. [Natural England survey](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/the-people-and-nature-surveys-for-england-adults-data-y5q4-january-2025-march-2025/adults-year-5-annual-report-april-2024-march-2025).

**If asset fidelity is the overriding priority, replace the UK with France.** France's authoritative building and lidar ecosystem makes it a strong alternative, but roof reconstruction, French localization, geographic variety and feed integration add work. Germany is another serious data-first option, but national basemap.de licence evidence conflicts: indexed BKG production metadata lists CC BY 4.0, while the current service page links to beta terms that prohibit download/storage outside the service. Commercial offline asset redistribution is **UNVERIFIED** until the precise production endpoint and terms are pinned; bulk LoD2-DE has its own restricted route. [Linked beta terms](https://basemap.de/data/produkte/3d/lizenzen/lizenz_basemapde_3D-Beta.pdf). If an Asia-Pacific presence is required at launch, New Zealand is the simpler scoped pilot; Japan provides a more distinctive urban showcase but needs a dedicated asset pipeline and weather-service review. Australia's high iOS proxy does not eliminate BoM's commercial licensing requirement.

## How the ranking was made

Assumptions: WorldEngine is iOS-first at launch; the existing US rendering and asset pipeline is reusable; the team can add some regional palettes but wants to minimize new language and feed operations; the business needs commercial use and redistribution of generated client assets. Paid weather or transit agreements are possible but are a launch cost and dependency. No budget, acquisition model or partner commitments were supplied.

We compare, in order: (1) legally usable building/3D/terrain inputs and practical conversion, (2) road/path and live-layer readiness, (3) iOS reach and plausible use cases, (4) visual distinctiveness relative to asset effort, and (5) legal and operating uncertainty. The ordinal ranking is lead-agent judgment, not an empirical weighted score. Small shifts in priorities can change the middle of the list. Neither data access nor a permissive licence proves client redistribution works after combining datasets.

OSM assessments are **qualitative and unverified against a current extract**. No national building-completeness percentage, road-completeness percentage, height-tag rate or roof-tag rate was measured. Existing imports and authoritative datasets indicate a route to quality, not proof of present OSM coverage. The 2023 global building-attribute study documents large spatial variation; it is not a 2026 country audit. Treat apparent road maturity separately from footprint presence, building:levels/height availability and roof:shape/roof:height detail. [Biljecki, Chow and Lee, 2023](https://filipbiljecki.com/publications/2023_bae_osm_qa.pdf).

The requested **iPhone ownership share is unverified**. The CSV reports a consistently dated substitute: September 2026 Statcounter iOS share of mobile web usage. It measures observed pageviews, not installed devices, iPhone sales, paying users or app adoption. Android/iOS web behaviour and sampled sites can bias it. The numerical precision is the source's, not the precision of a market forecast. September figures remain within the provider's 45-day revision window as of this report. The figures are attributed to Statcounter; its data is published under CC BY-SA 3.0. [Statcounter methodology and reuse terms](https://gs.statcounter.com/faq). Outdoor and mobility statistics are use-case proxies; maps/events demand, willingness to pay, acquisition cost and all suggested partners' licensing interest remain unverified.

## Shared world layers

- **OSM:** commercially usable under ODbL, with attribution and database duties. A rendered world can be a Produced Work; a merged or enhanced spatial database can trigger derivative-database obligations. Assess the actual data architecture using OSMF's collective-database and layer guidance. Keeping files separate is not by itself a legal conclusion. Avoid assuming every CC BY dataset can be imported into OSM without a waiver. [OSM licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [OSMF FAQ](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Licence_and_Legal_FAQ).
- **Night lights:** covered EOG VIIRS VNL composites are commercially reusable under CC BY 4.0 with credit and marking requirements. Use them for regional night ambience. They are historical, coarse grids, not live lights, individual lamps, illuminated windows or evidence of an event. Selected product extent matters in far northern Canada. Other EOG products can have different terms. [EOG licence](https://eogdata.mines.edu/files/EOG_products_CC_License.pdf).
- **Terrain:** Copernicus GLO-90 Full Free Open is a global fallback under its specific licence. It is a 90m digital surface model, not street-scale bare-earth terrain. Use national lidar DTMs where possible. The reviewed GLO90 grant does not authorize GLO30 or GLO10; review the selected higher-resolution product separately. [Copernicus licence, pp18–20](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-06/copernicus_contributing_mission_data_access_v2_cop_dem_licenses.pdf).
- **Trees:** ESA WorldCover supplies 10m land-cover classes for 2020/2021 under CC BY 4.0. It can guide tree density and biomes but cannot establish species, exact stem positions or current canopy. Stylized tree palettes are design judgments; add local inventories only after checking their licences. [WorldCover](https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access).
- **Weather fallback:** ECMWF's real-time catalogue became open under CC BY 4.0 on 1 October 2025. Free subsets and paid delivery services differ. It can support atmospheric effects across all 12 countries but does not replace authoritative national warnings or exempt an app from regulated weather-service rules. [ECMWF announcement](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/about/media-centre/news/2025/ecmwf-makes-its-entire-real-time-catalogue-open-all).

## What needs verification before launch

1. For each proposed city, pin an OSM extract date and authoritative dataset releases. Compare building count and area against reference footprints after geometry repair and matching. Measure usable height/levels and roof tags separately; inspect invalid values, multi-part buildings, bridges and tunnels. Test an urban centre, suburb and rural/outdoor area; results from a centre do not establish country completeness.
2. Validate the intended height pipeline: use semantic 3D where available, or a correctly aligned DSM–DTM estimate with canopy filtering. Levels-to-height conversion is an explicit approximation. Test renderer silhouettes, roof reconstruction, memory, load times and offline client packaging on launch iPhones. No sample downloads or conversions were tested in this research.
3. Record the exact product licence, version and attribution text for every selected layer. Check rights for derived mesh/tile redistribution, offline caches, mixed OSM databases and onward licensing to customers. Public download availability and GTFS/GTFS-RT format do not imply unrestricted reuse. Weather screen display, derived effects and raw-feed redistribution can have different permissions.
4. Test live feeds for keys, quotas, latency, outages, geographic scope and warning provenance. Do not depict static GTFS as realtime. Resolve the Netherlands warning migration, UK weather redistribution terms, Canada's MSC licence and agency-specific transit permissions. A feed description was verified; operational delivery and uptime were not.
5. Review the actual location-app design against the relevant laws in countries.csv: identifiable location, foreground/background collection, lawful basis or consent, retention, children, SDKs, cross-border processing and user rights. OS permission prompts do not complete this review. Public geodata rights do not grant rights to track people. Japan's forecast service rules and South Korea's location-service/map-export rules need product-specific review. The research is not comprehensive legal clearance.
6. Validate demand with a small localized launch test and discussions with the proposed partner categories. No partner was contacted. Separate licensing a world/data product to tourism or events organizations from permission to use their own data, logos, POIs or event content.

## Files and evidence conventions

- **countries.csv:** exactly 12 ranked countries, with detailed coverage, height/roof, weather/alerts, transit, terrain, visual/tree, market, partner and legal assessments. Shared night lights and vegetation layers are linked by IDs. Rank reasons and pilot scope are explicit.
- **datasets.csv:** dataset and feed shortlist, commercial and redistribution status, short licence excerpts, source URLs, conditions and unresolved checks. A blank licence quote means no relevant licence wording was verified; it is not permission. A quote can refer to the first row covering a shared licence; this avoids duplicating excerpts. Read the full linked licence, not just the excerpt.
- **sources.md:** source register with stable IDs, links, access date, claim scope and evidence limitations. Licence quotes appear in datasets.csv, not repeated here.

**VERIFIED** means the stated page/product/licence fact was found in a publisher, official legal source or explicitly identified source; it does not mean national completeness, pipeline feasibility or legal clearance was established. **UNVERIFIED** identifies unresolved evidence. **INFERENCE** identifies architecture/vegetation design, likely use cases, partner hypotheses and ranking judgment. Search-index evidence and access failures are preserved as limitations where applicable. Sources can change; pin the selected versions before implementation.

## Delivery validation

The CSVs were checked for 12 unique country rows, contiguous ranks, complete required fields, source-ID resolution and exact data-matrix preservation. 58 dataset/feed rows and 109 unique source URLs are included. CSV uses UTF-8 and quoted RFC4180-style records; it has no formulas. Dataset retrieval, licence negotiations and device rendering remain untested.
