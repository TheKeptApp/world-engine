# WorldEngine shortlist v1 — source register

Accessed 7 October 2026 unless otherwise stated. This is a decision shortlist, not a current OSM extract audit or completed legal review. Licence excerpts are in datasets.csv. URLs may redirect or require access; index-only evidence and conflicting licences are explicitly flagged.

Primary sources support dataset and legal facts. Statcounter is the original provider of the usage metric. OSM community documentation describes imports, not measured national completeness. AP News is a secondary cross-check for the Korean 2026 announcement where the official page could not be fully fetched. Art difficulty, partner candidates, market fit and rank are inference; partner interest and app demand are unverified.
