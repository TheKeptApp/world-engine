All empirical architectural shares remain unverified. Generator weights normalize only the five selected low-rise archetypes per metro; they exclude towers, mobile homes and omitted styles, and are not population estimates. Numeric pitch, setback, lot widths, tree spacing, parkway widths and hex palettes are explicit engineering proposals. Municipal document access does not license its photographs, illustrations or commercial bulk assessor redistribution. No real addresses or logos are examples here.
