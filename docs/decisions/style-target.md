# Style target: rich stylized (decision B)

Decided by R, 6 Oct 2026. Binding for 5A, P2 and the look loop (P3).

## The target

WorldEngine aims for **rich stylized**: simplified geometry with material hints, varied lawns, real haze, and surfaces that respond to wet and dry. It should read at phone size as a specific, lived-in place in a specific weather and light. It is not a photograph.

## Push toward photoreal in

- **Colour:** restrained, believable palettes; seasonal colour (fall, winter) that matches the region.
- **Light:** one coherent sun, warm/cool balance, long low-sun shadows, soft contact shading.
- **Atmosphere:** haze and depth by distance, smoke and fog that tint the whole scene, continuous horizons.
- **Weather:** wet sheen and puddles that read at a glance, patchy snow, rain darkening, overcast softness.

## Keep stylized

- Geometry: simple masses with clear roof and crown families; no modelled trim detail beyond what reads at phone size.
- Surfaces: material hints (tone, colour, a few large-scale patterns), not photo textures.

## Do not chase

- Individual bricks, shingles, grass blades or leaves.
- Photo textures or painted ground imagery.
- Realistic cars, signage or street clutter.
- Fine detail that only shows when zoomed in.

## Style anchors

The colour and mood anchors are the three regions concepts R called out:

- `docs/proposals/regions-chicagoland-miami/images/03-northshore-fall.png` (fall)
- `docs/proposals/regions-chicagoland-miami/images/04-northshore-snow.png` (winter)
- `docs/proposals/regions-chicagoland-miami/images/06-chicago-alley-snow.png` ("very Chicago")

Match their colour, light and mood. Where a concept is more photoreal than this target, match its light, colour and weather, not its surface detail.

## In practice

- **5A and P2:** favour colour, light, atmosphere and weather legibility over detail density. A change that makes the weather or time of day read faster at phone size beats one that adds geometry or texture.
- **Look loop:** GRADING.md §S applies this: colour, light, atmosphere and weather weigh more than surface detail, and missing photo-like detail is not penalized.
