# Daytime lighting master: what changed

Owner decision (R, 2026-10-07): `docs/proposals/house-contrast-v1/paintover-values.json` `sharedLighting` (id `shared_clear_midafternoon_hero_v1`) is the **daytime lighting master**; it wins over older paint-over values for sky, sun, shadow tint, exposure and ground colours. Night / blue-hour / fog (night-fog-v1) are separate states. Generated by hand from the pack files with a flatten-and-diff (read-only inputs). Who: **5A** = light/sky/shadow/exposure/grade, **P2** = ground, house, vegetation colours.

## Summary: biggest visible moves

1. Sky: per-view grades replaced by one shared 3-stop gradient `#73A5CC` (90°) / `#A2C4DC` (30°) / `#DBDCD1` (0°); Lakeview loses its lavender→peach golden sky (`#B8B5CC`→`#E7C6A4`), Denver's zenith deepens `#8DADC9`→`#73A5CC`. (5A)
2. Lakeview goes from golden hour (illustrative sun 8°, shadows 7.1× height) to clear mid-afternoon (sun 40° az 225°, shadows 1.19× height). (5A)
3. Warmth grade removed: +4 % (Denver) / +8 % (Lakeview) → 0 %; warmth now only from sun colour `#FFE8C6`. (5A)
4. Exposure unified at +0.35 EV once, no per-view overrides; Lakeview +0.20 → +0.35 (+0.15 EV brighter). (5A)
5. Shadow depth unified at 38 % (shadow/lit 0.62); Lakeview 44 % → 38 % (lighter shade). (5A)
6. New explicit shadow tint `#7F8F99` (cool-neutral gray-blue) and sky-fill colour `#AEBCCA`; old Lakeview 'lavender-blue shade' is gone. (5A)
7. Key:fill on the witness plane fixed at direct .38 / fill .62 (ratio .613); the legacy look-fix 2.2 key:fill must not also apply. (5A)
8. Saturation unified at 1.08; Lakeview 1.06 → 1.08. Contrast 1.06 everywhere (unchanged). (5A)
9. New dry ground base swatches: asphalt `#626A70`, concrete `#C5C0B3`, lawn `#73865B`, curb `#B9B7AD` (no paint-over-v1 hex before; engine summer road `#898A89` is much lighter). (P2)
10. Wilmette and the Lakeview postcard get the same lighting (no paintover-v1 frame before); postcard gains tree palette `#49664C/#6F8758/#879466`, bark `#716659`, 3 parked cars. (P2)
