# Foliage rendering v1 — A5 research

8 October 2026. Research only; no renderer, generator, profile, approval status or data ingestion changed. Implementation inspected at `efa639bdbe5fa497a5c38c558fabb569349c5b00`; A2 recipes identify their own earlier implementation/evidence commits. External documentation checked on this date. Numerical experiments below are **engineering guesses**, not measured phone performance or approved look values.

## 1. Rendering: recommendation and evidence

**Improve the existing opaque crowns first:** (1) layered lighting and bounded regional/individual colour, (2) better silhouette/normal allocation within the existing crown LOD budgets, then (3) a small near-view leaf-cluster-card experiment only if opaque crowns still fail. Preserve native instancing, independent shadow detail and stable seasonal identity. Do not begin with a forest-wide card conversion or a purchased photoreal asset library.

This order addresses A3's actual finding: all four native heroes had foliage **2/5**, with broad smooth crown shading and overly strong Lakeview greens. Individual leaves are explicitly not required by the look rubric. A3's observations, rather than an assumed triangle shortage, motivate this recommendation. See [native baseline](../lookloop/a3-baseline.md), [grading §S/§M/§N](../lookloop/GRADING.md), [current roadmap](../roadmap.md) and the separate [web baseline](../lookloop/web-baseline.md). **Likelihood of reaching 3/5 is a hypothesis; no new score is claimed.**

### Budget contract

The [device-tier study](../perf/device-tiers-v1.md) distinguishes the existing floor from proposed standard/hero ceilings. These are whole-world ceilings, not foliage allocations:

| Tier | Main triangles | Shadow triangles, all passes summed | Main draws | Resident textures | Total app footprint |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Floor, geometry/draw limits binding | <400,000 in existing strict test | 150,000 | 100 | 96 MiB proposed | 600 MiB proposed |
| Standard, provisional | 500,000 | 180,000 | 120 | 128 MiB | 750 MiB |
| Hero, provisional | 600,000 | 225,000 | 140 | 192 MiB | 900 MiB |

Standard/hero and all memory allowances remain guesses pending measurement. The user's iPhone 14 Pro is the current standard test priority; a laptop Chrome result does not qualify it, Safari, or the weakest floor phone. Existing Lakeview floor overage cannot be waived by calling the run standard. No new device tests were run here.

Cost model: `mainTris = sum(visibleInstanceCount × selectedMeshTris)`; `shadowTris = sum over lights/passes/cascades(sum(submittedCasterInstances × proxyTris))`. Instancing shares geometry storage and submissions, **not** transformed triangles. Draws depend on occupied mesh/material/LOD/spatial batches and render passes, not just species count. Texture figures below include full mip chains, approximately `width × height × bytesPerTexel × 4/3`, and exclude allocation alignment, render targets and staging. These are arithmetic estimates, not GPU captures. Alpha coverage and screen area can dominate time despite low triangle counts. Apple explains the loss of opaque hidden-surface-removal efficiency with alpha test/blending in [Metal performance guidance][apple-perf].

### Proven techniques and bounded cost estimates

Numbers marked “example” are proposed asset envelopes, independent of hardware; no millisecond claim can be inferred. Texture sharing assumes one shared atlas, not one per tree.

| Technique and primary evidence | Triangles and main draws | Texture/instance storage estimate | Shadow and phone trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opaque instanced crowns, spatially batched** — [Apple instancing][apple-instance], [three InstancedMesh][three-instance] | Existing P2 900/200/52/12 deciduous ceilings. Ideal one draw per occupied geometry/material batch per main pass; not one per tree. Five forms ×3 occupied LODs ×1 material =15 illustrative batches before cells/variants. | No new image texture required. Example float4×4 matrix +float4 colour =80 bytes/instance, 78.1 KiB per 1,000; engine buffers/duplication additional. | Lowest integration risk. Shadows still submit every instance per pass. Large merged bounds reduce culling precision; more cells improve culling but add draws. Reuse whole crown meshes rather than making every lobe a separate entity. |
| **Alpha-tested leaf-cluster cards** — [SpeedTree rendering][speedtree], [Crysis vegetation][crysis] | Example 40–60 quads =80–120 leaf triangles/tree, plus trunk; P2's 56 already lies here. Separate bark/card materials can add a batch per occupied group. | Shared 1024² R8 alpha 1.33 MiB mipped; RGBA8 colour+alpha 5.33 MiB. A second RGBA8 normal map adds another 5.33 MiB. No normal map proposed initially. | Overlapping cards shade discarded pixels and repeat alpha samples in every shadow pass. Low polygon count can be slower than opaque crowns. Avoid alpha-blended overlapping leaves; tightly fit cards to clusters. Thin edges shimmer or disappear as mips change. Use padded cells and preserve coverage in mip generation; test night, fog, leaf drop and snow. |
| **Billboard / multi-view impostor at distance** — [SpeedTree rendering][speedtree], [population/LOD documentation][speedtree-lod] | One camera-facing quad 2 tris; crossed pair 4; top-facing addition 2. Example 1,000 trees: 2k–6k main tris instead of P2's 12k skyline ceiling. Occupied atlas/material/cell groups still cause draws. | Example eight 256² RGBA8 views packed into one 1024×512 atlas: 2.67 MiB mipped per appearance family. Normal/depth textures, seasons and additional elevations multiply this. | Flat cards rotate, lose parallax and fail aerials unless capture angles include elevation. Baked sunlight conflicts with live sun; prefer albedo/normal relighting or keep P2's 12-tri shell. Use a stable geometric shadow proxy, never a camera-facing shadow card. Shadows can be omitted only where their projected contribution is negligible. Savings are modest against the existing skyline shell. |
| **Crown LOD chain with stable silhouette** — [three LOD][three-lod], [SpeedTree LOD][speedtree-lod] | Existing 900→200→52→12; preserve trunk/crown bounds and leaf fraction. Example 10% transition band is a **guess**, not a new pack value. Two rendered LODs during a cross-fade temporarily sum their costs. | Store all LOD meshes; image memory zero for current opaque path. Shared atlas for card variants. | Use projected pixel size and hysteresis as a proposed general policy, not tuned block distances. Avoid a sudden round-to-diamond shape change. Keep shadow LOD independent. Dither fades introduce noise; hard switches with matched envelopes/hysteresis may suit phone scale better. |
| **Per-instance colour/phenology variation** — [Apple WWDC25][apple-wwdc], [three instancing][three-instance] | +0 tris, +0 draws if carried in existing batch data; duplicating materials per tree destroys this benefit. | Example RGB float32 +12 bytes/tree (11.7 KiB/1,000); can instead reuse existing palette/seed data. No new image required. | +0 shadow geometry. Stable ID/position, species and date, not random every frame/LOD. Native already varies value and timing; fix regional colour precedence before widening random hue. |
| **GPU wind** — [GPU Gems tree wind][wind], [Crysis vegetation][crysis] | +0 tris/draws for displacement in existing vertex path; more vertex arithmetic. Bound growth must include motion. | Weights/phase in existing vertex or instance channels; no image needed for simple analytic wind. | Vertex work repeats in shadows if animated. Sharing the displacement in depth/shadow paths avoids detached shadows. Existing 3 cm native sway is already cheap; far crowns need no leaf flutter. Wind will not fix a poor still-image crown score. |
| **Cheap translucency / wrapped light, coherent normals and baked occlusion** — [Crysis shading][crysis], [Apple custom materials][apple-custom] | +0 tris/draws when folded into current material; extra arithmetic per shaded pixel. Rebalancing normals/AO can be texture-free. | Use vertex/instance mask; optional 512² R8 thickness map would be 0.33 MiB mipped, **not recommended initially**. | +0 shadow tris. Approximate thin-part backlighting only, tied to sun visibility; do not light a solid crown as if hollow or add permanent emissive glow at night. Match calibration's shadow/light witness; avoid double AO and double fill. |
| **Shadow proxy, caster culling and bounded shadow resolution** — [Apple shadow API][apple-shadow], [three Object3D][three-object], local PORT-LIST §4 | Main geometry unchanged if proxy only in shadow passes. Example 100×52-tri proxies =5,200 shadow tris per pass; three fully overlapping passes =15,600 versus 270,000 for 100×900 near meshes. | Example one 2048² depth32 shadow map =16 MiB; 1024² =4 MiB; actual format/layers/targets must be measured. | The 270k example exceeds even proposed hero's 225k shadow allowance. Coarse proxies may make too-solid shadows: compare pavement under canopy, winter branches, low sun and off-camera casters. Increasing filtering/resolution spends fragment/bandwidth/memory budget even with identical triangles. |

**Bushes and hedges:** apply the same material/colour/shadow rules to opaque clustered masses; do not fill hedge interiors with cards. Illustrative proposed bush chain 120/40/12 triangles means 60 near bushes cost 7,200 main triangles before any hedge geometry; these are new experiment values, not measured existing costs. A continuous hedge can share geometry/material batches, but culling and parcel boundaries still matter. Near bushes often occupy many pixels, so transparency is not automatically cheap.

**Worked block envelope, illustrative only:** 20 near deciduous trees ×900 +60 middle ×200 +120 far ×52 +200 skyline ×12 =**38,640 main triangles** (9.66% floor, 7.73% standard, 6.44% hero). Add the 60 example near bushes: **45,840**, or 11.46%/9.17%/7.64%. This leaves 354,160/454,160/554,160 nominal triangles for everything else (strict floor still needs a margin). It is not a measured “typical block” inventory. If 80 tree proxies of 52 tris overlap three shadow passes, they cost **12,480** shadow tris, before bushes/buildings. A 15-draw foliage allocation would use 15%/12.5%/10.71% of main draw ceilings; species/LOD/cell fragmentation can exceed it. Record actual totals, not just this estimate.

### Platform boundaries: portable technique, different renderer contract

Apple documents surface shaders and geometry modifiers for `CustomMaterial`, providing the appropriate native entry points for tint/light response and vertex wind [apple-custom]. `opacityThreshold` supports cutout semantics [apple-alpha]; the existing native material's blending/depth/culling still needs direct validation. Do not assume alpha-to-coverage or an arbitrary extra texture binding is exposed just because Metal supports it. Apple's WWDC25 explains `MeshInstancesComponent` and native instance-specific render data via `LowLevelBuffer` on iOS [apple-wwdc]. The repo already uses instancing; adding one entity/material per tree would regress that design. Verify installed SDK/deployment availability before proposing newer shadow controls [apple-shadow].

The inspected [A2 main.js](../../web/bakeoff/main.js) uses **`three/webgpu` WebGPURenderer with `forceWebGL:true`**, and [foliage.js](../../web/bakeoff/foliage.js) uses TSL nodes. This is a WebGL backend of the node renderer, not proof of drop-in compatibility with classic `WebGLRenderer` shader hooks.

**Three.js-specific: do not copy into RealityKit:** `BufferGeometry`/`mergeVertices`, `InstancedMesh`/`InstancedBufferAttribute`, TSL `leafTint`/`leafMask`, `castShadow`, camera/light layers, PCF settings, `customDepthMaterial`/`customDistanceMaterial` paths, GLSL/onBeforeCompile snippets, and web irradiance/ACES conversions. Native transfers are species dimensions, stable identity distributions, linear colour interpolation, LOD intent and measured lighting targets; implement them through native mesh/instance/material/shadow APIs. A2's seed strings need not match native hashing; don't reroll existing trees merely to match JS. Classic three custom shadow-material hooks also need renderer-specific support checks before use in A2's node renderer ([three Object3D][three-object]; local PORT-LIST).

In three, `alphaTest` discards below threshold; `alphaToCoverage` needs MSAA; `forceSinglePass` is relevant to double-sided **transparent** two-pass materials, not a universal cutout optimization [three-material]. Do not promise identical WebGL2 edge quality in all render targets. For custom Metal, alpha test/coverage are pipeline decisions; they are not assured RealityKit switches. Preserve depth and shadow correctness instead of blindly forcing early fragment tests [apple-perf].

### Three ranked experiments for P2 / 5A / A2; A3 acceptance

All experiments require a future implementation task; this document performs none. Keep calibration-v2 sun/fill/exposure/grade fixed and use generic species/region rules. Cost bounds are hypotheses, with baseline and candidate drawn from the same renderer.

1. **Layered crown shading and restrained colour — cheapest, first.** 5A/P2: on existing opaque meshes, first compare current shading with one coherent lobe/whole-crown normal and AO rebalance; separately compare regional green precedence with unchanged stable ±5% value and seasonal timing. Do not add extra random saturation. After isolating those effects, test a leaf-only sun-dependent light-through term replacing, not stacking on, the current constant emissive contribution. Start at zero and a small bounded candidate; numeric magnitude needs calibration, not invention here. **Expected budget:** 0 added triangles/draws/atlases; changed fragment arithmetic. **Guess:** highest chance of 2→3 because it targets the recorded gaps. **A3:** layered lit tops/deep but readable interiors, matte highlights, distinct regional greens; no neon crowns, black creases, glowing bark or night glow. Check bushes alongside trees.

2. **Spend existing triangles on silhouette and LOD continuity — second.** P2: compare one representative broad crown and one vase/open crown under the same species/height/seed using existing near 900 /middle 200 ceilings, reallocating tessellation to visible large lobes and improving normal transitions. Keep spatial placement and mapped dimensions; no pending-v2 values. Keep far envelope/trunk identity and test camera movement across all three native boundaries; optionally compare projected-size selection as a separate policy experiment. **Expected budget:** unchanged per-tree ceilings, no new texture, draws unchanged only if batching stays intact. Shadow proxies stay coarse. **Guess:** better near/mid layered volume without costly leaf noise. **A3:** species silhouette from street and aerial, no bead-like lobes, faceted diamonds, tree shrinking, flashing or bare-season disappearance; inspect transitions as video as well as stills.

3. **Sparse near-crown cluster cards — conditional third, higher risk.** P2/5A: finish a minimal native atlas/two-sided/depth/shadow path for a controlled comparison; A2 implements its own compatible material. Cheapest proposed quality test: **12–24 broad cluster cards on each of 10 closest qualifying crowns**, above an opaque interior, restricted by projected size and stable identity, not a named block. That is **240–480 additional main triangles**, shared R8 atlas about **1.33 MiB**, plus card batches; no individual-leaf normal textures. These counts are guesses; existing 56-card full-crown generator is a separate comparator, not the initial rollout. **A3:** organic edge breakup and soft layered volume at phone width, no lace holes, shimmer, cutout rectangles, excessive detail or scene-wide style shift. Reject if GPU/overdraw cost overwhelms the small visual gain. If opaque experiment 1/2 already reaches 3, defer cards.

**Common A3 protocol:** capture native Sloan's, Lakeview street and postcard, Wilmette and ready hold-outs at frozen camera/date/weather/resolution/lighting; West Highland and Greenville stay pending until capture-ready. Follow [A3 baseline contract](../lookloop/a3-baseline.md) and keep [web grading](../lookloop/web-baseline.md) separate. Review phone-size render/approved calibration pairs for foliage plus all six aspects. Candidate goal: foliage ≥3 per ready hero, no hold-out loss and no regression elsewhere; this alone does **not** meet overall closeness≥4/full-confirmation gate. Use `region_colours.py`, `calibration_colours.py`, `compare_runs.py` beside visual grades. Apply §N unchanged-frame check (<0.5% pixels differ by >12 luma levels); score shifts on unchanged frames are variance, not improvement. Record missing evidence as pending.

Future performance verification must log main/shadow/other-pass triangles and draws, resident texture bytes including targets, CPU/GPU time, peak footprint, thermal state and transition spikes. Inspect overdraw/shadow captures under a moving camera and low sun, not only a static hero. Use the tier study's sustained-device procedure; no GPU-ms, thermal, battery or Safari claim is established by this research. Avoid extra shadow cascades or wide alpha cross-fades to solve a still-image issue without counting their cost.
