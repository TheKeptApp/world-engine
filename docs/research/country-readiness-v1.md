## Size and update method

**Unverified estimates, not measured downloads.** “Metro” budgets below use explicit central-city planning squares, not administrative or whole metropolitan boundaries: Toronto and Amsterdam 20×20 km; London, Mexico City and Tokyo 40×40 km; Sydney 50×50 km. They may include water, missing coverage and peripheral municipalities. An actual metro polygon and catalogue tile sizes are required before procurement. No geographic area claim is implied by these chosen squares.

For one Float32 raster, payload = `4 × area_m² / cell_m²` bytes. For a zipped/raster download planning allowance use 0.25–1× that payload, with tile-boundary overhead additional; this compression factor is an **engineering assumption**, not a provider guarantee. At 1 m the six respective payloads are 1.6 / 6.4 / 1.6 / 6.4 / 10 / 6.4 GB (decimal); 0.5 m is 4×, 1.5 m is /2.25, 5 m is /25. A DTM+DSM pair doubles these figures. GSI XML may exceed the raster payload: budget 1–4× until file sizes are known. Vector/mesh ranges below are deliberately broad planning allowances: counts, vertices and compression were not measured. A lidar allowance of 0.03–0.15 GB/km² is assumed for planning, not evidence of a particular flight density. Never substitute these estimates for published tile sizes.

Catalogue refresh, product release and flight dates are distinct. “Unknown cadence” means no repeat-survey promise verified; retain each tile's acquisition date. Year-built unknown stays null; a roof outline, DSM or planning height limit is not a roof-form measurement or existing-building height. This follows [the existing inventory's cautions](../research-gpt/metro-data-coverage-v1/README.md).

## A1 pipeline compatibility — assessment, not tested implementation

Sizes: **S** one format/schema/metadata mapping; **M** ingestion plus datum/classification/identity matching and QA; **L** new semantic3D importer or major extraction route. These are planning judgements, not quotes or measured effort. No country runs end-to-end unchanged today: [A1's tools](../../Tools/regionkit/lidar/README.md) assume USGS EPT discovery and vendor classes, and [terrain](../../Tools/regionkit/terrain/README.md) validates against USGS TNM. General math/output formats can be reused after normalization; country-specific retrieval and vertical references cannot simply inherit US configuration.

This latter Overture label is our proposed clear credit, not a mandated literal. Retain licence link and change notice; the existing [WorldEngine public derivative-database offer](../data-licensing.md) must be implemented before distribution. Status remains **YELLOW release gate**, not RED commercial-use prohibition; no licence status in existing inventory is silently changed.
