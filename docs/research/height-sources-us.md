# US building-height sources — A9

Research checked **8 October 2026**. Coverage: Denver (including Sloan's Lake and West Highland), Chicago (including Lakeview), the Little Rock area, Greenville **South Carolina**, and a national fallback. Metadata, documentation and terms only; no bulk data or property/person records downloaded. This is a source decision document, not new measurements, pipeline changes or a rendered-look pass.

## Decision and ranking for the look

**Prefer QA-passing local roof heights derived from public USGS point clouds; resolve Denver's stereo roofprint rights and Greenville's municipal height units/rights next. Use Microsoft Global ML heights as the national gap filler. Assessor storeys help coarse residential massing but cannot certify roof height or a high-rise skyline.** Little Rock has promising PAgis elevation services, but its commercial redistribution restriction prevents treating them as unrestricted open data.

Ranking is expected visual usefulness, conditional on rights and quality gates, not a measured accuracy ranking:

1. **Local classified LiDAR + footprint-specific ground subtraction:** roof-top and eave evidence, strongest basis for roofline and skyline. Available established surveys for Denver, Cook County and Greenville; Little Rock's exact current public USGS work unit still needs verification.
2. **Height-attributed municipal stereo/photogrammetric polygons:** Denver and Greenville city candidates. Convenient for massing, but height convention, source date, parts and licence matter; they do not necessarily supply roof geometry.
3. **Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints heights:** a usable national fallback with permissive rights; modelled average height may flatten pitched roofs or miss towers. Local availability is not yet counted.
4. **Municipal story counts / assessor residence categories:** useful for low-rise scale where measured height is missing; floors-to-metres adds assumptions, and parcel/card joins can be ambiguous.
5. **Footprint-only layers, bare-earth DEMs, zoning maxima and proposed permits:** no actual building height; exclude as height sources. They can support matching, terrain or change checks after their own rights clearance.

## Ratings and redistribution

**GREEN** = verified source permission for commercial use and redistribution subject to stated terms. **YELLOW** = missing or conflicting exact-release rights, units, provenance or coverage; A11 must resolve the named condition before acquisition/use. **RED** = incompatible redistribution or no usable height content for this purpose. The source-rights rating below is distinct from look fitness: GREEN permission does not certify building-height accuracy. No YELLOW/RED raw source acquisition is proposed.

USGS public 3DEP products are free and have no use restrictions ([official products policy](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services?qt-science_support_page_related_con=0)); the [AWS registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/usgs-lidar/) identifies US Government public-domain licensing. This applies to released public products, not restricted partner holdings. Respect unavailable or restricted work units. Preserve source IDs, observation dates, units and provenance even when attribution is not legally required.

Joining public-domain or permissively licensed measurements to our OSM-derived footprints does **not** clear the combined release automatically. A11 must assess the resulting database and retain applicable ODbL attribution/share-alike/database-offer obligations. This research does not change the existing release hold.

## Join contract and quality limits

These are proposed uniform rules for a later authorized implementation, not changes to the ladder. Keep our frozen footprint geometry and canonical `osm:<way|relation>:<id>` key; never silently replace it with a source roofprint. Prefer a documented shared building key. Otherwise project both datasets to a suitable local metric CRS, group documented parts, and require a unique reciprocal best polygon overlap (proposed IoU ≥0.70). Mark low-overlap, one-to-many and ambiguous cases unmatched; do not choose a source because its height agrees. A roofprint/wall mismatch may fail legitimately. Parcel joins require verified building-card linkage or exactly one compatible improvement/principal building; exclude ambiguous parcels rather than propagating one count to all structures. No owner/address matching is necessary.

For point clouds, calculate roof statistics within the footprint and ground from the same survey's nearby class-2 returns. Record p95 roof-top, p50 typical roof and an eave proxy separately; subtract compatible ground elevations before labelling AGL. Reject inadequate point support and vegetation contamination. Existing [height contract](../data/building-heights.md) already defines these distinctions. Do not confuse horizontal CRS units with delivered Z units or NAVD88 altitude with building height.

Source point/ground accuracy is **not building-height accuracy**. Roof percentile conventions, ridge/eave differences, occlusion, roof overhangs, footprint alignment, ground slope and epoch changes contribute. Integer-foot municipal heights add quantization; floor-count conversion adds storey-height and roof assumptions. Microsoft average-height model error is not given by footprint confidence. Keep observed/inferred/default labels and source provenance; no guessed numeric accuracy grades.

Existing repository coverage results are evidence of feasibility only: Sloan 29.1%, Lakeview 93.5%, Greenville Downtown 64.2% of available OSM footprints accepted in their respective runs; all were uncalibrated grade D ([measurement documentation](../data/building-heights.md)). These are historical internal aggregates, not fresh calculations, metro completeness or look verification. Height alone does not recover roof shapes/parts, and a single extrusion can still lose important skyline detail.
