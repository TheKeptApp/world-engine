Shipping World.swift/LOD/culling/mesh/placement/shadow paths and shaders are unchanged. No live native promotion or denied-promotion measurement is claimed.

Enabled callers provide stable source IDs, 3D eye distances, valid far/skyline fallback, projected desired LOD and measured costs for every level in the requested chain. Shared occupied main draw slots are counted once; moving the last instance releases its old slot. ## Bounded completed-frame output witnesses

`CaptureOutputStability.swift` accepts a pixel observation only after a strictly newer completed-frame sequence and requires three identical decoded full-image samples. A changed pixel resets the count; duplicate/stale frame observations and empty data cannot count. Exact output identity is stronger than merely stable output luminance. Capture gain remains fixed 1.0. The app samples candidate snapshots only after the scene-ready handshake, checks scene signature/dimensions throughout, and uses a 30-second output-observer deadline (existing snapshot calls also have their own bounded timeouts). It saves the final accepted candidate rather than taking a new unchecked snapshot. Failed decode, changed scene, GPU failure or timeout rejects capture. Shipping render/mesh/shader/exposure sources remain unchanged.
