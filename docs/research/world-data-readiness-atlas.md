# World data-readiness atlas

Research only; snapshot 9 October 2026 UTC. One fixed frame: 60 city/administrative-centre cells, 30 suburban/rural candidates and terrain controls, plus two Antarctic controls. This extends [the US metro protocol](metro-skyline-heights.md); the requested `docs/research/metro-height-readiness` path is not present in this checkout. No pipeline, look, source-clearance, tracker or ship-policy changes.

## Interpretation and permission boundary

These are **capped source-record samples, not recall**. They do not measure the share of real buildings mapped, missing towers, height accuracy or usable road/water coverage. A release date is not an observation date. An empty sampled cell does not establish that no buildings exist. A city point is not a representative whole-city survey, and a continent ranking cannot certify every country.

A11's [data-licence inventory](../legal/data-licence-inventory-v1.md), commit `9cdc184`, governs the permission conclusions: Overture Buildings has a GREEN source grant for the stated internal research, while public web/app distribution remains **RED**, chiefly because the working ODbL derivative-database offer is absent. All ranked ship recommendations below are conditional planning priorities, not clearance or authorization to ship.

A11 marks Overture Base and Transportation **YELLOW** until the selected release/source notices are pinned. Road and water queries were therefore withheld. Every cell has `ND-A11`, meaning **unmeasured due to the existing licence gate**, not absence. No alternate source was queried to fill those fields. Street-pattern variety is an intended geographic sampling benefit, not a measured classification; the required road/water and street-pattern audit remains incomplete.

## Selection rule, fixed before feature queries

Use the small [World Bank country metadata response](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country?format=json&per_page=400), with [documented fields](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898590-country-api-queries). Exclude aggregate rows, empty city/coordinate fields and unknown income groups. The units are economies, including dependencies; these are not all sovereign states. Select ten named administrative centres per inhabited continent. Income codes are LIC, LMC, UMC and HIC; no population estimates were downloaded or used.

For each continent, allocate ten slots equally among its nonempty income groups, assigning remainder slots in LIC→LMC→UMC→HIC order. Within each income group sort by the metadata longitude, then ISO3; choose indices `floor((j+0.5)*n/k)` for `j=0..k-1`. If a group has fewer candidates than its quota, take all; fill remaining slots by repeatedly taking the median of the remaining list sorted by income order, longitude and ISO3. Thus no city is selected for a known good height fraction, landmark, personal interest or previous test result.

Continental mapping is frozen: SSF plus DZA/EGY/LBY/MAR/TUN→Africa; SAS and remaining MEA/EAS→Asia, except AUS/FJI/KIR/MHL/FSM/NRU/NZL/PLW/PNG/WSM/SLB/TON/TUV/VUT→Oceania; ECS→Europe except ARM/AZE/GEO/KAZ/KGZ/TJK/TKM/TUR/UZB→Asia; ARG/BOL/BRA/CHL/COL/ECU/GUY/PRY/PER/SUR/URY/VEN→South America; other LCN and NAC→North America. This is a sampling convention, not a geopolitical ruling. It does not force the US, Canada, Australia or every populous country into this particular frame. Small island centres and large capitals provide a size spread, but population and actual street morphology were not stratified or verified.

**Coordinate correction disclosed:** the initial World Bank city-labelled coordinates placed several cells outside their named cities. That preliminary pass was stopped voluntarily; its feature results are excluded. The same selected cities were retained, with centres resolved from [Wikidata coordinate statements](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) through bounded official API metadata requests: preferred nondeprecated P625, otherwise first nondeprecated P625. Exact city entity links appear in the table. Names with spelling/article aliases were resolved to the named city, not to the point with more buildings. Yaren is an administrative locality, not a large city. Bujumbura and Kyiv retain the selection-frame labels even where the World Bank label is outdated. A geographically defined city/atoll centre can be over water; cells are never shifted to capture more footprints. The corrected frame was frozen before its Overture queries, and all preliminary aggregates are excluded from rankings.

For each continent, choose the fifth city in the **original metadata-longitude ordering** of its ten selected cities as the counterpart anchor. Add cells 10 km north and 10 km south (suburban candidates), and 40 km north (rural candidate). These geometric offsets are deliberately unchanged across regions; suburban/rural land use is **unverified**, and an offset may land on water or inside a large metropolitan area. These are twelve suburban candidates and six rural candidates, not eighteen confirmed land-use labels.

Add one coastal rural control per continent at its approximate westernmost mainland point, shifted 500 m east: Pointe des Almadies, Cape Baba, Cabo da Roca, Cape Prince of Wales, Punta Parinas, and Steep Point (Australia-mainland convention). Add one mountain rural control 5 km east of its conventional highest summit: Kilimanjaro, Everest, Elbrus, Denali, Aconcagua, Puncak Jaya (Oceania/New Guinea convention). Six coastal and six mountain controls complete the thirty non-city cells. Offsets do not guarantee dry land, habitation or a true suburb. Examples of primary geographic metadata: [Australian continental extremes](https://www.ga.gov.au/scientific-topics/national-location-information/dimensions/continental-extremities), [Aconcagua coordinates](https://www.ign.gob.ar/NuestrasActividades/Geografia/DatosArgentina/MaximasAlturas). The approximate anchors and final bounds are published, rather than treated as surveyed positions.

Antarctica has no cities: include the largest research-settlement setting, [McMurdo](https://www.nsf.gov/od/opp/ail/mcmurdo-station), and highest ice-dome/plateau setting, [Dome A](https://english.nao.cas.cn/research/telescopesprojects/researchprograms/202103/t20210321_265712.html), as two extra controls. Their approximate cells are not a station-building census; in particular the Dome A control is not a verified Kunlun building location.

Query order is round-robin across Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America and Oceania: first city 1 in each, then city 2, through city 10; next each of the five control kinds in the same continental order; Antarctica last. This distributes a possible access stop without rearranging cells after seeing results.

## Read limits and metrics

Use only Buildings `building` from pinned Overture release `2026-09-23.1`, with collection licence `ODbL-1.0` verified in [STAC](https://stac.overturemaps.org/2026-09-23.1/buildings/building/collection.json). Select intersecting published STAC shards; use [cloud GeoParquet range access](https://docs.overturemaps.org/getting-data/cloud-sources/), not whole-file downloads. No direct Microsoft, OSM feature, Base or Transportation samples are used here. Country/city-coordinate metadata is selection evidence, not a new cleared engine data source.

Cell bounds: latitude half-width `500/111320` degrees; longitude half-width divided by cosine of centre latitude; round to six decimals. Bounds are west/south/east/north. Record density uses spherical cell area `R² * (east-west) * (sin(north)-sin(south))`, angles in radians, `R=6371.0088 km`. Near high latitudes this remains an approximation to a 1 km square, not a cadastral surface-area measurement.

The bounded query is:

```sql
SELECT id, height, sources
FROM read_parquet(selected_shards)
WHERE bbox.xmin <= east AND bbox.xmax >= west
  AND bbox.ymin <= north AND bbox.ymax >= south
  AND coalesce(is_underground, false) = false
ORDER BY id LIMIT 201;
```

Retain the first 200; the 201st indicates the cap. Read the three projected columns plus filtering fields, not building geometry, names, addresses or contributor metadata. Keep only aggregate counts, supplier names, source-date ranges, frame coordinates and audit hashes; no feature identifiers in the report. The [building schema](https://docs.overturemaps.org/schema/reference/buildings/building/) distinguishes parent buildings from parts; only parents are sampled. Bbox intersection can include a polygon whose actual geometry does not intersect the cell. Counts therefore refer to intersecting **record bounding boxes**, not deduplicated physical-building occupancy.

Hard limits, fixed before each pass: 2 MiB per range; 8 MiB per cell; 320 MiB per pass overall; 3,000 range requests; 2 MiB per STAC metadata response and 20 MiB cumulative STAC metadata. The larger overall budget than the US survey's 160 MiB reflects worldwide shards; per-cell and per-range limits are unchanged. A local guard refuses unbounded parquet GETs, requires remote HTTP 206, reserves bytes before transfer, and stops all subsequent queries on **any** access/network/query error or limit. DuckDB retries are zero, one query thread, 30-second access timeout. No mirror, retry, proxy around restrictions, or changed query is used after an access failure. The local guard forwards ordinary permitted public ranges; it is not an access workaround. Preliminary and corrected passes have the same limits, and their separate traffic is disclosed in Evidence.

`N*` = capped N=200; otherwise N is the returned eligible parent-record count. Density is `N / cell area`; `≥` means a capped lower bound for mapped bbox records only. It is not an estimated real-world building density or total. Valid height means numeric `0 < height < 1000 m`; fraction = H/N. Zero N makes fraction unavailable, not 0%. Valid here means syntactically plausible, not independently accurate or calibrated; reported/modelled heights and roof conventions can differ. GERS sorting is deterministic but not a uniform probability sample, and samples from different cells may overlap.

Source notes show supplier codes and the range of source `update_time` values where available. These are **revision dates, not imagery, construction or survey dates**. All rows use the September 2026 Overture release; no row is described as live. Provider attribution can occur multiple times per record/property; supplier counts are not unique-building counts and are not reported as coverage shares. No independently verified observation epoch was established from these samples.

Fallback tier labels are research routing summaries, **not new thresholds implemented in the engine**:

- **H1:** mapped footprints and at least 80% plausible metric heights in this capped sample; use each usable source height with provenance/date and existing QA, while routing every remaining building through H2. The 80% screening threshold is a declared research convention, not an accuracy claim or a tuned place-specific rule.
- **H2:** mapped footprints with insufficient metric heights; preserve mapped geometry. Use an already cleared, QA-passing independent height/roof source where available; otherwise existing reported height→recorded floors→generic regional/type default logic, with inferred/default labels and deterministic seed. Floors were not sampled, so their availability is not claimed. No invented precise tower heights, per-building patches or cross-country reuse of a US survey adapter without datum/metadata work. Existing survey sidecars are separate evidence, not proof of renderer consumption.
- **B0:** measured zero mapped records; first look for an already cleared alternative footprint source using the generic merge rule, then mark missing building coverage. Preserve independently known, licensed map layers; any abstract generated context must be explicitly inferred, never presented as a real house or a verified empty landscape. Zero source records do not establish absence of real buildings.
- **U:** no usable measurement (access stop, budget stop, or outside published shard inventory); mark source unknown and withhold observed-coverage claims. Do not convert it to B0 or retry around the stop.

The existing height/tag/default mechanism is described in [Overture source integration](overture-source.md#tags-and-heights); measurement QA and datum limitations are described in [building heights](../data/building-heights.md). This task changes neither.
