## One general rule and approved scope

**Select the least costly existing caster LOD that preserves shadows on the approved receiver region, including off-view and low-sun casters; never reduce that region to satisfy a budget.** This implements [mobile-rendering-v1 research](../research-gpt/mobile-rendering-v1/README.md), **“Top 10” T01 and “Shadows, time, trees and atmosphere”**, limited by the current approved reach and calibration. It does not adopt that research's older speculative all-pass draw ceilings. A8 ranked the gap at 6: receiver/low-sun coverage and a rendered light witness, not simply missing shadows.

**Approved authorities:** calibration-v2 `sharedLook.lighting.shadow`; existing `Sources/WorldGen/Profiles/look.json/shadows`; visual-v2 budget contract and the later owner tier decision. **Not approved, excluded:** radius halving (including the web implementation's fallback), new reduced-reach thresholds, optional CSM/cascade changes without a separate tested decision, pending `crown-silhouettes-v2`, `sky-cloud-v1`, `street-ground-v1`. Do not adopt foliage-exp1's experimental coefficients: it is a template, not a shadow target.

## Feature proof and stop conditions

Before/after: in addition to the named hero/hold-outs, keep the same receiver/caster scene while moving the camera and advancing to the existing below-15° solar condition. Compare the shadow footprint at the full normal/low-sun reach, including a caster outside the camera frustum whose shadow enters it. Log selected caster LOD and approved reach; ensure colour geometry and source IDs are unchanged. Sweep the existing LOD boundaries without changing light/grade. Measure all-pass cost; no additional texture, light or shadow pass is justified by this rule.

Stop if 120/150 m approved reach is shortened, off-view shadows disappear, low-sun coverage is clipped, hidden proxies draw in the colour pass, halving activates, counts exceed the applicable budget, or the native shader/SDK cannot implement the selection safely. Do not tune light, shadow opacity, fake AO or a camera-specific cutoff to rescue a budget or score. Stop for input drift, unapproved pack use, missing captures, a hold-out regression, or any merge conflict other than the documented add-only handoff exception. If the budget cannot be met at approved coverage, report the failure for owner resolution; do not claim parity.
