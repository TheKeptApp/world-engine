**Resolve one total view-ray extinction under the approved 5% MOR convention, then integrate its airlight once across every world surface.** Replace the legacy native convention and duplicated background terms; do not add a new fog grade. This implements [mobile-rendering-v1 research](../research-gpt/mobile-rendering-v1/README.md), **“Shadows, time, trees and atmosphere” (distant haze)**, with the exact approved implementation authority [haze-visibility-v1 README](../proposals/haze-visibility-v1/README.md) **“Formula and units”, “Data selection and layer handling”, “Explicit overrides”**. Its research T09 loading/cutoff proposal is deliberately outside this migration.

Do not call native migration complete because pack data or A6's independent API exists. **Approved:** haze-visibility-v1 STATUS overrides its historical JSON label and the enumerated legacy haze fields; calibration-v2 retains look/grade; preserve approved weather-moments/night-fog local effects outside the override scope. **Not approved, excluded:** `sky-cloud-v1`, `street-ground-v1`, `crown-silhouettes-v2`, unvalidated AQI→visibility conversion and mountain enlargement/contrast boosts. ## Exact values and future files

- `haze-visibility-v1/values.json/definition`: contrast threshold **0.05**, constant **2.995732273553991**; `sigmaPerM=-ln(0.05)/(1000*visibilityKm)`, `T=exp(-tau)`, `tau=integral(sigma ds)`. At the stated MOR, T must be 0.05. Recompute legacy visibility equivalents from actual sigma; do not relabel the old 2% or native fog-end distances as 5% MOR.
- Unit witnesses (not forced October weather): `regions[id=front-range].seasons.summer.clear` **75 km / 0.000039943097 m⁻¹**; `great-lakes` summer clear **20 km / 0.000149786614 m⁻¹**. Native production selection uses region + **local** season + verified state through pack `selection`, not these two fixed test anchors. Enumerate and snapshot the exact resolved pack row for every capture; missing mapping blocks execution.
- `airlight.stateDayHex.clear=#BDD0D9`, `timeMix.day=0`; decode hex once to linear. Homogeneous common-airlight equation is `Cout=T*Clit+(1-T)*Cair`; for different layer airlights integrate segments front-to-back, not a weighted-colour shortcut. `integration.applyOnce=true`, `noNearClearBubble=true`; sky/already-atmospheric reflections bypass a second mix.
- `selection`: observation/model precedes validated event adapter, then labelled authored regional fallback; one observed total replaces total sigma, never add its contributors twice. Invalid/nonfinite/nonpositive visibility is rejected. Censored observations retain lower-bound status. Darkness alone does not change sigma. Apply exact freshness/transition keys (**60/180 min**, **10 s**, severe validated low visibility within **1 s**) only in the consuming selector, not a new scene-specific smoothing rule.
- `overrides[pack=weather-moments-v1].momentMigration`: cover **all 21** before/after mappings and exact preserved fields; old universal clear baselines are removed, before-state resolves independently. Preserve night/marine local bounds/masks/densities including existing local **0.035/m** where applicable; do not treat a total observation plus its local layer as two independent attenuations. Pack `integration.localFog` governs traversal/fitting; if required spatial information is unavailable, block that case rather than invent it.
- `mountains`: real DEM/sightline; retained contrast **0.05**, fade/cull **0.03–0.05**, internal detail threshold **0.10**, relief **2 drawable px**; Earth radius **6,371,000 m**. MOR is not a clip plane. No hidden “clear bubble”, extra distance desaturation or painted-ridge scale fit.

## Feature proof and stop conditions

Assert T=0.05 at both unit-witness MOR distances and T=1 at zero distance; compare representative surfaces and terrain for identical optical depth with only one airlight application. Validate all 21 moment mappings before/after, censored/invalid/stale inputs, day/night sigma invariance, local-layer entry/exit, and no doubled observed-total extinction. Use existing named weather controls from approved weather-moments inputs; record exact IDs and source keys rather than selecting prettier weather. Resolve native October season independently from the unchanged web summer fixture. A homogeneous-only test cannot certify layer preservation.

Mountain tests must fail retention when occluded or below 2 px and fade below the contrast thresholds, without altering geometry. Keep lighting/exposure/saturation, water mechanics/shore/ice, wetness/snow history and actor content unchanged. Expected geometry/pass increment: **0 triangles, 0 draws, 0 shadow maps**; atmospheric arithmetic and transport costs remain unmeasured. No added full-screen fog pass or new shadow work; record shader cost and any transport memory instead of assuming a free migration. A3 reviews the named hero pair plus untouched Lakeview/Wilmette; broader moment correctness does not replace that visual check.

Stop for missing 21-case coverage, untraceable conversion, unresolved observed-total/local-layer fitting, stale data labelled live, double fog, source-pack edits, loss of approved local effects, or a hold-out regression. Stop on load/lock/disk/browser/prerequisite failure and non-add-only merge conflicts. Do not rescue a low ridge by weakening approved extinction. Report unresolved cases as blockers; no “native 5% MOR complete” claim until those tests pass.
