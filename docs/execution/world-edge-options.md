# Visible world edge — options for R

## Recommendation and first test

Recommend **(a), streamed, simplified context**, implemented in bounded spatial/LOD tiers rather than full buildings to the 150 km far plane. It is the only option that addresses nearby missing city and supports the endless-area goal without suppressing the user's wide view. Use data-derived coarse terrain/water/land cover as its eventual outermost tier, not as a replacement for nearby visible neighborhoods. This does not approve a combined implementation: first prove one context band, then decide whether to extend it. Keep the current look/crown controls unchanged until that isolated comparison is available. A camera cap is a possible disclosed temporary product limitation, not a look-gate fix; option (c) alone is inadequate where nearby buildings are still resolvable.

**First test, proposed only:** offline export/accounting of the already available Lakeview context band, clipped outside the exact core, with measured bytes, shared material/LOD draw counts, shoreline completeness and duplicate-ID checks. Do not fetch a wider ring or build streaming first. Establish whether the band even covers the 600 m frustum: existing building coverage is narrower than land coverage. Then, in a separately authorized capture task, compare before/repeat/after at the same Lakeview600 m and Sloan40/150/600 m cameras, using frozen `sceneBudget=1` controls and A3 blind review. Reject a new seam at the outer band or water painted as land. This task performs no exports, code changes, benchmarks or captures.

## What is known

[A7 diagnosis](../review/lakeview-600m-blank-field.md), filed `1417d6d`, establishes an export/consumption gap: Lakeview core is **1,000×1,000 m /25 cells**, Sloan **1,600×1,200 m /48 cells**; all listed files are present and hash-valid. Both boundary GLBs are **1,536 bytes**. `SceneGenerator` supplies a **12 km square lawn boundary**, not surrounding city. `WorldPackage` records context source provenance but exports no renderable ring; `WorldScene.load` loads the detailed chunk pool at LOD0 and boundary, not raw context. Waiting longer cannot load nonexistent context geometry. Native has a separate `World.startContext` / `ContextRing.build` path; web absence is not proof native lacks context.

The recorded600 m, pitch45°, vertical FOV50° view has a top-center ground ray approximately **1,648 m** forward. In Lakeview the western core boundary is only **441.1255 m** from the camera, versus Sloan **1,279.9372 m**. Predicted center-column seam rows are **374.95** and **63.39** in a565 px image. Side/corner coverage matters too. The far plane is150,000 m, but this is a clipping bound, not the distance at which urban building meshes should all be loaded.

[Context data documentation](../data/context-rings.md) records land/road context roughly3 km beyond the core, but both Sloan and Lakeview **building bands are only0.5 km** outside it because of the source-size cap. Raw audited context inputs are **23,102,065 bytes Lakeview** and **19,332,840 Sloan**, not GLB download sizes or decoded memory. The older context table rounds/differs by snapshot; prefer A7's pinned hashes for this diagnosis. Source existence does not prove complete water/land coverage: the documented large-relation cap can omit Lake Michigan. Inspect the exact source before drawing a fallback lake or shore.

Byte envelopes use decimal MB, not MiB;20–80 B/triangle spans indexed/shared vertices to less-shared attributes, **not a measured compression ratio**. Formula: indexBytes + vertexCount×attributeStride + GLB/metadata overhead, counted for each exported LOD. Download, decoded arrays, GPU resident buffers and simultaneous old/new overlap are separate ledgers. No material/texture memory is charged as zero unless assets are genuinely reused. Ring cells and LOD duplication can increase exported bytes even while reducing per-frame work.

Option(a) cannot literally extend existing0.5 km buildings to every visible distance without more source coverage. For illustration at the same geometry, the top-center ray exceeds the Lakeview core+western building band (about941 m from eye). The unfilled rest must be measured and sourced, not stretched or populated with invented buildings. Proposed envelope covers a first band, **not complete150 km city coverage**. Additional ring tiers need separate bytes/count measurements; distant terrain can use coarser DEM/horizon representation according to approved terrain/visibility rules.

For(b), the flat-ground center-column constraint is `h ≤ d_available*tan(pitch−verticalFOV/2)`. Lakeview west boundary441.1255 m at45°/50° gives **h≤160.56 m** without margin. A150 m cap is only a center-column illustration, **not a recommended universal cap**: image corners, lateral edges, pitched terrain, buildings, camera movement and camera FOV may require less. If pitch−FOV/2≤0, that ray approaches the horizon and no finite core-only height bound fixes it. Never alter frozen600 m grading cameras and call the edge repaired.

For(c), no-buildings must be justified by projected size/contrast, not lack of data. A10 m-high building at about1 km in this565 px/50° viewport projects roughly6 px before orientation effects (`pixels≈height*focalPixels/distance`, focal≈606 px). Nearby missing city can therefore remain readily visible. Atmospheric extinction uses approved visibility; do not add fog or darken land to conceal the seam. Flat land-colour boundaries must follow evidence, not regional stereotypes.

## Integration and what must be true on iPhone

- **A1** verifies ring coverage, large-water relation handling, source rights and observation/version metadata. No download or licence decision in this brief. **P2** owns generic context generation/LOD and core/ring exclusion. **A4** owns package/streaming format and web consumption handoff; **A2** owns bakeoff material/budget integration; **5A** owns native renderer/context parity. **A3** owns controls and blind scores. Exact lane handoff precedes implementation.
- Ring geometry must exclude or deduplicate the detailed core, share world coordinates/vertical datum, preserve coastline/islands and use explicit unknown coverage rather than invented lawns. Rendering lower-detail data must not change factual house classification or move features.
- Define coverage from allowed camera frusta plus guard band and approved visibility/horizon limits, not far-plane radius alone. Fetch/stream only necessary cells, prefetch predictable motion, evict outside a bounded working set and retain a safe coarse parent during transition. “Endless” means successive coverage as the camera moves, not resident unlimited geometry.
- Phone qualification requires actual device main/shadow counters, CPU/GPU frame-time percentiles, memory peaks during decoding/upload/eviction, thermal behavior and network interruption. Start with R's14 Pro under separately authorized testing; do not infer floor qualification from it. Maintain60 fps target and existing tier criteria; Simulator readiness and Mac timings do not establish iPhone throughput. No hidden resolution or shadow-reach reduction.
- Freeze context data, camera, FOV, sun/weather/exposure, loaded state and the sceneBudget policy in both sides. New source coverage is intentionally different; core geometry/materials must remain unchanged. Hold-outs include Wilmette, West Highland and Greenville when coverage/contracts exist. Missing proof is pending. A3 should see continuous plausible city/land/water without a new outer seam and no lower grade elsewhere; wide-view coverage is distinct from crown quality.

## Decision gates and evidence line

Proceed beyond the proposed first offline test only if measured export/residency costs fit a declared budget, missing-water/coverage risks are resolved, and the unchanged control is reproducible. A standard120-draw result has no reserve for later crowns: prefer fewer batches or report the tradeoff rather than spend all headroom silently. If context cannot fit, present measured LOD/coverage tradeoffs to R; do not switch to camera clipping/fog as an undisclosed substitute. Option(c) may be approved later as a far tier or loading fallback, but must remain truthfully coarse and cannot claim nearby-city completion.
