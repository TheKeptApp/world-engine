## Findings that limit a merge into the main viewer

- The latest images do **not** establish iOS parity or the visual look gate. Near crowns remain coarse, clouds oversized, water too cyan, and the actual DEM ridge much lower than the painted mountain backdrop. No camera-specific fix is applied.
- The colour test is incomplete evidence: Sloan has only sky and sidewalk boxes, no mountains/water/crowns. Its sidewalk box hits foreground grass in this export. Lakeview's road box hits grass, and the autumn crown loses the green-pixel mask. A lower/unchanged mean is not a geometry-quality verdict. Log coverage alongside every mean.
- The light step worsened both reported means. R's stated cross-view rollback trigger (Sloan improves **and** Lakeview worsens) did not fire. This is a recorded regression, not a claimed improvement.
- Pack resolver, hue-preserving transfer, deterministic species selection, shore field and cost instrumentation are reusable modules. Facade inference, coarse crown tessellation, static-chunk LOD and shadow cost need further review before integration into the main viewer. Merging this isolated experiment to `main` does not activate it in the production viewer.
